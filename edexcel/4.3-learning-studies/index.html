4.3 STUDIES
Watson & Rayner (1920) · Capafóns et al. (1998) · Prot et al. (2014) / Bastian et al. (2012) · 学习理论研究

Topic D · Learning theories and development Studies Spec 4.3.1–4.3.4

What this chapter is really asking

  1. One classic study, fixed: Watson and Rayner (1920) conditioned a phobia in one infant and showed fear can be learned by classical conditioning.
  2. One contemporary study, fixed: Capafóns et al. (1998) treated fear of flying with systematic desensitisation, the therapy built on the same learning principles.
  3. One contemporary study, your choice: Prot et al. (2014) on prosocial media, or Bastian et al. (2012) on cyber-dehumanization. You must know one of them in detail.
  4. All four are behaviour-change studies: exposure to a situation changes what people fear, feel or do. The classic study and Capafóns are two ends of one story: fear can be learned, and learned fear can be treated.

1. Getting Started

Textbook opener: all four studies are about how behaviour can be influenced by exposure to different situations. Four situations to think about designing an experiment for:

  • Being exposed to positive role models on television.
  • Being punished for being violent in a video game.
  • Being rewarded for helping an opponent in a video game.
  • Being conditioned to fear an object.
中文理解：四个研究一条线
这一章的四个研究讲同一件事：经历会改变行为。Watson & Rayner 证明恐惧可以被「教」出来；Capafóns 证明被教出来的恐惧可以被「治」回去；Prot 和 Bastian 研究媒体这种现代经历怎么长期塑造共情和自我认知。复习时按这条线串：先学（经典条件反射）再治（系统脱敏），再推广到媒体环境。

3. Watson and Rayner (1920): Aim and Sample

John Watson and Rosalie Rayner wanted to see if they could condition a phobic response to a white rat in an infant.

Aim

Using the principles of classical conditioning（经典条件反射）, investigate whether a human child could learn to be afraid of a previously neutral stimulus, a white rat, which initially caused no fear response, and whether this reaction would be generalised to similar objects.

Sample

  • A single case experiment（单一个案实验） with one child, 'Albert B', given the pseudonym Little Albert.
  • Nine months old, chosen for his 'stolid and unemotional' character（沉稳不动感情的）.
  • Raised almost from birth in a hospital environment: his mother was a wet nurse at the Harriet Lane Home for Invalid Children.
  • Healthy from birth, described as one of the best developed youngsters brought to the hospital, weighing 21 pounds at nine months.
中文理解：为什么选「不哭的」孩子
样本只有一个孩子，而且专挑情绪反应少的。课本评价里会考这一点：专挑情绪稳的孩子这个选择标准，让 Albert 可能不代表同龄孩子，这是代表性缺陷；但课本也提到，Watson 当时的考虑是情绪稳的孩子可能更能扛住条件反射训练的困扰。记住两组说法，评价题看问法用哪边。

The ending, which examiners like

Albert's mother withdrew him from the experiment before he could be deconditioned, so it is possible to speculate the child spent his life with a phobia of white rats. The textbook adds two counterpoints: since it took many conditioning trials to build the fear, his reaction would likely not have persisted for long; and Little Albert died young from a childhood illness.

6. Watson and Rayner: Conclusions and Evaluation

Conclusions

  • A phobia of an object that was not previously feared could be learned: the neutral rat became associated with the loud noise, so the rat (now CS) produced fear (now CR).
  • Stimulus generalisation was observed: the feared response transferred to other animals and objects broadly similar to the rat.
  • The fear response lasted for 31 days after the emotional tests, though it weakened towards the end. Longer testing was impossible because Albert left the hospital.
  • Since the reaction was still present one month after the initial association, such conditioned emotional responses have the potential to last a lifetime.

Evaluation

Strength 1 · Contribution to learning theory

A notable contribution to understanding how human behaviours are acquired through classical conditioning, highlighting the role of the environment in shaping behaviour.

Strength 2 · Scientific methodology

Control over the stimulus-response link: fear was measured before conditioning as a baseline; the study was conducted in a second room to eliminate context as an extraneous variable; behaviour at every stage was carefully documented, so the study could be replicated and its reliability assured.

Limitation 1 · Ethics and health doubts

Albert was obviously distressed and could not be deconditioned. Modern ethical guidelines did not exist, but psychological and physical well-being were still neglected. Worse, Fridlund et al. (2012) claim Albert had suffered hydrocephalus（脑积水） since birth and that Watson knew. If correct, this casts serious doubt over the findings and deepens the ethical concerns.

Limitation 2 · One child, one culture

A single case study of one child, selected for his unemotional character, so he may not represent individuals of the same age and gender, which could invalidate the findings. Albert and the researchers were all from one culture (USA), influencing design and analysis, so generalisability to other cultures is questionable. The tasks also lacked ecological validity: laboratory-like setting and tasks he would not normally face.

Wider issue · The nature–nurture debate

We would assume a natural startled reaction to a loud noise. The study shows that natural reaction can be adjusted to become associated with certain items. The textbook's takeaway: it may be more productive to see behaviour through a composite explanation (an interaction) of nature and nurture rather than one or the other.

Exam tip (from the textbook): you may be asked how you would improve this study. Pick the most sensible weakness to fix (for example the single, unrepresentative sample) and justify why the improvement is useful.
中文理解：W&R 评价的分档
优点两条好写：贡献大（证明恐惧能习得）加科学方法（基线、换房间控制情境变量、全程记录）。缺点里 Fridlund 2012 脑积水这条最值钱：它同时打击效度（被试本来就不健康）和伦理（Watson 可能知情），一句顶两句。单一样本和文化偏差是通用缺陷，写的时候挂上「专挑不哭的孩子」这个细节才落分。

7. Capafóns et al. (1998): Aim, Sample and Procedure

Juan Capafóns, Carmen Sosa and Pedro Avero aimed to validate the effectiveness of systematic desensitisation（系统脱敏） as a treatment for fear of flying, and to assess its therapeutic success for this specific phobia.

Aim

To investigate the effectiveness of systematic desensitisation as a treatment for a fear of flying.

Sample

  • Recruited through a media campaign advertising a free-of-charge intervention programme for fear of flying.
  • 41 participants came forward. 20 (8 males, 12 females) were randomly assigned to the treatment group (systematic desensitisation therapy); the remaining 21 (9 males, 12 females) formed the control group (waiting for therapy).

Measures used

MeasureWhat it capturesType
IDG-FVSpanish general diagnostic tool: life history of the fear of flying, including how afraid, whether they fly when there is no alternative, and 21 listed symptomsSelf-report interview
EMV scales (3)Perceived anxiety: fear during the flight; fear of flight preliminaries; fear without involvement (seeing a plane fly)Self-report scale
EPAV-A and EPAV-BFrequency of catastrophic thoughts (engines catching fire) and of different physiological reactions in a flight situationSelf-report scale
Physiological recordingHeart rate, muscle tension and skin temperature, measured for three minutes before the video, during take-off in the simulationObjective

Procedure

  1. All participants were interviewed individually and completed the IDG-FV.
  2. They watched a video of a plane trip (packing to arriving), preceded by a habituation session: three minutes to become acquainted with the situation while heart rate, temperature and muscular tension were measured. They were asked to 'feel as involved as possible'.
  3. Treatment group: approximately eight weeks later they retook the questionnaires and video test. In between: two one-hour sessions a week, 12 to 15 sessions in total, using breathing, progressive muscle relaxation and imagination; combining in vitro (imagined) and in vivo (real-life exposure) elements, plus stop-thinking and brief relaxation techniques.
  4. Control group: invited back after eight weeks for the same assessment. The interval between pre- and post-test was about eight weeks for both groups.
habituation（习惯化） — the process by which a response to a given stimulus decreases with repetition. progressive muscle relaxation（渐进式肌肉放松） — in each session the therapist focuses the client on tensing and relaxing one particular muscle group to create awareness of tension and relaxation.
中文理解：这个实验是 4.1.5 的实证版
你在 4.1.5 学的系统脱敏（恐惧阶梯 + 放松 + 逐级暴露），Capafóns 就是拿它做疗效验证：放松训练（渐进式肌肉放松）+ 想象暴露（in vitro）+ 真实暴露（in vivo）。设计上注意两点：对照组是等待治疗（伦理上的折中，考试要点出来）；测量是主观量表加客观生理指标双线，这条是它最大的优点。

8. Capafóns: Results, Conclusions and Evaluation

Results (Table 24.1 from the textbook)

Before treatment the two groups scored similarly, suggesting a fairly homogeneous sample.

MeasureTreatment pre → post (mean, SD)Control pre → post (mean, SD)Significance
Fear during flight (EMV)25.6 (4.2) → 13.25 (7.97)26.05 (3.67) → 25.81 (4.8)p < 0.001
Catastrophic thoughts (EPAV)10.30 (4.17) → 5.0 (2.64)9.76 (4.92) → 9.67 (5.61)p < 0.01
Heart rate (objective)1.04 (0.09) → 0.99 (0.04)1.07 (0.1) → 1.31 (0.09)p < 0.01
  • The control group's 'mere passing of time' produced no reduction in self-reported fear or objective arousal.
  • The treatment group showed significant reduction in both self-reported fear and physiological measures, except two participants.
  • Only ten per cent of those treated showed no significant reduction in fear measures.

Conclusions

Given the lack of improvement in the control group and the significant improvement of the treatment group, systematic desensitisation is an effective treatment for decreasing or eradicating fear of flying. Capafóns noted it is not infallible (ten per cent incorrectly classified) and future research should examine why the therapy works better for some patients.

Evaluation

Strength 1 · Scientific assessment

Fear was measured with quantifiable scales and objective measures (heart rate, body temperature); many of the scales were validated for reliability.

Strength 2 · Control group and standardisation

The control group provides a baseline comparison, letting researchers test statistical significance with confidence. The consistent laboratory-based environment standardised the initial stages for every participant, minimising extraneous variables.

Strength 3 · Practical application

If therapy benefits participants significantly, it should be offered as a main treatment for fear of flying, bringing personal, social and economic benefits: people are no longer prevented from flying for business or family.

Limitation 1 · Small sample

Only 41 people, so generalisability is limited. Future research should increase the sample and check consistency with other groups, possibly in other regions or countries.

Limitation 2 · Self-report validity

A main assessment was interview questioning. Self-report with closed-ended, pre-set responses (as in the IDG-FV) invites respondents to choose the answer that 'best fits' rather than describe their true view, limiting the validity of responses.

Limitation 3 · Ethics of the control group

Withholding treatment from the control group is not acceptable in itself. It was lessened because the control group was placed on a waiting list for treatment.

Exam tips (from the textbook): 'not significant' does not mean no difference was found; it means the difference could be due to chance at the five per cent level. A non-significant difference between a cheap therapy and an expensive one could actually help authorities direct resources to the cheaper one. Also: categorise your evaluation points by criteria such as validity, generalisability, objectivity, reliability or credibility.
中文理解：评价词要按标准分类背
课本给的 exam tip 很实用：把每个研究的评价点按 validity / generalisability / objectivity / reliability / credibility 五个标准归类。Capafóns 的分配：客观性强（生理指标）算 objectivity，样本 41 人算 generalisability，自评量表 best-fit 算 validity，等待组伦理算 ethics。背的时候带着标签背，考试按标签检索。

9. Prot et al. (2014): Two Studies, One Story

Prot studied whether prosocial media（亲社会媒体） encourages helping behaviour, and whether empathy（共情） is the mediating factor. Two studies: a cross-sectional first study, then a longitudinal second study to establish causality.

Study one · Cross-sectional, seven countries

  • Hypothesis: greater prosocial media use would be associated with more frequent helping behaviour, explained by empathy. They also examined the impact of age, gender and culture.
  • Sample: 2202 adolescents and young adults, mean age 21 (SD = 5.6), from Australia (462), China (203), Croatia (438), Germany (200), Japan (395), Romania (233) and the USA (307).
  • Procedure: questionnaire online or face to face with trained interviewers. Participants listed their three favourite TV shows, movies and video games with monthly use frequency, then rated each for prosocial and violent content (for example 'How often do characters help each other in this game?').
  • Measures: empathy via 14 items of the Interpersonal Reactivity Index (IRI, empathic-concern and perspective-taking subscales; example item: 'Before criticising somebody, I try to imagine how I would feel if I were in their place'). Prosocial behaviour via 10 items of the Brief Prosocial Scale (example: 'I try to be helpful to people even if I don't expect to see them again'). Empathy assumed to be a stable personality characteristic.
  • Result: race, education, socioeconomic status and parent education were not significantly associated, so not analysed further. Across all seven countries there was an association between prosocial media use and helping behaviour, with empathy as the influencing variable.

Study two · Longitudinal, Singapore

  • Why: study one was cross-sectional, so no causal conclusions could be drawn; study two used a longitudinal design（纵向设计） to establish causal direction.
  • Sample: 3034 children and adolescents from Singapore (73% male, 27% female), from six primary and six secondary schools, mean age 11.2. Second data collection: 2360; third: 2232.
  • Procedure: questionnaires via class teachers three times over two years. Children listed three favourite video games and rated play frequency on a 16-point scale (none to more than ten hours each usual week/weekend), and rated each game's violent and prosocial themes on a four-point scale (0 = never to 4 = almost always; violent item: 'How often do you shoot or kill creatures in this game?').
  • Measures: empathy via the Children's Empathic Attitudes Questionnaire (example: 'I would feel bad if my mother's friend got sick'); helping behaviour via subscales of the Prosocial Orientation Questionnaire (example: 'I would spend time and money to help those in need').
中文理解：为什么非要两个研究
研究一横断面只能给相关，给不了因果；研究二改纵向，两三年内三次测，看时间顺序（T1 玩亲社会游戏 → T2 共情变高 → T3 助人变多），因果链条才立得住。考试问 Prot 的研究设计，先答 study one 是 cross-sectional，study two 是 longitudinal，再各配一句为什么。这个「从相关到因果」的升级逻辑也是 AO3 素材。

10. Prot: Findings, Conclusions and Evaluation

Study two results

Prosocial video game use at Time 1 had a positive indirect effect on prosocial behaviour at Time 3 through empathy at Time 2. Violent video game use at Time 1 had a negative indirect effect on later prosocial behaviour through its effect on empathy. Prosocial games raised empathy which explained later helping; violent games lowered empathy which explained lower helping.

Conclusions

  • Both studies show prosocial media use is positively correlated with prosocial behaviour in real life, and the key to the relationship is empathy.
  • Study two found long-term positive effects of prosocial media and long-term negative effects of violent media, both mediated by changes in empathy.
  • More time using media of any content meant less prosocial behaviour (also mediated by empathy): less time interacting with others means fewer chances to learn empathy.

Evaluation

Strength 1 · Replicated（可重复）

The effects were replicated by Harrington and O'Connell (2016), who found prosocial video game use developed empathic concern and prosocial behaviour in children as reported by teachers, so Prot's results can be considered reliable.

Strength 2 · Reliable inventories

The Interpersonal Reactivity Index is a commonly used self-report inventory known to have internal reliability and construct validity, so other researchers can repeat the study with the same measure.

Strength 3 · Application

Findings demonstrate the power of media: parents can make informed choices, and policymakers and media corporations can decide what to censor or encourage.

Limitation 1 · Self-report and social desirability

Participants rated how often they helped or harmed others in games; social desirability means they may lie to look good, reducing validity, so we cannot be certain about the media-behaviour relationship. However, the ratings produced quantitative data that can be objectively analysed and compared statistically.

Limitation 2 · Sample strengths and limits

Study one's very large sample from seven countries allowed cross-cultural comparison and a general conclusion. But study two used only participants from Singapore, so the culture studied could have influenced the results, making them unrepresentative of children in other countries.

中文理解：研究二的文化局限是必考点
Prot 评价的陷阱是「大样本」三个字只属于研究一：2202 人七个国家是研究一，3034 人全是新加坡孩子是研究二。考试把两个研究的样本搞混就丢分。答题安全写法：研究一跨文化是大优势，研究二单一文化是局限，两个都说才完整。

11. Bastian et al. (2012): Cyber-Dehumanization

Brock Bastian, Jolanda Jetten and Helena Radke: negative effects of violent video games were well documented, but before this research it was unknown whether violent game play could have dehumanising effects（去人性化） on the players themselves: their perception of their own humanity and that of co-players.

Study one · Violent game vs non-violent game

  • Aim: investigate whether playing violent video games has dehumanising consequences for oneself and others, in a context where players were violent towards each other.
  • Sample: 106 participants (74 women, 32 men), undergraduates aged 17 to 34, randomly assigned to the violent game Mortal Kombat (52) or the non-violent Top Spin Tennis (54).
  • Procedure: a two-player game for 15 minutes with a dividing wall so players could not see each other. Questionnaire afterwards: how enjoyable, exciting and frustrating (1–7 Likert-type scales), plus eight 'humanness' questions: four on human nature ('I felt that I was emotional, like I was warm and responsive') and four on human uniqueness ('I felt like I was refined and cultured'), rating themselves and their opponent.
  • Results: both conditions found the games equally frustrating, but Mortal Kombat was more enjoyable and significantly more exciting. Mean self-humanity was lower in the violent condition (3.74 vs 4.35); mean other-humanity lower too (4.43 vs 4.93). Findings remained statistically significant even after accounting for gender, enjoyment and frustration.

Study two · Avatar control

Study one left questions: Mortal Kombat characters are not human; maybe participants saw themselves as game characters? Maybe any conflict, or feeling bad after playing, lowers perceived humanity?

  • Aim: investigate whether playing violently against computer avatars rather than a human opponent affects self-perception of humanity.
  • Sample: 38 undergraduates, mean age 20.18 (10 male, 28 female), paid by course credit or US $10.
  • Procedure: randomly assigned to Call of Duty 2 or Top Spin Tennis, played as co-players against a computer avatar (not opponents), with a split screen showing their own and the avatar's viewpoint. After playing: enjoyment/frustration/excitement ratings, the same humanity measure, plus State Self-Esteem Scale and the 20-item PANAS mood schedule, to ensure self-esteem and mood did not drive the results.
  • Results: self-humanity still lower after the violent game (3.82 vs 4.48), but other-humanity did not differ (4.89 vs 4.86).

Conclusions

  • Playing a violent video game reduces perception of our own humanity even when the opponent is an avatar.
  • Playing violent games does not make us feel bad or see ourselves negatively; it only affects how human we feel.
  • Perception of others' humanity is only affected when the other person is an opponent, not a co-perpetrator.
中文理解：研究二是「排除法」设计
研究二的每个改动都在排除一个备选解释：对手换成电脑（排除真人互动的作用）、合作而非对抗（排除冲突本身的作用）、加测自尊和情绪（排除「玩完心情差所以评自己差」）。结果自我人性化仍然下降，说明是暴力内容本身在起作用。考试解释 study two 的目的时，就用「排除备选解释」这个说法。

12. Bastian: Evaluation

Strength 1 · Real-world application

Gaming is very popular and consoles ever more affordable, so questions about effects on behaviour matter. This study contributes to the debate and has helped justify age certification of games.

Strength 2 · Convergent with prior research

Results concur with previous video game research (Anderson and Bushman, 2001; Anderson, Gentile and Buckley, 2007), adding academic weight to Bastian's unique examination of dehumanisation, which had not been examined previously.

Strength 3 · Ethically appropriate

Individuals were fully informed in their decision to take part; limited if any deception; the team demonstrated competence throughout, maintaining the moral standards expected of research.

Limitation 1 · Victim confound

Participants playing violent games were also victims of game violence (attacked by another player or an avatar). Perhaps it is being a victim that reduced self-humanity scores, not playing violently.

Limitation 2 · Generalisability

A small and biased sample: across both studies the majority were women, a gynocentric（以女性为主） result that may not represent typical computer gaming populations.

Limitation 3 · Rating validity

Bastian himself admits participants might have felt compelled to rate themselves as less human given the context. However, participants indicated they did not suspect the research concerned a link between game violence and dehumanisation.

Wider issue · How psychological understanding develops over time

Games have existed in basic forms for 20 to 30 years, but only in the last ten years have consoles produced life-like perspectives. Gaming was not a focus of psychological interest 40 years ago; now it is at the forefront. Psychology cannot be a static science: it changes with social behaviour and interest, and better knowledge of gaming effects can inform how games are developed in the future.

中文理解：victim 这个混淆变量要会用
Bastian 评价里最高级的一条是 victim confound：被试在游戏里既施暴也挨打，自我人性化下降可能是因为「挨打」而非「施暴」。考试写这条时先摆现象再给解释，这就是 AO3 的因果推理。样本局限（多数是女性）配 gynocentric 这个词，课本原文用它，答题也用它。

(2012), study one: to investigate whether playing violent video games has dehumanising consequences with regard to oneself and others. 一分题不值得冒险：写动词开头的 investigate 句，挂一个具体情境就满分。四个版本的 aim 上面都给了，背你选的那个研究的第一条即可。注意 Prot 要写清 study one 还是 study two，Bastian 同理。

Question 2 — Watson and Rayner (1920) (from the textbook)

Watson and Rayner (1920) used classical conditioning in their study on Little Albert. (a) Identify the unconditioned stimulus (UCS). (1) (b) Explain two ethical weaknesses. (4) (c) Explain one improvement to the sample. (2)

✍️ 2a — Identify the UCS1 mark

The loud noise: the steel bar being struck behind Albert's head. It naturally produced the fear response (the UCR) without learning.

✍️ 2b — Two ethical weaknesses4 marks

Weakness 1 (protection from harm): Albert experienced obvious distress, crying and trying to crawl away, and his fear was deliberately strengthened over many trials, so his psychological well-being was neglected; a modern study would stop the conditioning at the first signs of sustained fear. Weakness 2 (no withdrawal from harm / inability to undo): Albert's mother withdrew him before he could be deconditioned, so the fear he had been given was never removed; researchers today must remove any conditioned response they install, and participants (or guardians) must be able to withdraw at any point with the effects reversed.

中文方法框：伦理弱点四分公式
每条两分：点出伦理问题名词（1 分）+ 在这个研究里的具体表现（1 分）。W&R 最稳的两条：保护（明知会吓哭孩子还反复加码）、退出与善后（妈妈带走时恐惧还没去除）。别只写「不道德」，要挂 Albert 的细节。

✍️ 2c — Improve the sample2 marks

Watson and Rayner used one infant chosen for his unemotional character, so Albert may not represent children of the same age. An improvement would be to use a larger sample of infants selected randomly, across a range of temperaments, checking that none had pre-existing fears. This would make the sample more representative, so the finding that phobias can be classically conditioned could be generalised to infants in general rather than to one unusual child.

Question 3 — The 8-mark evaluation (from the textbook)

Evaluate the contemporary study by Capafóns et al. (1998): Systematic desensitisation in the treatment of the fear of flying. (8)

✍️ 3 — Evaluate Capafóns8 marks

Strength 1 (objectivity): the study used a scientific method of assessment: fear was measured with validated scales and with objective measures such as heart rate and skin temperature, so the outcome did not rely only on what participants said they felt. Strength 2 (control): the control group gave a baseline comparison, and both groups completed the same pre- and post-tests about eight weeks apart in a standardised laboratory environment, so the significant differences (for example fear during flight dropping from 25.6 to 13.25 while the control stayed near 26) can be attributed to the therapy. Limitation 1 (generalisability): the sample of 41 volunteers recruited through a media campaign is small and may attract people unusually motivated to be cured, so the findings may not generalise to all people with a fear of flying. Limitation 2 (validity of self-report): key assessments such as the IDG-FV used closed-ended questions with pre-set responses, so respondents may have picked the answer that best fitted rather than their true view, limiting the validity of the self-report data despite the objective measures. Overall, the combination of objective physiological measures and a waiting-list control makes this a fairly convincing demonstration that systematic desensitisation can reduce fear of flying, though replication with larger samples is needed before it is treated as definitive.

中文方法框：8 分评价带数字才有说服力
四段结构：优点一（客观性：生理指标）→ 优点二（控制：等待组 + 前后测对齐）→ 缺点一（推广性：41 人志愿者）→ 缺点二（效度：封闭式自评）。每段一句「因为…所以…」，最后收一句总结。优点里引用具体数字（25.6 降到 13.25，对照组不动）是这条评价区别于背稿子的地方。

15. Key Terms

classical conditioning 经典条件反射
Learning by association: a neutral stimulus becomes linked to a stimulus that naturally produces a response.
single case experiment 单一个案实验
An experiment conducted on one participant, as in Watson and Rayner's study of Little Albert.
stimulus generalisation 刺激泛化
The conditioned response transferring to objects broadly similar to the conditioned stimulus (furry objects for Albert).
stimulus discrimination 刺激辨别
Responding to the conditioned stimulus but not to other stimuli (Albert played with the wooden blocks).
deconditioning 去除条件反射
Reversing a conditioned response; Albert left the study before his fear could be removed.
hydrocephalus 脑积水
Accumulation of cerebrospinal fluid in the brain; Fridlund et al. (2012) claim Albert suffered it from birth.
systematic desensitisation 系统脱敏
Treatment pairing relaxation with gradual exposure to the feared stimulus; tested by Capafóns et al.
habituation 习惯化
The process by which a response to a given stimulus decreases with repetition.
progressive muscle relaxation 渐进式肌肉放松
Tensing and relaxing one muscle group at a time to build awareness of tension and relaxation.
in vitro / in vivo 想象暴露 / 真实暴露
In vitro exposure uses imagined situations; in vivo uses real-life situations.
waiting-list control group 等待组
A control group waiting to receive the treatment; softens the ethics of withholding therapy.
cross-sectional design 横断设计
Data collected at one time point across groups; finds associations but not causes (Prot study one).
longitudinal design 纵向设计
The same participants measured repeatedly over time; can establish causal order (Prot study two).
empathy 共情
Understanding and sharing others' feelings; the mediating variable between media use and helping behaviour.
mediating variable 中介变量
A variable that explains how one factor produces its effect on another (empathy for Prot).
dehumanization 去人性化
Reduced perception of one's own or others' humanity; Bastian's violent game effect.
human nature / human uniqueness 人性 / 独特性
The two components of Bastian's humanness measure (warm and responsive vs refined and cultured).
PANAS 正负情绪量表
The 20-item Positive and Negative Affect Schedule used in Bastian study two to control for mood.
social desirability 社会期许效应
Participants answering to look good rather than truthfully; a validity threat in Prot's self-reports.
gynocentric 以女性为主
Bastian's sample was majority female across both studies, limiting generalisability to typical gamers.
