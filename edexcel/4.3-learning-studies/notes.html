Using the principles of conditioning classical, investigate whether a human child could learn to be afraid of a previously stimulus — a white rat that initially caused no fear neutral — and whether this reaction would be to similar objects generalised.

W&R: Baseline Test and First Conditioning Trials4.3 STUDIES · PROCEDURE 1/2
Baseline test 基线测试

First, Albert's responses were tested to various stimuli: a , a mask, a white rat, wool and burning dog; cotton; newspaper. He responded adversely to none. Then a was struck on a four-foot steel pole hanging from the ceiling hammer: first hit → startled; second hit → lips began to pucker; third hit → broke down in a fit crying. The hammer strike became the stimulus unconditioned (UCS).

中文理解：基线测试存在的意义
先证明这五样东西都不吓人，再证明响声本身能吓哭他。两头卡住之后，「白鼠 × 响声」配对产生的恐惧才说得清是谁带来的。没有基线，恐惧可能本来就在，因果链就断了。这就是评价里「有 control」的证据。

What was the UCS? the loud noise of the steel bar being struck

Generalisation vs discrimination 泛化与分化

Stimulus generalisation = fear spreading to objects to the rat similar: evidence = he feared the rabbit, dog, seal fur coat and the mask Santa Claus. Stimulus discrimination = no fear of objects clearly from the rat different: evidence = he played calmly with the wooden blocks.

W&R: Conclusions and Evaluation4.3 STUDIES · CLASSIC STUDY
Conclusions 结论
  • A phobia of a previously unfearful object can be learned: rat (NS→CS) + loud noise (UCS) → fear (CR).
  • generalisation was observed: fear transferred to similar animals and objects Stimulus.
  • Fear lasted days after the emotional tests 31, so such conditioned emotional responses have the potential to last a life time.
Evaluation 评价
Point
Strength methodology: control over the stimulus-response link Scientific. Measured fear before conditioning as a comparison baseline; conducted part of the study in another room to eliminate as an extraneous variable context; behaviours at every stage carefully , ensuring replication documented
Weakness 1Lack of validity: laboratory-like location; tasks not those of normal everyday life ecological
Weakness 2: Albert obviously distressed; could not be deconditioned; physical and psychological well-being neglected Ethical issues
Weakness 3Generalisation: one child, selected for his unemotional character, may not represent others of the same age and gender individual
Weakness 4 bias: Albert and researchers all represent one culture (USA) cultural
Later doubtFridlund et al. (): Albert had suffered since birth (build-up of cerebrospinal fluid in the brain) and Watson knew 2012; hydrocephalus — casts doubt on the findings and raises further ethical questions
中文理解：评价 Alberta 的三条主线
方法线（有基线、换房间控制情境，科学）；伦理线（吓哭婴儿还没法消除恐惧）；样本线（就一个情绪稳的娃，还可能脑积水）。三条线任何一条都能写成 4 分题。nature–nurture 也能挂上：响声的惊吓是天生反应，把它绑到白鼠身上是环境干的，两个都有份。

Exam tip from the textbook 课本考试提醒
'Not significant' ≠ worthless
'Not significant' does not mean no difference was found — it means a five per cent greater likelihood the results were due to chance. If two therapies show no significant difference but one is cheap, health authorities can direct resources to the cheaper one.

Prot et al. (2014): Study One4.3 STUDIES · CONTEMPORARY STUDY 2
Background and aim 背景

Prosocial media can encourage behaviour in real life helping. The researchers hypothesised that greater prosocial media use would be associated with more frequent helping, and that this association would be explained by empathy. Aim: investigate the association across different countries, and the impact of age, gender and culture seven.

Sample 样本
CountrynCountrynCountryn
Australia 462China 203Croatia 438
Germany 200Japan 395Romania 233
USA 307Total adolescents and young adults, mean age years (SD = 5.6) 2202; 21

Design: a analysis cross-sectional.

Procedure 步骤
  • Questionnaire online or to face face; list three favourite TV shows, movies and video games with monthly frequency; rate each for prosocial and violent content.
  • Game items included 'How often do characters help each other in this game?' and 'How often do characters try to injure each other?' physically.
  • Empathy: items from the empathic-concern and perspective-taking subscales of the Reactivity Index (IRI) 14; Interpersonal; assumed a stable personality characteristic.
  • Prosocial behaviour: items from the Brief Prosocial Scale ten, e.g. 'I try to be helpful to people even if I don't expect to see them again'.
Results 结果

Race, education, socioeconomic status and parental education were associated with media use or helping not significantly, so not analysed further. In all seven countries there was an association between prosocial media use and helping behaviour with empathy as an influencing variable each.

中文理解：cross-sectional 是「快照」
七个国家同一时刻拍一张合照：谁玩亲社会游戏多、谁共情高、谁爱助人，横向一比。快、量大，但拍不到变化过程，所以得不出因果，这正是 study two 存在的理由。

Conclusions and evaluation 结论与评价
  • Playing a violent game reduces perception of our own humanity, even if the opponent is an avatar. It does not make us feel bad or see ourselves negatively; it only affects how human we feel.
  • application: gaming popular, consoles affordable, animation realistic; such studies helped justify age certification of games Real-world.
  • Results previous research (Anderson and Bushman, 2001; Anderson, Gentile and Buckley, 2007) concur with.
  • Problem: participants were also of game violence (by a player or avatar) — perhaps being a victim lowered self-humanity scores victims.
  • Ethically appropriate: fully , limited deception, competent research team informed.
  • Generalisability: small, biased sample; majority women across both studies ( result) gynocentric — not representative of a typical gaming population.
  • Validity: Bastian admitted participants might have been to rate themselves less human given the context; but participants did not suspect the dehumanisation link compelled.
中文理解：victim confound 是本研究的招牌批评
在游戏里你既打人也挨打。自我人性感降低，可能是「玩暴力」造成的，也可能是「挨打当受害者」造成的，混在一起分不开。考场上一句 victims of game violence 就能体现你真读过这个研究。
