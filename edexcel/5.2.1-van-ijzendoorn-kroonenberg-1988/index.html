5.2.1 CLASSIC STUDY: VAN IJZENDOORN & KROONENBERG (1988)
Cross-cultural patterns of attachment · A meta-analysis of the Strange Situation · 跨文化依恋模式与元分析

Topic E · Developmental psychology Classic study Spec 5.2.1 📖 Textbook Book 2 · Ch.4 · pp.55–58

Learning Objectives 学习目标

  • Describe the aim, method and sample: a meta-analysis of 32 Strange Situation studies, 1990 mother–infant pairs, and the four inclusion criteria every study had to meet.
  • Recall the key findings: the global distribution (B 65% / A 21% / C 14%), the individualist–collectivist pattern, the Li-Repac sample, and intra-cultural variation at 1.5 times inter-cultural variation.
  • Explain the conclusions: great caution when generalising from small samples, and relatively modest cross-cultural differences possibly encouraged by Western mass media.
  • Evaluate the study on comparability, the Bonferroni correction, secondary data and ethics (strengths) against the SES confound, missing regions and the imposed etic (weaknesses).
  • Answer exam questions on the sample (1 mark), one strength of study selection (2 marks) and a full 8-mark evaluation.

What this page is really asking

  1. Can one country's findings travel? Ainsworth's US studies put secure attachment at about 70 per cent. Small studies elsewhere kept failing to match each other — even northern and southern Germany disagreed. Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg asked how big the differences really are.
  2. The answer came from a database, not a lab. They re-analysed the raw data of 32 Strange Situation studies — 1990 mother–infant pairs — as one large data set, without observing a single new infant.
  3. Similar across, different within. Secure (Type B) attachment was the majority type everywhere, but variation within countries was 1.5 times the variation between them — so a small local sample cannot stand in for a nation.
  4. The method has its own trade-offs. Strict selection criteria and the Bonferroni correction protected validity, but socioeconomic status was never controlled and whole regions (South America, Africa, Eastern Europe) were missing from the data.

追问一：小样本研究的结果彼此打架时，不重做实验，怎么判断哪个数字可信？

提示

把已发表的研究合并起来，重新分析全部原始数据：样本一大，单个小样本的偶然性就被摊薄了。这就是 meta-analysis（元分析：把已有研究的数据合并起来再做一次统计分析）。但它带来新问题：合并进来的研究质量参差，怎么保证比出来的是真差异而不是垃圾数据？往下读时留意研究者设了哪些门槛。

追问二：合并之后如果发现两国数字不同，就能说「是文化造成的」吗？

提示

先别急。如果同一国家内部的研究差异比国家之间还大，那国家间的小差异可能根本轮不到「文化」来解释：每项研究自己的地区、家庭收入都可能撑起这个数字。所以要先算清国内差异有多大，再谈国家之间差多少。这正是这项研究最值得记的发现。

带着这两个追问往下读：Van IJzendoorn 和 Kroonenberg 用「合并别人的数据」这个不碰一个婴儿的办法，量出了文化对依恋的真实影响有多大，也顺手证明了「小样本慎推广」不是一句空话。

1. Background, Aim and Method

You have just spotted the hole in every small-sample cross-cultural claim: one lab's twenty-six families cannot speak for a whole country. Marinus Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg (1988) argued that great caution is needed when drawing conclusions from studies like those on pages 20–21: they used small samples, and their findings often did not match the distribution of attachment types in other parts of the same country — attachment types differed between northern and southern Germany. The researchers were curious about the size of intra-cultural differences（文化内差异：同一文化内部研究结果之间的差异） compared with inter-cultural differences（文化间差异：两个或多个文化之间的差异）.

Thinking like a psychologist: culture or nation?

You may rightly feel uneasy about researchers using "culture" to mean a whole nation. Countries such as the US are so large that people in different regions, and in urban versus rural areas, display significant cultural differences from one another. This is why Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg deliberately explored how attachment varies within the same country across regions. It would perhaps be more accurate to call these differences intra-national, to acknowledge that each nation includes families from widely ranging cultural backgrounds.

The aim was to investigate how the distribution of attachment types compares within one culture and across cultures, with the findings of Ainsworth (1978) serving as the baseline（基线：用来作对照的参照数据）, and to get around the problems of small, unrepresentative samples that miss the diversity inside a country.

  • Method: a meta-analysis（元分析：把已有研究的数据合并起来再做一次统计分析） pulling together 32 studies from a range of countries, among them the US, the Netherlands, Germany and Japan, plus one study of Chinese Americans resident in the US. Every study had used the Strange Situation Procedure to classify mothers and infants as Type A, B or C.
  • Procedure: the researchers searched large multinational databases using the key term 'attachment' to find suitable Strange Situation replications. Because they used secondary data（二次数据：其他研究者收集好的现成数据）， there was no procedure as such: they identified suitable data sets and re-analysed the raw data as one large data set.
  • Statistics: they tested whether differences between the distribution of attachment types were greater than expected by chance, using the chi-squared test and a technique called correspondence analysis to compare distributions within and between countries.

Maths tip: guarding against a Type 1 error

A Type 1 error（第一类错误：错把没差异的当成有差异） means a researcher wrongly rejects the null hypothesis, sometimes called a false positive（假阳性：看起来显著，多半只是碰巧）. With 32 studies and many comparisons, the odds of stumbling into a "significant" result by luck rise quickly. Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg used the Bonferroni approach（Bonferroni 校正：把显著性门槛收紧，降低碰巧显著的概率） to reduce the likelihood of a Type 1 error, which increases the validity of their findings.

  • Sample: 1990 pairs of mothers and infants. The 32 studies were all included only if they used:
    • infants aged two years old or younger;
    • samples of 35 or more pairs;
    • typically developing infants (none with learning disabilities) who did not have a twin;
    • infants who had participated only once — studies with overlapping samples were excluded.

Back to the briefing: your supervisor's rule was "database only, no new infants". The researchers worked under exactly that constraint — and turned it into a strength, because the inclusion criteria above made 32 different labs' data comparable in the first place.

中文理解：方法先记三个数：一项元分析，合并了 32 项研究里的 1990 对母婴，数据全部是二次的。四条纳入门槛缺一不可：婴儿不超过 2 岁，样本至少 35 对，参加者为典型发展且没有双胞胎，每名婴儿只出现一次（样本重叠的研究直接剔除）。统计工具两件：chi-squared（卡方检验：看分类数据的差异是否超出碰巧范围）加 correspondence analysis（对应分析：比较国家内与国家间的分布），再加 Bonferroni 校正防「假阳性」。最容易被问到的点：这是二次数据研究，全程没有观察任何新婴儿。

2. Evidence: The Global Picture

Across all 32 studies, the overall distribution of attachment types was far from even — but secure attachment was clearly the majority type. Table 4.1 gives the overall percentages.

Attachment typePercentage of infants receiving this classification in the SSP with their mothers
Type B (secure)65%
Type A (avoidant)21%
Type C (resistant)14%

Table 4.1: distribution of attachment types across the combined sample.

  • Individualist versus collectivist cultures split in opposite directions. The more individualist cultures（个人主义文化：更看重独立与自立的文化，如荷兰、德国） had fewer Type C and more Type A classifications than expected by chance; the opposite was true in the more collectivist cultures（集体主义文化：更看重群体和谐与相互依赖的文化）. In Japan, there were no Type A classifications at all.
  • The Chinese American sample sat below the global secure rate. In the study by Li-Repac (1982), only 50% of infants were classified Type B — fewer than expected — while Type A and Type C were distributed equally, at 25% each.
  • Within-country variation dwarfed the between-country gap. Intra-cultural variation was 1.5 times the level of variation between cultures, and it was particularly large among the studies conducted in Germany and the US. The combined US samples matched the global distribution, yet several individual US studies showed more Type C than Type A classifications.

Textbook erratum 课本勘误

The chapter text states that "Type A was the most common attachment type (the mode) in seven out of the eight countries", but Table 4.1 on the same page shows Type B at 65% and Type A at only 21%, so Type A cannot be the mode in seven countries. The original 1988 paper reported that Type B (secure) was the modal classification in seven of the eight countries, which is consistent with the table. If an exam question asks which type was most common, answer secure attachment (Type B).

Back to the briefing: the database answered question one — the big picture really is similar, with secure attachment the majority type everywhere. But the 1.5× figure quietly answers question two before you even ask it: a number from one small study is not a number for the nation.

中文理解：结果记「一表三线」。一表：全球合并分布 B 65%、A 21%、C 14%，安全型在每个国家都是多数。三线：① 个体主义国家（荷兰、德国）A 型偏多 C 型偏少，集体主义国家相反，日本干脆没有 A 型；② 华裔美国样本（Li-Repac 1982）B 低到 50%，A、C 各 25%，安全型低于预期；③ 国内差异是跨国差异的 1.5 倍，德国和美国内部差异最大，美国合并后总数对上全球分布，但内部有几项研究 C 型反而多于 A 型。勘误要记：课本正文说「A 型在 7/8 国是众数」是笔误，原始论文和表格都是 B 型（安全型）在 7/8 国为众数。

3. Conclusion: Similar Across, Different Within

Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg concluded that 'great caution should be exercised' when generalising from small samples to entire nations, because of the wide intra-cultural differences. Unusual results need to be replicated before anyone uses them to draw conclusions about cross-cultural differences in attachment. At the same time, they described the cross-cultural differences they did find as 'relatively modest', and suggested one reason: Western mass media may encourage certain approaches to parenting, quietly narrowing the differences between countries.

The findings speak to three bigger questions from the chapter:

  • Bowlby's theory: secure attachment being the majority type in every country fits the idea that humans everywhere are wired to form attachment bonds, but the 1.5× within-country variation shows the environment shapes where each child lands on the A/B/C distribution.
  • Ainsworth's caregiving hypothesis: if caregiver sensitivity drives attachment type, then differences in caregiving, whether cultural or economic, should shift the distribution, which is exactly what the individualist–collectivist pattern and the SES confound (see evaluation) both suggest.
  • Nature and nurture: the global similarity supports a shared human starting point (nature), while the within-country variation and the media explanation show nurture doing the fine-tuning.

Back to the briefing: your two questions now have a combined answer. Cross-national generalisation is possible but modest, because the between-country differences are small compared with the noise inside each country.

中文理解：结论两句话加三个回望。结论：① 从小样本推广到整个国家要极其谨慎，异常结果必须先被重复出来才能当成跨国差异的证据；② 跨国差异「相对温和」，西方大众媒体可能在背后推动各国的养育方式趋同。三个回望：安全型处处过半支持 Bowlby 的普遍性；1.5 倍国内差异和媒体解释把天平拨向教养一侧；caregiving hypothesis（照料假说：照料者的敏感程度决定依恋类型）能同时解释个体主义与集体主义的分化。

4. Evaluation: What Merging 32 Studies Can and Cannot Tell Us

Strengths

  • Strict, comparable selection. The researchers carefully selected studies so they were similar in key variables: all children aged two or under, none with learning disabilities, all observed with their mother. That shared ground made the 32 data sets comparable, which strengthens the validity of the comparison.
  • Statistical caution built in. The Bonferroni approach reduced the likelihood of a Type 1 error, so the differences reported are unlikely to be chance findings, which increases the validity of the conclusions.
  • Large, diverse sample at no extra risk. Secondary data allowed the researchers to gather nearly 2000 infants from many cultures, increasing external validity; the US data alone combined 18 studies and represented multiple states, which is exactly what a single small sample could never do.
  • No ethical issues. Because secondary data were used, no additional infants were exposed to the Strange Situation Procedure.

Weaknesses

  • Socioeconomic status was a confounding variable. Some studies sampled professional families (high SES), others families from low-income areas. so the within-country differences may reflect economic conditions rather than cultural ones.
  • Whole regions were missing. There were no studies from South America, Africa or Eastern Europe, and only one from Asia, so the findings lack population validity. Remote regions less influenced by globalisation, such as Western media, might have shown even greater intra-cultural and cross-cultural differences.
  • The imposed etic problem. The findings of the Japanese studies may lack validity because of an imposed etic（强加的跨文化等值：把一种文化里发展出的测试范式直接搬到另一种文化使用）： infants from very traditional families who had not experienced separation before may have shown extreme separation distress as a temporary reaction to the testing situation, and been classified as Type C when this did not represent their typical behaviour towards their mothers.
  • Numbers without meaning. The study relied entirely on quantitative data, so the meanings behind the observed behaviours were ignored; a qualitative or ethnographic approach could have explained why distributions differed.

A fair verdict: the method is honest about its own limits. Selection criteria, Bonferroni and secondary data make the global picture trustworthy; the SES confound, the missing regions and the imposed etic mean the cultural interpretation of each gap needs caution.

Exam tip: the 8-mark evaluation is marked AO1 4 + AO3 4. Spend the AO1 marks on aim, method and findings (meta-analysis of 32 studies, 1990 pairs, B 65% / A 21% / C 14%, intra-cultural variation 1.5×), then make linked AO3 judgements — for example: strict selection criteria + Bonferroni support validity, but the SES confound + missing regions + imposed etic limit how far the "culture" part of the story can be pushed.

Close the loop

Your two briefing questions now have answers. Cross-national generalisation is possible but modest: secure attachment is the majority type in every country (65% globally), so the big picture is similar, but with intra-cultural variation 1.5 times larger than the between-country gap, and whole regions missing from the data, a small local sample cannot stand in for a nation. The database, not the observation room, turned out to be the tool that could answer the question.

Reflect before the Exam Lab: the SES confound and the imposed etic problem could both explain away parts of the "cultural" differences. Before trusting any single-country distribution, what would you want to see: more samples from that country, or samples matched on family income?

中文理解：评价记「四绿四红」加一句裁决。绿：纳入标准让 32 项研究可比较（提升效度）、Bonferroni 降低假阳性概率（提升效度）、二次数据攒出近 2000 名婴儿的大样本（美国一国之内的 18 项研究覆盖多个州）、不碰新婴儿所以零伦理问题。红：家庭社会经济地位没控制（国内差异可能是经济而非文化）、南美非洲东欧零研究且亚洲只有一项（人口效度不足）、日本研究存在 imposed etic（传统家庭的分离哭闹被误判成 C 型）、纯定量数据丢掉了行为背后的含义。裁决句：全球大图景可信，「文化」层面的每一条解释都要留有余地。

5. Exam Lab

Q1 · Describe 1 mark

Describe the sample used in Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg's (1988) study.

The sample was secondary data from 1990 pairs of mothers and infants, taken from 32 studies of the Strange Situation Procedure conducted in eight countries, including the US, the Netherlands, Germany and Japan.

Marking guidance: 1 mark for an accurate description. Both the number of pairs (1990) and the source (32 studies / eight countries) should appear.

Q2 · Explain one strength 2 marks

Explain one strength of the way Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg selected the studies for their meta-analysis.

Strength: the inclusion criteria made very different studies comparable. Only studies with infants aged two or under, samples of 35 or more pairs, typically developing infants without twins, and non-overlapping samples were included. Because every data set shared these key variables, differences in attachment distribution between countries could be attributed to the countries rather than to differences in age, development or repeated counting of the same infants, increasing the validity of the comparison.

Marking guidance: 1 mark for identifying a valid strength of the selection (comparability through inclusion criteria; the Bonferroni correction; excluding overlapping samples), 1 mark for explaining why it strengthens the study (validity of the cross-cultural comparison).

Q3 · Evaluate 8 marks

Evaluate Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg's (1988) study.

AO1 — what the researchers did and found. Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg conducted a meta-analysis of 32 Strange Situation studies from eight countries, including the US, the Netherlands, Germany and Japan, re-analysing the raw data of 1990 mother–infant pairs as one large data set. All studies met strict inclusion criteria (infants aged two or under, samples of 35 or more pairs, typically developing infants without twins, no overlapping samples). They used the chi-squared test and correspondence analysis with the Bonferroni approach, taking Ainsworth's (1978) findings as a baseline. Overall, 65% of infants were classified Type B, 21% Type A and 14% Type C; individualist cultures such as the Netherlands and Germany showed more Type A and fewer Type C, collectivist cultures the reverse, and Japan showed no Type A at all; the Chinese American sample (Li-Repac, 1982) showed 50% Type B with A and C at 25% each. Intra-cultural variation was 1.5 times the variation between cultures.

AO3 — strength: comparability and statistical caution. Careful selection made the 32 studies similar in key variables, increasing validity, and the Bonferroni approach reduced the likelihood of a Type 1 error, so reported differences are unlikely to be chance.

AO3 — strength: scale without extra risk. Secondary data produced a large, culturally diverse sample of nearly 2000 infants, increasing external validity — the US data alone combined 18 studies across multiple states — and because no new infants were observed, there were no ethical issues.

AO3 — weakness: socioeconomic status and coverage gaps. SES was a confounding variable: some studies sampled professional families and others low-income areas, so intra-cultural differences may be economic rather than cultural. There were no studies from South America, Africa or Eastern Europe and only one from Asia, weakening population validity; remote regions less influenced by globalisation might show even greater differences.

AO3 — weakness: imposed etic and numbers without meaning. The Japanese findings may lack validity through an imposed etic: infants from very traditional families may have shown extreme separation distress as a temporary reaction to the unfamiliar testing situation and been misclassified as Type C. The reliance on quantitative data also meant the meanings behind the behaviours were ignored.

Overall judgement. The global picture — secure attachment the majority type everywhere, with modest cross-cultural differences — is well supported. The cultural explanations behind each gap need caution, because economic confounds, missing regions and the imposed etic all offer rival explanations.

Marking guidance: AO1 (4) accurate description of aim, method, sample and findings; AO3 (4) linked evaluation with evidence (Bonferroni; secondary data scale; SES confound; missing regions; imposed etic; quantitative-only data). A judgement is needed for the top marks — not four unconnected strengths followed by four weaknesses.

7. Key Terms

meta-analysis
元分析：把已有研究的数据合并起来再做一次统计分析；样本变大，单个小样本的偶然性被摊薄（本研究合并了 32 项研究、1990 对母婴）
intra-cultural differences
文化内差异：同一文化内部各研究之间的差异；本研究发现它是跨国差异的 1.5 倍
inter-cultural differences
文化间差异：两个或多个文化之间的差异；本研究测得「相对温和」
secondary data
二次数据：其他研究者收集好的现成数据；本研究因此没有观察任何新婴儿，也没有伦理问题
baseline
基线：用来作对照的参照数据；本研究用 Ainsworth (1978) 的分布作基线
Type 1 error / false positive
第一类错误（假阳性）：错把没有差异的当成有差异；比较次数越多越容易碰上
Bonferroni approach
Bonferroni 校正：把显著性门槛收紧，降低碰巧显著的概率；本研究用它降低第一类错误的风险
imposed etic
强加的跨文化等值：把一种文化里发展出的概念和测试范式直接搬到另一种文化使用；日本样本的 C 型可能由此误判
individualist / collectivist culture
个人主义文化看重独立与自立（荷兰、德国：A 型偏多）；集体主义文化看重群体和谐与相互依赖（C 型比例相反方向）
confounding variable (SES)
混淆变量：没被控制却影响结果的变量；本研究里家庭社会经济地位可能才是国内差异的真正来源
population validity
人口效度：结论能否推广到更大范围的人群；南美、非洲、东欧零研究，亚洲只有一项，限制了本研究的推广
