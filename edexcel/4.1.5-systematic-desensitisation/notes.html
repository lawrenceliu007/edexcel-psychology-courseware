The whole therapy in one sentence 一句话总结
Because you cannot be anxious and relaxed at the same time (reciprocal inhibition), a client who builds a hierarchy of fears with the therapist, learns relaxation, and climbs one rung at a time will — through counterconditioning — replace the learned fear with calm: 18 of Capafons et al.'s (1998) 20 flying-phobia clients did exactly that.

3 · What systematic desensitisation is4.1.5 SYSTEMATIC DESENSITISATION
Key term — learn this word for word

Systematic desensitisation: a directive treatment typically used to treat disorders, such as phobias, developed by in the 190s and based on the principles of conditioning.

The name says it all 名字拆解
WordMeaningIn therapy
systematicGradually facing the phobic object via a of exposure, from least fearful to most fearfulNobody starts by holding a spider — start with a picture
desensitisationBrought about by techniques taught facing the objectRelaxation must be available the moment fear appears
The logic in one sentence 一句话逻辑

Phobias are acquired through an unhelpful response to a stimulus — so just as you can learn to fear something, you can the fear by relaxing in contact with the phobic object.

中文理解 名字就是方法
systematic（系统的）＝按「恐惧等级表」一级一级来，不许跳级；desensitisation（脱敏）＝越来越「不敏感」，像打疫苗从小剂量开始。
一句话：先练好放松，再按等级表一点点接近，直到害怕消失。

4 · The one rule: reciprocal inhibition4.1.5 SYSTEMATIC DESENSITISATION
Key term — the engine of the whole therapy

Reciprocal inhibition（交互抑制）: you cannot be and at the same time.

Draw it 画一画

Sketch the balance beam: one side ANXIETY (fast heart rate, tension), the other RELAXATION (slow breathing, loose muscles). Label which side goes down when the other goes up.

Why the order of the therapy follows from the rule 顺序问题

If the client meets the spider first and learns relaxation afterwards, why will the technique fail?

中文理解 为什么「同时」这么关键
紧张和放松不能同时全开。手上已经握着一杯水，就没办法同时握拳。
治疗师要做的是先让你学会「握水杯」（放松），再把你带到害怕的东西面前。放松必须像灭火器——火灾发生前就备好。

Counterconditioning or extinction? 辨析

Extinction would present the spider repeatedly without anything unpleasant until the fear fades. How is counterconditioning more active than that?

The sentence to remember 背这句
Counterconditioning does not erase the old response — it attaches a competing response to the old stimulus, and because the two responses inhibit each other, the old one cannot run.

11 · Evaluation: strengths 3 and 44.1.5 SYSTEMATIC DESENSITISATION
Strength 3 — self-administration 可自助

Because the method is a set of explicit steps, it can be delivered through such as apps or software. That widens access for people who cannot travel, cannot afford a therapist, or feel too ashamed to seek one.

Strength 4 — few ethical issues 伦理风险低

The techniques — the fear and techniques — ensure the client is not exposed to any high-anxiety situation. There is no deception and the client controls the pace. Compare flooding, which deliberately puts the client in the most frightening situation at once.

中文理解 为什么「伦理风险低」是加分点
同属行为疗法的 flooding（满灌疗法）直接把人放进最害怕的情境，效果快但可能造成创伤。SD 的等级表相当于安全绳。写「ethical」时必须说清为什么（等级表＋放松保证不进入高焦虑），否则是空话。

12 · Evaluation: limitations4.1.5 SYSTEMATIC DESENSITISATION
Four limitations 四条限制
LimitationThe reason behind it
1. Only some anxiety disorders The object must be clearly for a hierarchy to be built — so it is inappropriate for anxiety disorder, where the anxiety spreads across many situations.
2. Evolutionary fears resist it Phobias with a survival component — the dark, dangerous animals, heights — may be -rooted; they once kept our ancestors alive, so they are very difficult to remove.
3. Symptom, not cause SD changes the and says nothing about why the fear arose; if the phobia expresses an unresolved conflict, the anxiety may reappear in another form.
4. Depends on client skills The client must be able to on cue and tolerate exposure; self-help apps remove the therapist's judgement about when to stop.
中文理解 三个限制其实是一条逻辑
SD 要求「具体、可分级、可接近的恐惧对象」：对象不明确（GAD）＝做不出等级表；对象是进化写进基因的＝等级表压不住；恐惧只是更深问题的表面＝治好表面里面还在。

Answer key (1 of 2) — pp. 2–114.1.5 SYSTEMATIC DESENSITISATION
p.2 引入

belief · response. Quick check: (b) — the therapy pairs the feared object with an incompatible state (relaxation); explaining targets thoughts and avoidance makes fear worse.

p.3 两对概念

Biological treatments work through the body; examples: medication, psychosurgery. Directive = instructs; non-directive = makes sense of the client's experiences. SD: directive, psychological. Psychoanalysis: non-directive, psychological. Medication: (not directive — biological treatment), biological.

p.4 定义

anxiety disorders; Joseph Wolpe; 1950s; classical. systematic = gradually facing the object via a hierarchy of exposure (least → most fearful); desensitisation = brought about by relaxation techniques taught before facing the object. Logic: unhelpful learned response → you can unlearn the fear.

p.5 交互抑制

anxious / relaxed. Order question: because relaxation must already be present to inhibit the fear — if fear comes first there is nothing to inhibit it, and the fear is (at best) unchanged or reinforced. Relaxation must be automatic before exposure.

p.6 五步 1–2

nature of the anxiety; possible triggers. hierarchy: least → most. Key detail: client input (client knows which rung feels worse). Bird example order: thinking about a bird → seeing a picture → same room as a caged bird → (e.g.) bird in the garden → holding the bird.

p.7 五步 3–5

suit them best; visualisation. gradually; client. pairings; relaxation. Order: functional analysis → hierarchy of fears → relaxation training → gradual exposure → counterconditioning.

p.8 等级表

Numbering: 1 imagine the spider on your hand · 2 look at a picture in a book · 3 be in the same room · 4 walk towards the spider · 5 hold the spider. Rules: reinforced (too early exposure strengthens fear); relax.

p.9 in vivo / in vitro

real; imaginary; transfer. VR: what appears and when. Quick check: imagining = in vitro; holding the real spider = in vivo.

p.10 放松训练

heart; releasing; peaceful. Why it fails: under fear the body reverts to whatever it has practised — a technique used for the first time under threat is not automatic, so it cannot compete with the anxiety response.

p.11 Capafons et al. (1998)

41; flying. 20; 21, waiting; 18, anxious. What it shows: SD beats no treatment in a clinical phobia. What it does not prove: placebo effects (waiting control ≠ placebo), behaviour change (self-report only, not boarding a plane), long-term maintenance (no follow-up).
