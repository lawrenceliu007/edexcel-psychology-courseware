The whole topic in one sentence 一句话总结
A sampling technique is the rule that decides who gets to stand in for everyone: stratified builds a miniature of the population (most representative), random gives equal chances but leaves the outcome to luck, and opportunity and volunteer buy convenience at the price of sampling bias — which limits how far conclusions can be generalised back to the target population.

2 · Sampling bias1.2.5 SAMPLING TECHNIQUES
Definition 定义

If the sample gathered is not representative, because of an or -representation of a particular type of participant in the sample, a sampling will occur.

What decides the technique 什么决定技术选择

The technique used will depend on the of research being conducted and the of the participants.

Quick sort 快速判断
StatementRepresentative / biased / can't tell?
A sample of 100 drawn by lottery from the full school roll
A sample of 100 students who all attend the same chess club
A sample where 70% are boys although the school is 50/50
中文理解 偏差 = 长得不像
sampling bias：样本里某类人太多或太少（over-/under-represented）。
检验只问一句：样本长得像不像总体？不像 → 有偏差 → 推广受限。
注意：bias 是结果（样本变形），不是方法名——任何技术都可能产出 bias。

Where it breaks 两个裂缝

① You can select an sample at random, too — chance has no memory.
② Even a representative list needs from each participant; those who to take part leave you with an unrepresentative final sample.
③ Like stratified sampling, random sampling is consuming.

Link to the classics 呼应经典研究
