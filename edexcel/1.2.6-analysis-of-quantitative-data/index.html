1.2.6 ANALYSIS OF QUANTITATIVE DATA
Data tables · Central tendency · Dispersion · Graphs · Distributions · 定量数据分析

Topic A · Social psychology Methods Spec 1.2.6 📖 Textbook Book 1 · Ch.5 · pp.45–58

What this chapter is really asking

  1. Numbers need a summary before they mean anything. Raw scores are just a list; every tool on this page exists to compress that list into something readable.
  2. Two kinds of summary. Central tendency finds the typical score (mean, median, mode); dispersion finds how spread out the scores are (range, IQR, standard deviation).
  3. Then a picture. Bar charts for categories, histograms for continuous scales, and distribution curves for the overall shape.
  4. Shape matters. A normal curve is symmetric and its three averages coincide; a skewed curve pulls the mean away from the peak.
  5. Part-whole language. Percentages, ratios and fractions express how a part relates to the whole, and exam calculations often test exactly this.

Learning Objectives 学习目标

  • Turn a table of raw scores into a conclusion by summarising it, and quote the numbers to justify what you say.
  • Calculate mean, median and mode, and choose the right one for nominal, ordinal or interval/ratio data.
  • Calculate range, interquartile range and standard deviation, and explain why the SD is the strongest measure of spread.
  • Read and draw summary tables, bar charts and histograms, and say which graph suits which kind of data.
  • Convert between percentages, ratios and fractions and calculate a percentage of a frequency.
  • Describe normal and skewed distributions and place the mean, median and mode on each curve.

1. Getting Started — Reading a Table of Averages

A researcher gathered data on a range of variables from a sample of students. The table shows the average scores for each variable for sports students and science students.

Sport studentsScience students
Average sleep per night (hours)89
Average time spent revising each day (mins)5090
Average time spent exercising per week (hours)255
Average percentage grade on a recent test70%71%
Average number of merits achieved over a year1211
Average peer group size84

What does this data tell us about the students? Sport students exercise far more and revise far less, yet their grades are almost identical to the science students' grades. Science students have smaller peer groups. Notice that you could not say any of this from a pile of individual scores: the table works because the scores have already been summarised into averages.

Two kinds of data

Investigations can produce quantitative data（定量数据：用数字记录的结果） or qualitative data. Investigations that gather quantitative data produce numerical results called raw scores（原始分数：每个参与者各自的那一个数）. This chapter is about what to do with those numbers. Qualitative data, and how to analyse it thematically, is a separate skill covered later.

中文理解：这一章要解决的事
研究做完以后手里是一堆数字，可能是 30 人的服从评分，可能是全班的记忆词数，也可能是每个学生的身高。数字本身不会说话，必须先压缩成几个能看的量：平均在哪（集中趋势）、散开多远（离散程度）、整体长什么样（分布）。这一章就是一套「把数字变成结论」的工具箱：先整理成表，再算平均和散布，然后画成图，最后从图上读出趋势。每一节对应工具箱里的一件工具。

2. From Scores to Conclusions — The Analysis Pipeline

Every quantitative analysis follows the same route. Wherever a question on the exam says "analyse the data" or "what conclusions can be drawn", you are walking this pipeline:

Figure: the analysis pipeline, from raw scores to a defensible conclusion

原始分数是一个人一个数，谁也读不出规律。第一步整理成数据表（原始表或频次表），让数字排队；第二步算概括统计量（典型值和散布），把整列数字压成两三个数；第三步画图，让肉眼能看见模式；第四步才下结论，而且结论必须能指回前面某张表或某幅图里的具体数字。顺序不能颠倒：没概括就画图，画出来的是噪声；没数字支撑的结论，只是感想。

3. Data Tables — Raw and Frequency

Data tables（数据表） present the quantitative data found in an investigation. They can show the raw data gathered, or frequencies, or the summary statistics calculated from them.

Raw data table

A raw data table（原始数据表：所有单个分数的原样罗列） lists every individual value measured in the study. Below are the individual self-ratings of obedience (out of 10) from a small study of males and females.

4. Measures of Central Tendency

Central tendency（集中趋势：找最能代表整组数据的典型值） is a descriptive statistic that calculates the average or most typical value in a data set. Calculations which describe trends from raw data, including the mean, median, mode and standard deviation, are called descriptive statistics（描述性统计：用几个数概括一批数据的走向）.

The arithmetic mean

mean（平均数）：把所有分数加起来除以分数个数。X = ΣX ÷ n

For example: data set 3, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13. ΣX = 58, divided by the number of scores (7) gives a mean of 8.3.

The mean is the most sensitive and therefore the most powerful measure of central tendency because all the scores in the data set are used in the calculation. But the same sensitivity is its weakness: it can be affected by extreme values. The mean is often used on interval/ratio data.

The median

median（中位数）：把分数从小到大排好后正中间的那一个。
  • Odd number of scores: 3, 6, 8, 9, 10 → the median is 8.
  • Even number of scores: 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 → the mean of the two middle scores, 6 and 7, gives a median of 6.5.

The median is a simple calculation and is not affected by extreme scores. However, it is less sensitive than the mean because it does not include all the values in its calculation, and it is not useful on small data sets because the middle score may not represent the typical score. The median is typically used on ordinal data.

The mode

mode（众数）：出现次数最多的分数。

Data set 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 4, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 9 → the mode is 7, the most frequent score. If there are two most frequent scores the data set is bi-modal and both should be reported. If more than two modes appear, the mode becomes a meaningless measure.

The mode is used on nominal data and is very easy to determine, and it is not affected by extreme scores. But it is not useful on small data sets and is less sensitive than the mean, because not all the values are included in the calculation.

均值最敬业也最脆弱：每个分数都参与计算，所以最能代表整组数据，但一个极端分数就能把它拽偏。中数只看位置：排好队取中间，极端值再大也碰不到它，代价是没有用到全部信息。众数最直观：数出现次数就行，而且它是名称类数据唯一能用的「平均」。数据类型决定选择：能归类的用众数，能排名的用中数，能加减的用均值。

5. Measures of Dispersion

Dispersion（离散程度：分数互相之间拉开多远） is a descriptive statistic that calculates the spread of scores in the data set. Measures of central tendency can be misleading if you do not know the variation between the scores: two classes can share the same mean while one is full of similar scores and the other is all extremes.

Range

range（全距）：最高分减最低分，最简单的散布量数。

Data set 2, 4, 6, 8, 9 → the range is 9 − 2 = 7. A high range means the scores are spread out; a low range means they are closer together.

The range is affected by extreme scores, so it may mislead when there are outliers in the data set. It also does not tell us whether the scores are bunched around the mean or more equally distributed.

Interquartile range

interquartile range（四分位距：掐头去尾后的全距）：切掉最低和最高的四分之一，取剩下中间一半分数的全距。

If a data set has extreme scores, it is often better to calculate the interquartile range instead. Cutting off the top and bottom 25% removes exactly the outliers that distort the ordinary range.

Standard deviation

standard deviation（标准差：每个分数离平均值平均有多远）：用一个数概括所有分数围绕均值的散布。

Deviation（离差：单个分数与均值之差） is the distance of each value from the arithmetic mean. If the mean obedience rating for the male participants was 7, a male rated 9 has a deviation of +2; a male rated 5 has a deviation of −2. Every score has a deviation, so a single value is needed to represent them all: the standard deviation. The higher the standard deviation, the greater the spread of scores around the mean.

The five-step calculation, worked in full

Take the data set 70, 80, 90, 100, 110, 120, 130, whose mean is 100:

  1. Subtract the mean from each score to get the deviation scores (done above).
  2. Square each deviation to remove the minus signs (final column).
  3. Sum the squared deviations: 2800.
  4. Divide by the number of scores minus 1: 2800 ÷ (7 − 1) = 2800 ÷ 6 = 466.6. This figure is the variance（方差）.
  5. Take the square root of the variance: √466.6 = 21.6. This is the standard deviation.

6. Summary Tables

Summary tables（汇总表：把集中趋势和离散量数放进同一张表） represent the measures of central tendency and dispersion clearly, usually one column per group. The obedience self-ratings from Section 3, fully analysed:

MalesFemales
Mean obedience rating4.17.4
Median obedience rating47
Mode obedience rating46, 7 and 9
Range of obedience ratings33
Standard deviation11.2

Reading it: the typical score is higher for females than males, on every measure of central tendency. The modal score for females is not useful as three modes have been calculated. The spread of scores, indicated by the range, is the same for both groups, and the standard deviations are similar, suggesting a roughly equal spread around the mean in both conditions. The conclusion: females consistently rate themselves as more obedient than males, and males consistently rate themselves as less obedient than females.

中文理解：读汇总表的固定顺序
先看典型值：均值、中数逐行比较两组谁高。再看散布：全距和标准差回答「高出来的那组是人人如此，还是少数人拉高」。最后看异常信号：这组女生的众数出现了三个（6、7、9），等于没有众数，写结论时要说明这一点而不是硬拿它当典型值。

7. Graphical Representation of Data

Graphs illustrate summary data or data frequencies. The two most common both use bars, but they answer different questions:

Bar chart 条形图

Presents data from a categorical variable（类别变量）, such as the mean, median or mode of each category. The categories sit on the x-axis and the height of the bars represents the value. The bars are separated by a space, because the categories are discrete: nothing exists between "males" and "females".

Histogram 直方图

Presents the distribution of scores by illustrating the frequency of values. The possible values run along the x-axis as a continuous scale. The bars are joined, because the data is continuous rather than categorical: a score of 7.5 really does sit between 7 and 8.

Figure: the bar gap is the whole difference between the two graphs

Patterns within the data may be easier to see if the data is grouped before being shown on a graph. Raw scores in a graph look messy and are not useful to draw conclusions from; graphical representations are supposed to be meaningful and summative, and plotting raw data does not achieve this.

Exam tip: If you are asked to draw a graph from a table, you must plot the values carefully, label both axes clearly and give the graph a title. If you are asked to interpret a graph, describe the trends and justify your conclusions by referring to the data presented.
中文理解：条缝即分界
判断一张图该不该留缝，只看 x 轴上的东西是什么：是彼此独立的类别（男女、发型），条与条留空，用条形图；是有连续刻度的量（年龄、分数），条连成一片，用直方图。另一个易错点：图是用来概括数据的，把几十个原始分数直接堆上去，画出来一片混乱，什么结论都得不到；先分组或先算汇总值，再画。

8. Percentages, Ratios and Fractions

Both ratios and fractions show how a part relates to a whole or to another part. They turn counts into comparable quantities.

Percentage

A percentage score gives the relative proportion of people who achieved a particular score: divide the number who achieved the score by the total number, and multiply by 100.

Example: 5 people achieved an obedience score of 9, out of 49 participants. 5 ÷ 49 × 100 = 10.20%. About one participant in ten scored exactly 9.

Ratio

ratio（比）：两个量之间的倍数关系，各部分之间用冒号隔开。
  • Part-to-whole ratio: 5 : 10 shows five parts out of a total of ten.
  • Part-to-part ratio: in a group of five people, 3 : 2 could represent 3 girls to 2 boys.
  • Worked example: 100 people obeyed a command and 500 did not, so 100 : 500, which simplifies to 1 : 5.

Fraction

fraction（分数）：分子是被数的部分，分母是整体总量。

If 100 people did not obey and 500 people did obey, obeying participants are 100 out of a total 600: 100/600, which simplifies to 1/6.

Fractions convert easily to decimals by dividing the numerator by the denominator (1/4 = 0.25), and decimals convert to percentages by multiplying by 100 (0.25 × 100 = 25%).

中文理解：三件套各管一头
百分比回答「一百个人里有几个」，比回答「这一份是那一份的几倍」，分数回答「占了整体的几分之几」。同一个事实可以有三种写法：100 人服从、500 人不服从，写成比是 1 : 5，写成分数是 100/600 即 1/6，写成百分比约是 16.7%。算题时先分清问的是「部分比部分」还是「部分比整体」，比值和分数的分母由此决定。

9. The Normal Distribution

Small samples only support the summary statistics seen so far. With larger samples it becomes useful to examine the overall distribution（分布：整组分数的整体形状）, the frequency of each value across the data set, and to estimate the distribution of scores in the whole population.

When the frequency distribution of a population is plotted, many measurements form a bell-shaped curve: a normal distribution（正态分布：中间高两头低的对称钟形）. Height is the classic example: most people gather around the average, and the further a score is from the average, the rarer it is.

Figure: a normal distribution curve, with the 68% and 95% bands marked

Three features define the normal distribution:

  • Symmetry around the mid-point. The mean, median and mode should all be aligned at the centre.
  • The tails never touch the horizontal axis. Extreme scores are always possible, just vanishingly rare.
  • Fixed proportions under the curve. It is a rule of normal distribution that 68% of the population falls between one standard deviation each side of the mid-point, and 95% falls between two standard deviations each side.

Using σ to count people

Standard deviation is given the symbol σ, so 1σ means one standard deviation. Example: the standard deviation for a set of obedience scores was 2.8 and the mean score was 7. One standard deviation above the mid-point is 7 + 2.8 = 9.8; one below is 4.2. So 68% of the sample would score between 4.2 and 9.8, and 95% would fall between two standard deviations each side of the mid-point, that is between 1.4 and 12.6. To know exactly what values those intervals represent, the standard deviation must be calculated on the raw scores.

中文理解：钟形曲线为什么重要
正态分布三个记忆点：三点重合（均值、中数、众数都站在正中间）、尾巴不落地（极端分数永远存在只是极少）、比例固定（±1σ 装下约 68% 的人，±2σ 装下约 95%）。最后的例子演示怎么用这两个比例做估算：知道均值 7、标准差 2.8，就能断言大多数人的分数落在 4.2 到 9.8 之间。这把「散布量数」从描述工具变成了预测工具。

10. Skewed Distributions

Some distributions are not symmetrical: they are skewed（偏态：峰不在中间，被挤向一边）. The cause is usually the difficulty of the test or the nature of the sample.

Figure: the tail always drags the mean; the median stays in the middle and the mode stays on the peak

How each skew arises

  • Negative skew: if a test is easy, or the aptitude of the sample is unusually high, most people score highly. Scores bunch on the right with a tail on the left. The mean is pulled left by the few low scores, so the mean is lower than the mode.
  • Positive skew: if a test is particularly difficult, or the aptitude of the sample is low, most people achieve low scores. Scores bunch on the left with a tail on the right. The mean is pulled right by the few high scores, so the mean is higher than the mode.

Placing the three averages

When plotting the mean, median and mode on any distribution curve: the median is always exactly in the middle of the x-axis, the mode sits where the most values are (the peak), and the mean is dragged toward the tail, right on a positive skew and left on a negative skew.

A memory trick

A common confusion is which skew is "positive". Remember: a positive skew has its tail at the positive (high) end of the horizontal axis, and a negative skew has its tail at the negative (low) end. Some students draw a face on the curve so it becomes a whale: a whale swimming towards the vertical axis is coming home, so it is positive; a whale swimming away is negative, because it is leaving.

中文理解：尾巴拽走均值
偏态曲线只需要抓两件事。第一，命名跟尾巴走：尾巴拖在分数高的一端叫正偏态，拖在低的一端叫负偏态。第二，三个平均数的排队规律：中数永远在正中间，众数站在峰顶（分数最密集处），均值被少数极端分数的尾巴拉走。道理很朴素：均值要平均每一个分数，尾部的少数离群值就在它身上占了分量；中数和众数只看位置和次数，尾巴碰不到它们。所以偏态分布里「均值和众数谁大」，答案取决于尾巴在哪边。

12. Exam Lab

Quantitative data questions are among the most frequent on Paper 1: a calculation, a choice of statistic to justify, and data interpretation. The four questions below come from one exam-style scenario, worked from the first calculation to the comparison of statistics.

Exam practice — Rong-jun's obedience study

Rong-jun was investigating the obedience of students at a school. She wanted to find out if obedience levels changed depending on the status of the authority figure. She sampled a total of 300 students across the whole school.

Q1 (1 mark)

She found that 92 per cent of the students sampled said they would obey a senior teacher even if they disagreed with the senior teacher's command. Calculate the number of students who said they would obey the senior teacher.

0.92 × 300 = 276 students. Show the working line, not just the answer: percentage × total = number. One mark for the correct figure with visible workings.

Q2 (1 mark)

A total of 30 students in Rong-jun's sample said that they would follow the instructions of another student (a peer) even if they disagreed with them. The other students said that they would not. Calculate the ratio of students who would follow the instructions of another student to those who would not.

30 : 270, which simplifies to 1 : 9. First find both parts: 30 followers and 300 − 30 = 270 non-followers. Then simplify the part-to-part ratio by dividing both sides by 30.

Q3 (2 marks)

Rong-jun asked the students to rate their level of obedience on a scale of 1 (very disobedient) to 10 (very obedient). She calculated the mean obedience rating. Explain one reason why Rong-jun calculated the mean rating rather than the mode score.

The mean is the most sensitive measure of central tendency because every score in the data set contributes to the calculation, whereas the mode only counts the most frequent score. One mark for a valid reason to prefer the mean (uses all scores / most sensitive), one mark for the linked weakness of the mode (ignores most of the data, can be unrepresentative, especially with a small data set or several modes).

Q4 (2 marks)

Explain why Rong-jun calculated the standard deviation of the obedience ratings rather than using the range.

The range uses only the two extreme scores, so a single outlier would distort it, and it says nothing about how the remaining scores are arranged. The standard deviation uses every score's deviation from the mean, so it represents the spread of the whole data set. One mark for a limitation of the range, one mark for the advantage of the standard deviation.

Three rules that earn marks on data questions:
  • Show your workings. A calculation answer needs the working line and the final figure, not the figure alone.
  • Quote the data. When asked to describe trends or draw conclusions, refer to the actual values in the table or graph.
  • Draw graphs properly. Plot values carefully, label both axes, and give the graph a title.
