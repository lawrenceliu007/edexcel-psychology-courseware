The whole study in one sentence 一句话总结
Burger partially replicated Milgram with modern ethics: rigorous two-stage screening, a hard stop at 150 volts, and a visible refusal condition. Obedience held (70% baseline vs Milgram's 82.5%), watching dissent changed nothing (63.3%), so the power of the situation survived 45 years, while whether participants would have obeyed further remains only an assumption.

1 · Why replicate Milgram?1.3.2 BURGER (2009)
Fill the gap 填空

In 2009, decided to replicate Milgram's () study in contemporary society. He too was interested in whether people would obey an figure.

Some psychologists believe people today are more of the consequences of unthinkingly following orders, so they would their actions when asked to cause potential injury.

Burger believed that despite the large gap, his study would still produce similar levels of . Society's culture and values had changed, but he felt this would not have a effect on obedience.

The second constraint 伦理约束

Milgram's study had been subject to criticism, so Burger adapted his research to adhere more closely to current ethical guidelines and cause participant distress.

16 · Evaluation: validity trio1.3.2 BURGER (2009)
Population validity 总体效度
Pushes it upPulls it down
Males and females; different levels; varied ; ages 20–81 participants were not selected after screening; obedience levels may only apply to people without issues such as anxiety or depression
Reliability and objectivity 信度与客观性

Standardised: all participants read the instructions and heard the verbal prods, so the research can be retested . Objectivity: the 150-volt measure is open to interpretation by other researchers.

Ecological validity 生态效度

Conducted in a at Santa Clara University. Shocking a stranger over word pairs is unlike real-life obedience (complying with a teacher or parent), so the task lacks . The 70% may not be found in an ordinary setting.

中文理解 一页三考点
population validity：广（人群多样）对窄（筛掉 47 个脆弱的人）。
reliability + objectivity：标准化 + 150 伏开关。
ecological validity：实验室任务缺 mundane realism。三个名字别写混。
