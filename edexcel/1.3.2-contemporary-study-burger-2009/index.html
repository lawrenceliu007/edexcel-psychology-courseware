1.3.2 CONTEMPORARY STUDY · BURGER (2009)
Replicating Milgram: Would people still obey today?

One contemporary study for Topic A: forty-five years after Milgram, Jerry Burger rebuilt the obedience experiment with modern ethical brakes, and stopped it at 150 volts.
Topic A · Social psychology Studies: contemporary Spec 1.3.2

What this study is really asking

  1. Society has changed since 1963. People know more about blindly following orders. Would obedience rates still hold up? Burger thought they would.
  2. He could not run the full experiment ethically, so participants were stopped at the 150-volt point: the question became "do you try to continue past 150 volts?", and 70% did.
  3. Watching someone else refuse changed nothing. In the modelled refusal condition, 63.3% still went to continue.
  4. Conclusion: obedience levels were similar to Milgram's experiment 5 (82.5%). Time, culture change and a visible dissenter did not shift them; the power of the situation dominated.

1. Getting Started

Put yourself in a psychology lab in 1963. A man in a grey lab coat tells you to keep delivering electric shocks to a stranger. You know the shocks hurt. He insists. What would you do?

Almost everyone who reads that question answers the same way: I would have stopped. Almost nobody actually did stop.

🎯 Have a guess first No notes

Now fast-forward to 2009. People have watched films about the study, schools teach it, the phrase "blind obedience" is common. If the experiment ran again with modern safeguards, would obedience rates drop?

Burger bet on option B, and the data went his way. How he tested that bet under modern ethics is the story of this study.

中文理解：这一讲的关键词
三个词撑起整讲：replication（复制研究），把 Milgram 的实验在 2009 年重跑一遍；150 volts（150 伏特），新实验的终点线，谁想继续就算服从；screening（筛查），Burger 在实验前把心理脆弱的人一层层筛掉，这是现代伦理要求逼出来的设计。
考试最爱问的数字：70%（基线条件想继续的比例）对比 Milgram 的 82.5%。

2. Why Replicate Milgram?

In 2009, Jerry Burger decided to replicate Milgram's (1963) study in contemporary society. He too was interested in whether people would obey an authoritative figure. Two beliefs pulled the decision:

The case for change（该改的理由）

Some psychologists today believe people are more aware of the consequences of unthinkingly following orders from an authoritative figure, and as a result would reconsider their actions when asked to cause potential injury to others.

Burger's prediction（Burger 的预测）

Despite the large time gap since Milgram's study, Burger believed his study would still produce similar levels of obedience. Society's culture and values had changed, but he felt this would not have a significant effect on obedience.

There was a second constraint. Milgram's obedience study had been subject to ethical criticism, so Burger needed to adapt his research to adhere more closely to current ethical guidelines and cause minimal participant distress. Those adaptations shape every part of the design that follows.

中文理解：为什么要重做
两个动力拉着他做：一派人说今天的人更有主见，不会再盲从命令；Burger 自己预测服从水平不会变，因为文化变了、服从的机制没变。再加上旧研究的伦理批评逼着他改设计，这三件事拼起来就是这页的全部逻辑。

3. Aim

Aim（目的）： to investigate obedience by partially replicating Milgram's (1963) study, to examine whether obedience to an authoritative figure would still be present today.

Two words carry marks in that aim. Partially: the study stops at 150 volts, so it tests the first stage of obedience rather than the full run. Today: the whole point of a contemporary study is to check whether an old finding survives in a new world.

4. First Screening

Participants responded to adverts and flyers in a local newspaper and local establishments (library, businesses) as well as online. The adverts promised US$50 for taking part in two 45-minute sessions. Individuals expressed interest by phone or email, and then received a call from a research assistant who began a screening procedure.

Question askedWhy it was asked
Have you been to college? If yes, did you take psychology lessons?Anyone who had taken two or more psychology classes was excluded, to rule out people familiar with Milgram's original study.
How is your physical and psychological health?To spot obvious risks of taking part.
Have you suffered any traumatic childhood experiences?Distress from the procedure could reopen old wounds, so these participants were screened out.

A further 30 per cent of participants were excluded from further involvement after this health and history screening. This first process eliminated anyone with knowledge of Milgram's study, or with obvious issues about taking part.

5. Second Screening

Survivors of the first screening moved on to a second stage, led by two clinical psychologists and held at Santa Clara University campus. It had two parts: a set of questionnaires, then a structured interview.

First, the questionnaires (Table 6.1 in the textbook):

QuestionnaireItemsWhat it measures
Interpersonal Reactivity Index28Empathy levels
Beck Anxiety Inventory21Own anxiety levels, rated against 21 anxiety symptoms
Desirability of Control Scale20Impressions of self-control over one's own life
Beck Depression Inventory21Severity of depression
Plus a demographic sheet: age, occupation, education and ethnicity

Second, the interview. Participants were interviewed by a clinical psychologist to assess who might be negatively affected by the study. The interview was structured around the Mini International Neuropsychiatric Interview (MINI) procedure, which touches on psychological disorders and let the psychologist identify anyone who should not take part. Each interview lasted approximately 30 minutes.

Figure 1 — The screening funnel. Burger removed anyone who knew the original study or might be hurt by it, before a single shock button was touched.
中文理解：两轮筛查各管一件事
第一轮管知道内情的人：学过两门以上心理学课的可能认出这是 Milgram 的实验，实验就没法"骗"他们了。
第二轮管会被伤害的人：量表加 MINI 访谈，把焦虑、抑郁、童年创伤重的人请出场，钱照付。47 人被请出，占 38.2%。这份谨慎是 Milgram 当年没有的，也是评价题里"伦理改进"的得分点。

6. The Final Sample

A total of 76 individuals were invited back a week later, but 6 dropped out at this point, 5 of whom expressed awareness of Milgram's research during the second session. That left a final sample of:

FeatureDetail
Size70 participants (29 males, 41 females)
AgeRanged from 20 to 81 years, with a mean of 42.9 years
RecruitmentAdverts and flyers in a local newspaper and local establishments (library, businesses), plus online
PaymentUS$50, kept even if the participant withdrew

Compare that with Milgram's sample of men aged 20 to 50. The age span alone makes Burger's sample a different animal, which matters later in the evaluation.

7. Baseline Condition · Setting Up

Participants were randomly split into two groups, with an attempt to keep equal ratios of gender in each. Forty participants were randomly assigned to the baseline condition and 30 to the modelled refusal condition.

In the baseline condition:

StepWhat happened
The castThe participant met the experimenter and the confederate. Both were selected for their resemblance to Milgram's original experimenter and learner.
The moneyParticipant and confederate were both given US$50 and told to keep it even if they withdrew.
The scriptBurger used a similar script to Milgram's, explaining the procedure and the electric generator.
The rigged drawThe role choice was rigged, as in Milgram's study: the confederate always became the learner, the participant the teacher. Both signed consent forms.
The electrodeThe learner was taken to the adjoining room. The teacher watched the experimenter strap an electrode to the learner's left wrist, seal it with paste, and explain it would provide good contact and prevent blisters or burns.
The taskThe teacher read out a list of 25 word pairs, delivering a stronger shock for each wrong answer.
The detailAt this point the confederate revealed a heart problem detected at a hospital visit, exactly as in Milgram's study.

8. Baseline Condition · The Shocks

From here the trial follows Milgram closely, with one hard stop built in:

  • The learner was shown how to use the generator and given a 15-volt sample shock.
  • The same four verbal prods from Milgram's study were used.
  • Pre-recorded voice feedback: grunts from the learner became audible at the 75-volt level.
  • At 150 volts, a standard recording of the learner's voice demanded to be released and complained of heart issues.
  • After the experimenter used the verbal prods, if the teacher was still resistant, the experiment ended there. It was also forcibly ended at the point where the teacher read out the next instruction following the 150-volt shock.
  • On finishing, the experimenter immediately admitted the shocks were not real and introduced the teacher to the learner, to show he was perfectly fine.
Figure 2 — The trial timeline. Everything is arranged around a single decision: continue past 150 volts or refuse.
中文理解：为什么偏偏选 150 伏
Milgram 的数据里，150 伏附近是分水岭：能熬过这一关继续按的人，后面几乎一路到底。Burger 把实验停在 150 伏，等于只测"会不会迈过这道坎"，迈过去就算服从。
这个设计能成立，靠的是 Milgram 数据里那句话：150 伏是 point of no return（不归点）。评价题里会再问你：这个假设稳不稳。

9. Modelled Refusal Condition

In this variation, all participants followed the same procedure as the baseline condition, with a few exceptions:

  • Two confederates were used instead of one. The second posed as a participant and was the same gender as the real participant.
  • The drawing of roles was rigged again: a learner (confederate), Teacher 1 (a confederate) and Teacher 2, the real participant.
  • Teacher 1 took the lead, asking the questions and administering shocks, while Teacher 2 sat alongside.
  • At 75 volts, Teacher 1 hesitated after hearing the learner grunt, as scripted.
  • At 90 volts, Teacher 1 stated "I don't know about this".
  • The experimenter prompted Teacher 1, but he/she refused to carry on and pushed the chair back from the table.
  • The experimenter then asked Teacher 2, the real participant, to continue.

What this condition tests

Milgram had found that a dissenting peer sharply cut obedience. Burger wanted to know whether that effect still holds today. The visible refusal is a model of dissent, hence the name modelled refusal: the participant sees disobedience modelled in front of them, then has to choose.

10. The Safety Rail (Ethical Adaptations)

Burger acknowledged the ethical concerns associated with Milgram's original experiments, and the textbook lists what he did about them:

AdaptationDetail
Rigorous screeningParticipants deemed unsuitable were not selected (see sections 4 and 5).
Right to withdraw, three timesParticipants were informed three times before the experiment that they could leave without consequence.
A gentler sample shock15 volts rather than the 45 volts given in Milgram's study.
A clinical psychologist as experimenterInstructed to stop the experiment if excessive stress was detected during the procedure.
Immediate debriefParticipants were debriefed immediately afterwards to alleviate any distress or anxiety caused.
The hard stop at 150 voltsNobody ever went further, so nobody heard the learner at his most distressed.

11. Results · Baseline Condition

In the baseline condition, 70 per cent of participants had to be stopped before attempting to continue past 150 volts. That rate is just lower than Milgram's 82.5 per cent, found in his variation experiment 5.

Figure 3 — Table 6.2, condensed. The 2009 baseline sits just under Milgram's experiment 5; the visible refusal barely dents it.
中文理解：这两个百分比怎么用
70% 对 82.5%，差了 12.5 个百分点，但注意两个实验停的时点不同：Milgram 让人一路走到 450 伏，Burger 只走到 150 伏。想继续过 150 伏的人未必都会走到底，所以这 70% 只能算一个下限估计。
考试写法：报告数字，再补一句"the results were very similar to Milgram's"，评价时再提"assumption"的坑（第 16 节）。

12. Results · Modelled Refusal

In the modelled refusal condition, 63.3 per cent went to continue the procedure after 150 volts, despite the expectations held by Burger and by the confederate who withdrew. The results were very similar to the baseline condition.

13. Gender and Personality

  • Gender: little difference in obedience levels in either condition. In the baseline condition, 66.7% of men and 72.7% of women pressed the 150-volt switch. The point at which men and women needed the first prod was also similar.
  • Empathy and control: Burger compared the results with the screening tests relating to empathy and control, and found little difference between those who stopped and those who continued.
  • One personality echo: those who showed reluctance to give the shocks early in the procedure scored higher on desirability for control in the baseline condition. No difference was found when comparing the modelled refusal and baseline conditions on personality scores overall.

Read the pattern, not the single number

Personality traits correlated with early hesitation but not with final obedience. People who disliked being controlled felt the discomfort sooner, yet they still went to continue at the same rate. That ordering, discomfort first and obedience anyway, is exactly what "the power of the situation" predicts.

14. Conclusion

The textbook gives four conclusion points. Learn them as pairs:

  1. Results found in both conditions are similar to Milgram's research over 45 years ago. Time and changes in society's culture did not have an effect on obedience levels, nor did the refusal of the confederate.
  2. Witnessing another person refuse to continue had no effect on obedience levels, so it seems that the power of the situation had a greater effect.
  3. No difference was found between genders, nor did age, education level or ethnicity have an effect on the obedience levels found.
  4. There was some impact of personality: those with high empathetic concern expressed reluctance to continue early in the procedure. However, this did not ultimately affect their obedience.
中文理解：结论一句话版
四十五年过去，社会变了、人变了，可是同样的情境还是逼出同样比例的服从。看见别人拒绝也没用，性别年龄学历族裔统统没用，性格只影响"多早开始难受"，不影响最后按不按。
这正是 1.1.3 讲过的 situational 解释对 dispositional 解释的一次正面胜利，答题时可以把两讲连起来。

15. Evaluation · Ethics: Fixed and Unfixed

What Burger fixed（修复的伦理）

Rigorous screening kept unsuitable participants out. Participants were reminded three times of their right to withdraw. The sample shock was 15 volts, not 45. The experimenter was a clinical psychologist, instructed to stop on signs of excessive stress. Debriefing was immediate.

What stayed worrying（残留的担忧）

Participants were still deliberately placed in a situation that would cause anxiety. The verbal prods used by the experimenter could have been understood to remove the previously established right to withdraw, even if only temporarily.

中文理解：伦理这题怎么答才不空
别只写"改进了伦理"。答题结构是：列改进（筛查 / 三次告知退出权 / 15 伏 / 临床心理学家主试 / 即刻汇报）→ 转折（情境本身仍制造焦虑，prods 一出口，退出权等于被临时没收）。有改进有残留，才是完整的伦理评价。

16. Evaluation · The Partial Replication

Burger only partially replicated Milgram's study: participants did not go beyond the 150-volt level, due to the ethical concern that they would experience greater distress beyond this point, as observed in Milgram's study.

The ethical strength（伦理上的加分）

Stopping at 150 volts protected every participant from the most distressed moments of the procedure.

The scientific cost（科学上的代价）

It can only be assumed that participants would have continued to obey after 150 volts. That assumption rests on Milgram's finding that the majority continued once past this "point of no return". We cannot be certain that Burger's participants would have continued.

This trade, ethics bought at the price of certainty, is the single most examinable idea in the whole study. （伦理上加分，科学上吃亏：150 伏就停，被试之后还会不会继续服从只能靠推断，这层推断的依据是 Milgram 的「不归点」发现。）

中文理解：这笔交易怎么写进答案
先写收益（保护被试不再受苦），再写代价（150 伏之后的服从水平只能假设，不能确定），最后点一句「假设的依据是 Milgram 的实验 5」。三句话，就是这道评价题的满分骨架。

17. Evaluation · Population Validity

What pushes generalisability up（推高推广性）

Burger tested males and females, participants of different education levels and varied ethnicity, aged 20 to 81. The study therefore had population validity, so the conclusions about obedience to authority can be generalised to the US population.

What pulls it back down（拉低推广性）

47 participants were not selected following the screening process, because they would have found the procedure too distressing. This might indicate that the obedience levels found would only apply to people without psychological issues such as anxiety or depression, which may limit the generalisability of the findings.

中文理解：一把尺子的两端
往上推：男女都有、年龄跨度 20 到 81、教育族裔多样，比 Milgram 那批 20 到 50 岁的男性广得多。
往下拉：最脆弱的 47 人被筛掉了，等于实验结论只对"心理没负担的人"负责。两句话一起写，population validity 这题就满了。

18. Evaluation · Reliability, Objectivity, Realism

Reliability · standardised（信度·标准化）

All participants were read the same instructions and given the same verbal prods. The research can be retested exactly, to check whether the 70% obedience level would be consistent. Burger found a similar obedience level to Milgram's experiment 5, which suggests obedience is consistently observed among participants in the same situation.

Objectivity · the 150-volt switch（客观性·150伏开关）

Burger used the point at which participants broke off or continued, at 150 volts, as the measure of obedience. This measure could be regarded as objective because it is not open to interpretation by other researchers.

Ecological validity · mundane realism（生态效度·现实感）

The study was conducted in a laboratory at Santa Clara University, an artificial setting that does not represent real-life obedience at home, in school or in the workplace. The 70% obedience level may not be found in an ordinary setting. Laboratory research demanding obedience to shock another person on a word-pair task is unlike real-life obedience, which can be a very ordinary experience, such as complying with the request of a teacher or parent. The task therefore lacks mundane realism.

中文理解：三顶帽子各记一句话
信度：同样的话术、同样的催促，谁来做结果都一样，Burger 的 70% 和 Milgram 实验 5 的 82.5% 接近，说明服从水平稳定。
客观性：以「150 伏处停不停」计数，数字摆在那里，换谁来评都一样。
生态效度：大学实验室里对陌生人按电击键，和现实里听老师家长的话差得远，结论往外推要留一手。

21. Exam Lab

The question below comes from the textbook's exam practice for this chapter.

中文点题：两个环节各说一步：电话初筛（剔心理学生和健康风险），二筛（四份问卷 + 临床心理学家 30 分钟 MINI 访谈）。说清「做了什么、剔掉了谁」各得一分。

Q1 — Describe how Burger (2009) screened the participants before they took part in his study. (2 marks)

One mark per distinct step; name the step and what it removed.

Firstly, a research assistant phoned interested respondents and screened out anyone who had taken two or more psychology classes (who might have known Milgram's original study), then asked about physical and psychological health and any traumatic childhood experiences, excluding a further 30 per cent. Secondly, at Santa Clara University two clinical psychologists led a second screening: participants completed the Interpersonal Reactivity Index, Beck Anxiety Inventory, Desirability of Control Scale and Beck Depression Inventory, plus a demographic sheet, and were interviewed for about 30 minutes using the MINI procedure, after which 47 of 123 were removed.

中文点题：背一个数字就够：基线条件 70% 愿意继续过 150 伏（Milgram 实验 5 是 82.5%），或者模型拒绝条件 63.3%。

Q2 — Describe one finding of Burger's (2009) study into obedience. (from the chapter checkpoint)

In the baseline condition, 70 per cent of participants had to be stopped before attempting to continue past 150 volts. This rate was just lower than the 82.5 per cent found in Milgram's variation experiment 5, suggesting obedience levels had changed little across the 45 years between the studies.

中文点题：从三个方向比：过程（同话术同催促、150 伏就停）、伦理（筛查、三次告知退出、15 伏示例、临床心理学家）、结果结论（70% 对 82.5%，结论一致）。

Q3 — Compare Burger's study with Milgram's original. (from the textbook's Activity 2)

Procedure: both used a rigged role draw, the same script style, the same four prods and word-pair task; Burger added a hard stop at 150 volts and a modelled refusal condition. Ethics: both caused distress and used deception, but Burger screened out vulnerable participants, reminded them three times of the right to withdraw, gave a 15-volt sample shock instead of 45, employed a clinical psychologist as experimenter and debriefed immediately. Results/conclusions: Milgram's experiment 5 reached 82.5% continuation at the equivalent stage; Burger found 70%, and both concluded that the power of the situation produces high obedience in ordinary people.

22. Key Terms

partial replication（部分重复验证）
Repeating part of a study; Burger stopped at 150 volts, so obedience beyond that point remains an assumption.
150-volt criterion（150伏特标准）
The measure of obedience: whether the participant went to continue after the learner demanded release at 150 volts.
baseline condition（基线条件）
The direct copy of Milgram's procedure; 40 participants, 70% went to continue.
modelled refusal condition（示范拒绝条件）
30 participants watched Teacher 1 refuse at 90 volts; 63.3% still went to continue.
screening（筛查）
Two rounds of checks (phone and clinical) removing psychology students, health risks and anyone likely to be distressed.
MINI（迷你国际神经精神访谈）
Mini International Neuropsychiatric Interview; a structured interview touching on psychological disorders, run for about 30 minutes.
Interpersonal Reactivity Index（人际反应指数（测共情））
28-item scale measuring empathy levels.
Desirability of Control Scale（控制欲量表）
20-item scale measuring impressions of self-control over one's own life.
verbal prods（口头催促语）
The same four spoken prompts Milgram used to push participants onward; they could have been heard as cancelling the right to withdraw.
mundane realism（世俗现实性）
Whether a task resembles everyday life; shocking a stranger over word pairs does not resemble ordinary obedience, so it lacks mundane realism.
point of no return（不归点）
Milgram's finding that most participants who passed 150 volts continued to the end; the basis of Burger's assumption.
power of the situation（情境的力量）
The conclusion that situational forces dominated: witnessing refusal, and all personal characteristics measured, failed to change obedience levels.
