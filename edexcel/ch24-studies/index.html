1. Learning Objectives（学习目标）

⚡ Quick Summary — 本节要学什么

This chapter is different from Chapters 22 and 23. Those chapters taught you theories (what causes depression / anorexia). This chapter teaches you studies — real research that psychologists actually carried out. You will meet one classic study and five contemporary studies, each using a different research method.

🎯 By the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  • Describe and evaluate one classic study: Rosenhan (1973) 'On being sane in insane places'
  • Describe and evaluate one contemporary study relating to schizophrenia: Suzuki et al. (2014) 'High prevalence of underweight and undernutrition in Japanese inpatients with schizophrenia'
  • Describe and evaluate a choice of one of the two optional contemporary studies for your chosen 'other' disorder:
    • Unipolar depression: Hans and Hiller (2013) 'Effectiveness of and drop out from outpatient cognitive-behavioural therapy for adult unipolar depression: A meta-analysis of nonrandomised effectiveness studies' — OR — Ma, Quan and Liu (2014) 'Mediating effect of social support on the relationship between self-evaluation and depression'
    • Anorexia nervosa: Becker et al. (2002) 'Eating behaviours and attitudes following prolonged exposure to television among ethnic Fijian adolescent girls' — OR — Reichel et al. (2014) '"Glass fairies" and "bone children"...'
中文理解：这一章和前面的章节不一样。前面学的是"为什么会得病"（理论），这一章学的是"心理学家做过哪些真实研究"（研究）。按照考试范围，精神分裂症部分你需要准备 Suzuki et al. (2014)；抑郁部分需要从 Hans & Hiller 和 Ma 中选一个来准备；厌食部分需要从 Becker 和 Reichel 中选一个来准备。把所选 disorder 的两个研究都了解当然有帮助，但考试只要求掌握其中一个。

2. Getting Started — A Lived Experience（真实经历）

🗣️ Dr Eleanor Longden — hearing voices Lived Experience

Dr Eleanor Longden began hearing a voice when she was a university student. The voice said things like "She is leaving the room" and "She is going to the library". Initially, she found the voice strange but reassuring. Over time the number of voices increased and they became malicious and aggressive. Dr Longden was eventually diagnosed with schizophrenia and admitted to hospital on multiple occasions.

In 2013, she presented a candid TED talk about her experiences. She now works at a schizophrenia research centre (the Psychosis Research Unit) in Manchester, in northern England. This centre employs many researchers with lived experience of schizophrenia as part of their sustained effort to conduct research which helps reduce distress and restore autonomy and dignity for people who hear voices.

Key Term — lived experience（亲历经验）
Unique insight gained through first-hand / direct experience of a certain set of personal circumstances. 可以理解为"亲历者视角"——研究者自己也有过相同的病症或经历，因此对患者的世界有外人难以体会的理解。
中文理解：Eleanor Longden 是一位大学生，突然开始听到脑子里有个声音在"解说"她的行为。一开始这个声音是无害的，但随着诊断和精神科经历，声音变得充满敌意。后来她成为一名心理学家，用自己的亲历经验帮助研究。

她的故事说明：诊断标签和医疗环境本身可能影响一个人对症状的体验——这正是 Rosenhan 研究要探讨的核心问题之一。
Exam tip: The "Getting Started" activity asks you to think about how society reacts to a diagnosis of schizophrenia. This connects directly to Rosenhan's study — what happens when a normal person is labelled as mentally ill?

3. Classic Study: Rosenhan (1973) ⭐

On Being Sane in Insane Places（在疯狂之地保持理智）

⚡ Quick Summary — 30 秒看懂这个研究

  1. 8 个正常人（3 女 5 男，包括 Rosenhan 自己）假装听到声音，到 12 家精神病医院预约就诊
  2. 他们全部被收治入院，并大多被诊断为精神分裂症
  3. 入院后他们表现完全正常，但平均住了 19 天才被放出
  4. 出院诊断是"精神分裂症缓解期"——没有一个人被认为是"从来没病"
  5. 结论：精神病学诊断不可靠，医院无法区分正常人和病人；"病人"标签会扭曲工作人员看到的一切
Figure: Textbook page introducing Rosenhan's classic study "On Being Sane in Insane Places"
AO1 — Knowledge & Understanding Describe the study

Background and Aim（背景与目的）

David Rosenhan (1973) was inspired by the antipsychiatry movement（反精神病学运动） led by Scottish psychiatrist R.D. Laing. This was a group of people in America who questioned the authority of psychiatric institutions. They campaigned for change and argued that the current medical model stigmatised already vulnerable people. They demanded a more humane approach and rejected the medical model（医学模型）. Instead they believed that practitioners should focus less on individual factors as the source of symptoms and more on the social context in which those symptoms arise. Psychiatric labels such as 'schizophrenia' were not seen as illnesses with biological causes but as 'social constructs' or labels created by the more powerful people in society to control the behaviour of those who did not fit in with social and cultural norms.

Rosenhan believed that psychiatrists have a tendency to perceive many behaviours as indicators of underlying disorders rather than typical reactions to unusually difficult circumstances. To this end, he aimed to show that psychiatrists are unable to tell the difference between people with genuine psychological symptoms and those who say they have these symptoms but do not. He also aimed to investigate interactions between staff and patients.

Key Term — Antipsychiatry movement（反精神病学运动）
A group of intellectuals who rejected biological labels, explanations and treatments for mental disorders; the group began challenging the power dynamic between psychiatrists and patients; these ideas were popularised from the 1960s onwards. 他们反对把精神痛苦简单归为"脑部疾病"，认为诊断标签很多时候是社会用来控制"不合群者"的工具。
Key Term — Medical model（医学模型）
The biological approach to understanding and treating abnormal behaviour through identifying symptoms and making specific diagnoses, which lead to biological treatments such as drugs or surgery. 把心理问题当成身体疾病来诊断和用药的模型。

Method and Sample（研究方法与样本）

Key Term — Covert participant observation（隐蔽参与观察）
Covert（隐蔽的） = the researchers hid their true identity. Participant observation（参与观察） = the researchers joined the group they were studying, experiencing hospital life as "patients". 就像记者假扮成工人混进工厂，亲身体验并偷偷记录，工厂里没人知道他是记者。

This study was a covert participant observation in a naturalistic setting. The sample included duty psychiatrists, nurses and other staff at 11 state-run public hospitals and one private hospital. The hospitals were located in five states on the east and west coasts of the United States. Some were old and shabby, while others were newer. Some were understaffed and some had good staff-to-patient ratios.

Eight pseudopatients（假病人） (three women and five men, one of whom was Rosenhan) took part. They were a psychologist, a psychiatrist, a paediatrician, a housewife, a painter and a graduate student.

Key Term — Pseudopatients（假病人）
Researchers who pretend to be patients as part of a research study.

Procedure（研究程序）

  • Each pseudopatient telephoned one of the hospitals for an appointment. When they arrived they told the staff they were hearing a voice saying "empty", "hollow" and "thud"（"空虚""空洞""咚"声）. The voice was described as unclear, unfamiliar and the same gender as the patient.
  • These words were chosen to reflect potential concerns in the pseudopatients about the meaninglessness of their existence. Research in this area contained no mention of existential crisis as a cause of psychosis which should have made the doctors more cautious about their diagnosis.
  • Following admission, all pseudopatients were friendly and cooperative. They behaved normally and answered all questions truthfully, e.g. if asked they said they were no longer hearing voices.
  • Every single one was admitted to hospital. They were diagnosed with schizophrenia（精神分裂症）.
  • Once inside, the pseudopatients stopped reporting any symptoms and behaved completely normally.
  • They collected data in the form of handwritten notes about their observations of hospital life and the behaviour of other patients and staff. Initially this was done covertly but as none of the staff seemed interested, the pseudopatients began making notes more publicly.
  • They had agreed to get out by cooperating, taking medication (pocketed, not swallowed), and saying they felt fine.

Results（结果）

FindingDetail
Length of stay（住院时长）7 to 52 days; average 19 days
Release diagnosis（出院诊断）"Schizophrenia in remission"（精神分裂症缓解期）— nobody was recorded as sane
Who saw through them（谁识破了他们）Not the staff — but some real patients suspected them, making remarks like "you're not crazy, you're a journalist or a professor"
Staff contact（医患互动）Staff spent an average of only 6.8 minutes a day with patients. Staff avoided patients; psychiatrists came onto wards only rarely; nurses stayed in glassed-off offices
Normal behaviour misread（正常行为被误读）Queuing early for lunch = "oral-acquisitive nature of the syndrome"; writing notes = pathological "writing behaviour"; pacing the corridors out of boredom = symptom
中文理解（最重要的结论）：一旦贴上"精神分裂症"标签，你做的一切都会被解读成病态。等待吃饭排队是病，写笔记是病，走来走去也是病。医护人员每天只和病人相处平均 6.8 分钟，很难真正了解病人。

标签就像一副有色眼镜——一旦被贴上，医生看你的一切行为都自动带上了"病"的颜色。

Conclusions（结论）

  • Psychiatric hospitals cannot distinguish the sane from the insane in this setting.
  • Diagnosis is unreliable（不可靠） and sticky — once labelled, the label "sticks" and every behaviour is interpreted through it.
  • Hospitals are experienced as depowering（剥夺权力感的） environments — patients lose dignity, privacy and any sense of control.
  • An approach that increases contact time between staff and patients, and moves away from diagnostic labels towards helping people to solve problems causing their distress, may be more beneficial.
▶ Exam Key Points — Rosenhan (1973) AO1 + AO3
  1. Method: covert participant observation — 8 pseudopatients, 12 hospitals, 5 US states. Quote "empty, hollow, thud" for the voices.
  2. All admitted and diagnosed with schizophrenia; average stay 19 days; released as "schizophrenia in remission".
  3. Normal behaviour was reinterpreted as pathological; real patients, not staff, saw through the disguise; staff spent only 6.8 minutes per day with patients.
  4. Conclusions: diagnosis unreliable; labels are "sticky"; hospitals are depowering environments.
  5. AO3 angles: ethics (deception of staff, pseudopatients' own distress), no informed consent from real patients, but massive real-world impact on psychiatric practice.

4. Evaluating Rosenhan — Critics Respond（批评者的回应）

⚡ Quick Summary — 三个批评者 + 一个毛利视角

  1. Spitzer：假病人说"听到声音"本身就是症状，医生没有做错
  2. Lando：住院期间可能真的有精神问题，医院让他们缓解期出院反而说明医院"起作用了"
  3. Slater：2005 年重复实验发现医生没那么好骗，开了门诊转诊
  4. te whare tapa whā：毛利文化认为健康需要四个支柱平衡，诊断必须考虑文化背景
AO3 — Evaluation, Analysis & Application Criticise and debate

Strength: Diversity of Hospitals

A strength of Rosenhan's study is the diversity of different hospitals used, from old to new, private to public, and east to west coast location. This is important as it means that the findings regarding the validity of diagnosis can be generalised with greater confidence and that negative treatment of patients was widespread and not restricted to one hospital or state.

That said, the generalisability is limited by the fact that the pseudopatients only presented with hearing voices, and it is possible that psychiatrists may have been more hesitant to admit people with other types of mental disorder. Also, the study was conducted in the United States in the 1970s, suggesting that the findings may be ethnocentric.

Strength: Standardised Procedure

A further strength is that Rosenhan's team of researchers (pseudopatients) followed a standardised procedure up to the point at which they were admitted. They were told exactly what they should say and how to answer questions about the nature of the voices that they were hearing. This meant that every psychiatrist was faced with the exact same information upon which to make their diagnosis, strengthening the validity of the findings.

The procedure was also replicable as demonstrated by journalist Lauren Slater (2005), who presented at nine psychiatric emergency wards reporting the exact same issue. She says she was consistently diagnosed with psychotic depression and prescribed a variety of medications. This replication suggests that Rosenhan's claims had temporal validity and were not era-bound, despite changes made to the DSM since version II, which was used in the original study.

Critic 1: Robert Spitzer（斯皮策，2005）

Spitzer was responsible for the development of DSM-III. He argued the pseudopatients did have a symptom when they arrived — they claimed to hear voices. Hearing voices is a genuine symptom of mental disorder, so doctors were right to admit them. The failure would only exist if the hospitals failed to detect sane behaviour after admission — but since the pseudopatients arrived with a real complaint, the diagnosis was not actually wrong.

Spitzer also noted that doctors are trained to believe patients' self-reports. A doctor who ignores a patient claiming to hear voices could face tragic consequences. The "fraud" was on the pseudopatients' side, not the hospitals'.

He sent a detailed summary of Slater's findings to 431 psychiatrists with a short questionnaire. Of 74 responses, only 6 per cent said they would have diagnosed psychotic depression; 82 per cent would not have admitted her to the hospital, suggesting outpatient care（门诊治疗） as an alternative. Only a third said they would prescribe medication. This suggests that the claim that diagnostic labelling is driven by the desire to prescribe is unfounded and challenges the reliability of Slater's claims, since the psychiatrists' self-reports are inconsistent with Slater's observations. Spitzer (2005) claims both the work of Rosenhan and Slater is flawed and sensationalist, and causes harm through undermining public confidence in psychiatry.

Key Term — Outpatient care（门诊治疗）
Hospital treatment and/or support that does not require the patient to be admitted (stay overnight).

Weakness: Harry Lando — the ninth pseudopatient（第九个假病人）

The textbook (p.317) treats Lando as a weakness: there is evidence that Rosenhan failed to report all of the data he collected. A ninth pseudopatient, Harry Lando, was also diagnosed with schizophrenia, but he described his 19-day admission as "extremely favourable" — a very different picture from the one Rosenhan painted.

  • While Rosenhan reported that staff spent an average of only 6.8 minutes a day with patients, Lando said patients and staff were engaged for at least an hour a day in activities such as group and drama therapy, watching television/playing cards.
  • Lando noted that patients were respected and listened to by caring and attentive staff.

This suggests that Rosenhan's data are highly subjective, and Lando's account seriously detracts from the credibility of Rosenhan's claims.

Critic 3: Lauren Slater（斯莱特，2005）

Slater repeated a version of the experiment for her book Opening Skinner's Box. She presented herself at psychiatric hospitals claiming to hear the word "thud". She was never admitted — doctors gave her prescriptions or referrals, and most diagnosed her with depression with psychotic features or "anxiety", not schizophrenia. Slater argued that modern psychiatry (with DSM-IV criteria) is more cautious than in Rosenhan's day.

中文理解：三位批评者从不同角度反驳 Rosenhan。Spitzer 说"你谎称有症状，医生收治你没错"；Lando 说"医院 19 天就把人治好放走，恰恰说明医院有效"；Slater 用 2005 年的重复实验说"现在的医生没那么好骗"。

Cultural perspective: te whare tapa whā（毛利"四壁之屋"健康观）

🏠 te whare tapa whā — the four-sided house Cultural Response

In Māori culture (New Zealand), health is like a house with four walls. If any one wall fails, the house falls:

  • taha hinengaro — mental/emotional wellbeing (the mind)
  • taha tinana — physical wellbeing (the body)
  • taha whānau — family and social wellbeing (relationships)
  • taha wairua — spiritual wellbeing (the spirit)

From this perspective, a diagnosis must consider all four dimensions. A diagnosis that looks only at symptoms (like hearing voices) ignores family, spirit and cultural context — and can mislabel culturally normal experiences as illness.

te whare tapa whā 字面意思就是"四壁之屋"：健康需要心理、身体、家庭、心灵四面墙齐全，缺一面墙房子就会塌。而西方诊断往往只检查"心理"这一面墙，就给整座房子下了结论。

🏆 Gold-standard paragraph (use this structure in essays)

A key criticism of Rosenhan's study is that it may be outdated. When Slater (2005) repeated the procedure, claiming to hear the word "thud", she was not admitted to any hospital — doctors offered prescriptions and outpatient referrals instead. This suggests that modern diagnostic systems (DSM-IV and later) are more careful than in 1973, and that Rosenhan's conclusion about the unreliability of diagnosis may not generalise to contemporary psychiatric practice. However, Slater's sample was just one person presenting once, which is far less systematic than Rosenhan's 8 pseudopatients across 12 hospitals, so her refutation is itself limited in reliability.

▶ Exam Key Points — Evaluating Rosenhan AO3
  1. Strength: diverse hospitals (old/new, public/private, east/west coast) → greater generalisability.
  2. Strength: standardised procedure → every psychiatrist received same information; replicable by Slater (2005).
  3. Spitzer: pseudopatients arrived with a genuine complaint (voices) — doctors were right to admit; believing self-reports protects genuinely ill patients.
  4. Lando: the hospitals worked — symptoms improved and patients were released within weeks ("in remission"); his account contradicts Rosenhan's negative picture.
  5. Slater (2005): repetition found doctors did NOT admit her — modern psychiatry may be more accurate; but tiny sample limits her claim.
  6. te whare tapa whā: diagnosis should cover mind, body, family and spirit — Western symptom-only checklists risk cultural bias.
  7. Ethics of Rosenhan: deception of staff, no consent, stress for pseudopatients and possibly real patients — weigh against its enormous influence on psychiatric reform.

5. Contemporary Study: Suzuki et al. (2014) ⭐

Underweight and Undernutrition in Japanese Inpatients with Schizophrenia（日本精神分裂症住院患者的体重不足与营养不良）

⚡ Quick Summary — 30 秒看懂这个研究

  1. 研究目的：调查日本精神分裂症住院患者中体重不足和营养不良的流行率
  2. 样本：333 位精神分裂症患者 + 191 位年龄性别匹配的健康对照组，来自日本新潟县 9 家医院
  3. 方法：横断面研究（cross-sectional），按 BMI 分组，测空腹血液营养指标
  4. 结果：患者组体重不足比例更高（14.1% vs 4.2%）；低蛋白血症、低胆固醇更常见
  5. 结论：日本精神分裂症住院患者体重不足问题突出，需监测蛋白质和血脂指标
AO1 — Knowledge & Understanding Describe the study

Background and Aim（背景与目的）

Prolonged use of antipsychotics is associated with significant weight gain, meaning people with schizophrenia are at greater risk of obesity and associated health problems such as high blood pressure. In fact, life expectancy is 15 years shorter in people with schizophrenia than the general population with 50–75 per cent dying of heart disease (Hennekens et al., 2005). However, this data only refers to people in North America and Europe where being overweight or obese is also more common in the general population than it is in East Asian countries such as Japan.

In contrast with the West, people with schizophrenia in Japan are more likely to be underweight than overweight but this too can lead to a variety of serious medical concerns. Research focusing on nutrition and health in psychiatric patients is scarce in Japan and differences may be linked to differing treatment plans. For example, hospital admissions are considerably longer in Japan compared with the West and 67 per cent of all inpatients remain in hospital for more than 12 months; 60 per cent of these people have schizophrenia or delusional disorder.

The aim was to investigate the prevalence of obesity or being underweight and collect other laboratory data from Japanese inpatients with schizophrenia.

Key Term — Cross-sectional study（横断面研究）
Data is collected from different groups of people at one point in time, then compared. 就像在同一个时间点给三个班级各拍一张"快照"对比成绩，而不是跟踪一个班级三年看它怎么变化。

Method and Sample（研究方法与样本）

  • Design: Cross-sectional study; participants were grouped by health status (patient with schizophrenia versus healthy control) and BMI (underweight, standard, overweight).
  • Participants: 333 inpatients with schizophrenia (diagnosed using DSM-IV-TR) aged 18–60 years were compared with 191 age- and sex-matched healthy volunteers.
  • Location: Patients were selected from nine hospitals in Niigata, on the west coast of Honshu, Japan.
  • Exclusions: All participants were free from physical illnesses and had not changed drug treatment in the last four weeks. Other than medication for schizophrenia, none were taking any drugs other than benzodiazepines (drugs used to treat anxiety disorders) and mood stabilisers.

Procedure（研究程序）

Body mass index (BMI) was calculated for every participant by dividing their weight (in kilograms) by their squared height (in metres). The BMIs were used to sort them into three groups:

  • Overweight/obese: BMI 25 or more
  • Standard weight: BMI 18.5 to less than 25
  • Underweight: less than 18.5

Blood samples were taken following an overnight fast, meaning participants had not eaten for at least nine hours. This was used to determine whether participants had:

  • Hypoproteinemia（低蛋白血症）: total protein (TP) levels below 6.7 g/dL
  • Hypoglycemia（低血糖）: fasting plasma glucose (FPG) levels below 70 mg/dL
  • Hypocholesterolemia（低胆固醇血症）: total cholesterol (TC) levels below 150 mg/dL
  • Hypotriglyceridemia（低甘油三酯血症）: total triglyceride (TG) levels below 50 mg/dL
中文理解：这个研究不只是"称体重、验血"那么简单。研究者把每个人按 BMI 分成三类（过轻/标准/超重），再检查四种血液营养指标。发现患者不仅更可能过轻，而且血液里蛋白质、胆固醇等指标也更低——这说明住院精神分裂症患者的营养不良问题需要被重视。

Results（结果）

There was a significant difference between people with and without schizophrenia in all three BMI categories (p < 0.001). Being underweight was more common in people with schizophrenia than healthy controls (p < 0.001) — see Table 24.1.

People with schizophrenia were significantly more likely to have hypoproteinemia (low levels of protein) and hypocholesterolemia (low levels of cholesterol). FPG was also significantly lower in the schizophrenia group than the control but there was no difference in hypoglycemia. Hypotriglyceridemia (low levels of a certain type of fat) was significantly more common in people with schizophrenia who were underweight than those who were standard weight or overweight.

Conclusions（结论）

  • Being underweight is significantly more common in Japanese inpatients with schizophrenia than the general population.
  • Issues associated with being underweight including low levels of protein and different types of fat and cholesterol should be monitored to avoid further health problems.
  • Conditions like hypocholesterolemia mean people are at high risk of cerebral haemorrhage（脑出血）.
  • Being underweight is also associated with low bone density, meaning inpatients with schizophrenia could be at higher risk of fractures and conditions such as osteoporosis.
▶ Exam Key Points — Suzuki et al. (2014) AO1
  1. Aim: investigate prevalence of underweight/obesity and collect laboratory data from Japanese inpatients with schizophrenia.
  2. Method: cross-sectional; 333 patients vs 191 age- and sex-matched controls from Niigata, Japan; grouped by BMI and tested blood nutrients after overnight fast.
  3. Table 24.1: underweight 14.1% (patients) vs 4.2% (controls); standard weight 59.2% vs 73.8%; overweight/obesity 26.7% vs 22.0%.
  4. Results: patients more likely to have hypoproteinemia and hypocholesterolemia; lower FPG; hypotriglyceridemia more common in underweight patients.
  5. Conclusion: underweight and nutrient deficiencies are common and need monitoring to prevent serious health problems.

6. Evaluating Suzuki et al. (2014)

⚡ Quick Summary — 评价的四个抓手

  1. 信度：使用标准化验血程序、客观量化数据，且结果与 Kitabayashi (2006) 相互印证
  2. 效度问题：横断面设计无法判断因果；未记录运动、饮食等混淆变量
  3. 诊断主观性：DSM-IV-TR 依赖临床判断，患者可能并非都患精神分裂症
  4. 泛化性：仅日本新潟县住院患者，样本可能不具代表性
AO3 — Evaluation, Analysis & Application Strengths and weaknesses

✅ Strengths（优点）

  • Reliability: The findings corroborate Kitabayashi and colleagues (2006), who also found that being underweight was more common in patients with schizophrenia in Japan than the general population. This consistency between two studies demonstrates that the findings are reliable. Suzuki's study was conducted in Niigata in the north west and Kitabayashi's study was conducted in Nara which is further south, suggesting the findings can be applied to different areas of Japan and have good external validity.
  • Objective data: Suzuki et al. used standardised scientific procedures to conduct the blood tests, e.g. using a centrifuge（离心机） to collect plasma to test for glucose. The cut-off points to decide whether a person had a nutrient deficiency made these measures more objective, which helps improve the credibility of the results.

❌ Weaknesses（缺点）

  • Sample size and representativeness: The sample size was fairly small (n = 333) and the researcher states that they may not have been representative of the wider population of patients with schizophrenia as some had very short admissions, which is not typical in Japan.
  • Subjectivity of diagnosis: Although the study has many objective aspects, the DSM-IV-TR was used to diagnose schizophrenia and this requires considerable clinical judgement as it is a difficult disorder to diagnose due to overlapping symptoms. This means that some of the patients may not have had schizophrenia.
  • Missing variables: Although the researcher collected data on participant variables such as age, they did not record other relevant variables such as exercise or eating behaviours. For example, it is unclear why patients were underweight — was this a consequence of low appetite or due to inability to absorb nutrients from their food? Both of these may be side-effects of antipsychotic medication.
  • Cross-sectional design: If the researchers had conducted a longitudinal study they could have monitored changes in weight over time to see whether patients lost weight throughout their hospital admission and whether this varied depending on how long they had been taking different combinations and dosage of antipsychotic drugs.
Key Term — Centrifuge（离心机）
A laboratory device that separates components of a liquid using rapid spinning, e.g. blood can be separated into plasma and the heavier blood cells.
中文理解：这个研究最大的优点是客观——BMI 和血液指标都是机器测量、有明确 cutoff。但最大的局限是横断面：我们只能看到"患者更瘦、营养指标更低"，但不知道是先有营养不良再有精神问题，还是长期住院/吃药导致营养变差。

🏆 Gold-standard paragraph

A major weakness of Suzuki et al. (2014) is the cross-sectional design. Because data was collected at a single point in time, the study can only show that patients and controls differ — it cannot establish whether being underweight or having abnormal blood chemistry is a cause or a consequence of schizophrenia, or indeed a side-effect of antipsychotic medication that all patients were taking. This weakens the study's validity as a basis for clinical intervention. A stronger design would be longitudinal, tracking patients' weight and nutrient levels over the course of their hospital stay to see how antipsychotic dosage and length of admission relate to nutritional status.

▶ Exam Key Points — Evaluating Suzuki AO3
  1. Strength: corroborated by Kitabayashi et al. (2006) in a different Japanese region → reliability.
  2. Strength: objective machine-measured blood data and standardised procedures (centrifuge) → high objectivity.
  3. Weakness: cross-sectional → no cause-and-effect conclusions possible.
  4. Weakness: DSM-IV-TR diagnosis requires clinical judgement → some patients may not have had schizophrenia.
  5. Weakness: missing variables such as exercise/eating behaviours; medication confounds cannot be ruled out.
  6. Weakness: sample drawn only from inpatients in Niigata, Japan → limited generalisability.

7. Contemporary Study: Hans & Hiller (2013)

Outpatient CBT for Unipolar Depression — A Meta-Analysis（门诊 CBT 治疗单相抑郁的元分析）

⚡ Quick Summary — 30 秒看懂这个研究

  1. 问题：门诊 CBT 在真实日常临床环境中治疗抑郁是否有效？脱落率如何？
  2. 方法：元分析（meta-analysis） — 合并 34 个非随机门诊 CBT 疗效研究
  3. 关注点：个体 vs 团体 CBT、治疗次数与效果的关系（剂量-反应）、与 RCT 基准比较
  4. 结果：完成者和 ITT 样本的效应量都很大；个体 CBT 脱落率高于团体；实际临床效果低于 RCT 基准
  5. 结论：门诊 CBT 可以移植到日常临床实践，但需要改善高脱落率
AO1 — Knowledge & Understanding Describe the study

Background and Aim（背景与目的）

Previous meta-analyses of randomised controlled trials (RCTs) have supported cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), a treatment for adults with depression (Cuijpers et al., 2008). This can be offered through individual or group treatments; however individual treatments seem to be more effective and lead to lower dropout rates. Research also suggests a dose–response relationship（剂量-反应关系）, meaning the more sessions a person attends, the better the overall outcomes.

However, these RCTs typically measure efficacy rather than effectiveness, meaning what happens under ideal circumstances. In clinical practice, patients may have more complex needs, including comorbid disorders. RCTs exclude people with the most serious symptoms for their own safety. In clinical practice this could be as many as 76 per cent of patients (Stirman et al., 2003). Therapists are also highly trained, supervised and monitored. This ensures that CBT is delivered according to strict instruction manuals and therefore standardised as far as possible. In practice, therapists rarely use manuals. They adapt the therapy to meet their patients' needs and sometimes offer more sessions than would be available through a RCT.

This study aimed to assess the effectiveness of and drop-out rates for CBT offered to adults diagnosed with unipolar depressive disorder in routine clinical practice. It also aimed to investigate differences between individual versus group CBT and dose-response relationships. Finally the researchers aimed to compare their findings with previous high quality RCTs in a process called 'benchmarking'（基准比较） (baselining).

Key Term — Meta-analysis（元分析）
A statistical technique that combines the results of many separate studies on the same question into one overall conclusion. 一个评委只看一场比赛可能判断失误；元分析就像把多位评委的打分汇总起来算总分——样本更大、结论更稳。
Key Term — Dose–response relationship（剂量-反应关系）
A positive correlation between the amount of a certain medication or treatment administered and the degree of improvement observed.

Method and Sample（方法与样本）

This was a meta-analysis of effectiveness studies. The meta-analysis included 34 studies. All were non-randomised clinical trials representative of routine practice. All studies focused on face-to-face CBT for depressed adult outpatients. Efficacy studies were excluded.

The researchers found 30 studies using electronic databases (e.g. MEDLINE and PsychINFO). Search terms included: community mental health centre, effectiveness, naturalistic, outpatient clinic and uncontrolled. Six more studies were found through asking experts to identify other relevant but unpublished, or non-peer reviewed studies, e.g. dissertations.

Studies were included if patients:

  • had a main diagnosis of major or minor depression or dysthymic disorder
  • did not have other medical conditions or were elderly
  • were allowed to continue taking medication
  • were not randomised to different treatment/control groups
  • were representative of a wider range of people seeking help for depression than are typically seen in RCTs

Therapists needed to be:

  • not monitored or given additional training
  • professional and flexible in their approach

In total, the studies included 1,880 patients who completed treatment and 1,629 patients who were intended to be treated; 68 per cent were women, with an average age of 38 years. Of those, 54 per cent were taking antidepressants.

Key Term — Intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis（意向治疗分析）
Data is analysed even if the participant did not finish the treatment programme or did not engage with all aspects as this is more authentic to real-world situations.

Procedure（程序）

Studies were coded to give an indication of their clinical representativeness and methodological quality. This included:

  • low drop-out rate (30 per cent)
  • intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis
  • formal diagnostic investigation (i.e. whether diagnoses were checked)
  • minimum sample size of 30
  • minimum follow-up length of six months

Effect sizes were calculated for end-of-treatment and six-month follow-up effects for depression severity. These were calculated separately for people who completed all therapy sessions and those who dropped out (called intention to treat). Effect sizes were also identified for completers for the following measures: dysfunctional cognitions, general anxiety, psychological distress and functional impairment. Mean drop-out is also reported.

Studies were grouped by effect size; e.g. small (0.2), moderate (0.5) or large (0.8). Studies with effect sizes that were outliers (i.e. more than three standard deviations from the mean) were rejected. Drop-out was defined as anyone who attended at least one session but did not attend the number advised by the therapist.

A selection of suitable RCTs were chosen to provide a benchmark (baseline) for comparison of effect sizes for efficacy versus effectiveness studies (Cuijpers et al., 2010).

Results（结果）

Table 24.2 shows the average number of sessions attended. Individual sessions lasted 50–60 minutes and group sessions were 90–120 minutes; 77 per cent of the studies had samples of 30 or more. Drop-out data was obtained for 68 per cent of studies. The mean drop-out rate was 24.63 per cent; range from 0 per cent to 68 per cent. Follow-up data was obtained for 29 per cent of studies, at least six months after the final treatment session.

The average effect size for the completers was 0.75–2.03 with a mean effect size of 1.13.   • The considerable treatment drop-out rate, especially in individual CBT, must be improved.
  • The small number of available studies and low quality of some reports stress the need for high-quality effectiveness studies.

7b. Evaluating Hans & Hiller (2013)

⚡ Quick Summary — 评价的四个抓手

  1. 编码信度高：第一、第二作者共同编码，一致性极高，提升可信度
  2. 减少发表偏倚：主动寻找未发表/非同行评审研究，并用漏斗图检验
  3. 研究质量参差不齐：24% 的研究中治疗师仍被监督，降低日常代表性
  4. 诊断来源不一致：仅 44% 的研究使用正式诊断工具，其余靠临床判断
AO3 — Evaluation, Analysis & Application Strengths and weaknesses

✅ Strengths（优点）

  • Reliable coding: All studies were coded by the first and second author regarding clinical representativeness and methodological quality, based on key features of the samples and procedures. This coding was highly reliable as shown by near-perfect inter-rater reliability. When disagreement did arise, ratings were discussed until a consensus was reached.
  • Reduced publication bias: The researchers used various ways to reduce and then identify publication bias. Firstly, they asked experts to recommend unpublished and non-peer reviewed effectiveness studies such as dissertations. They also used funnel plots and a statistical test to identify any potential publication bias. No evidence of publication bias was found for studies of completers.

❌ Weaknesses（缺点）

  • Small-study bias in ITT data: The data for the ITT group did show evidence of small-study bias, whereby studies with smaller samples tended to have higher effect sizes. This suggests that the overall effect size derived from the meta-analysis of ITT data may be exaggerated — those who drop out of therapy may not have such impressive outcomes.
  • Some studies did not meet criteria: Despite attempting to only include effectiveness studies which represented routine clinical practice, some of the studies did not meet these criteria. For example, in 24 per cent of studies therapy was monitored either by extensive supervision of the therapist or formal adherence checks. This reduces the validity of the findings as in regular clinical practice therapists may be allowed more professional freedom.
  • Diagnostic uncertainty: Diagnostic interviews and/or checklists were only used in 44 per cent of studies. In the remaining studies, there was no mention of a specific diagnostic instrument or diagnosis was based on clinical judgement alone. This is a weakness as it means there is uncertainty around whether all participants were actually suffering from a depressive disorder. As anxiety and depression are often comorbid, some may have had a different primary diagnosis. This is one of the problems with using secondary data.

A graph used to identify publication bias in a meta-analysis; effect size is plotted on the horizontal axis and sample size on the vertical axis. A symmetrical inverted funnel indicates no publication bias whereas an asymmetrical funnel indicates bias.

  2. Strength: searched for unpublished/non-peer-reviewed studies and used funnel plots to assess publication bias.
  3. Weakness: ITT data showed small-study bias → overall effect size may be exaggerated.
  4. Weakness: 24% of studies still monitored therapists → reduces representativeness of routine practice.
  5. Weakness: only 44% used formal diagnosis → uncertainty about whether all participants had depression; secondary-data problem.

⚡ Quick Summary — 30 秒看懂这个研究

  1. 问题：核心自我评价（怎么看自己）和社会支持（感受到被关心）怎么影响抑郁？
  2. 方法：相关研究（correlational study） — 538 名中国大学生在网上填三份问卷
  3. 三份量表：CSES（核心自我评价）+ MSPSS（感知社会支持）+ Zung SDS（抑郁）
  4. 关键结果：核心自我评价与抑郁 r = −0.636（强负相关）；社会支持起部分中介作用（partial mediation），间接效应 −0.18
  5. 结论：自我评价积极的人更容易获得社会支持，从而更不容易抑郁；但相关研究不能证明因果
AO1 — Knowledge & Understanding Describe the study

Background（背景）

Previous research has found a negative correlation between self-evaluation and depression — the more negatively you see yourself, the more depressed you tend to be. Negative beliefs about the self may be a causal factor in depression, help to maintain the disorder and/or trigger relapse (De Raedt et al., 2006). Another factor which may mediate this relationship is actual and/or perceived social support — the belief that one is cared for — which may be a protective factor（保护因素）. However, how these three factors interact is unclear, and most research only focused on two of the three factors at a time.

中文理解：以前的研究知道两件事：① 越看不起自己越容易抑郁；② 有人关心是"保护伞"。但没人把三个变量放在一起看：自我评价 → 社会支持 → 抑郁这条链是怎么走的？这就是 Ma 等人要补的空白。

Aim（目的）

To investigate how self-evaluation influences depression and whether social support is a mediating factor in this relationship. Ze-Wei Ma and colleagues (2014) predicted:

  • a negative relationship between core self-evaluation and depression
  • a positive relationship between perceived social support and core self-evaluation
  • that social support will mediate the relationship between core self-evaluation and depression.
Key Term — Core self-evaluation（核心自我评价）
How a person fundamentally evaluates themselves — the bottom-line judgement of "am I good enough?". It combines four ingredients: self-esteem（自尊）, self-efficacy（自我效能感，相信自己能做成事）, neuroticism（神经质，情绪稳定性） and locus of control（控制点，觉得人生是自己掌控还是命运掌控）.
可以这样理解：它不是你某一刻的心情，而是你对自己"总体的打分"——就像手机的系统版本，比单个 App（某一次成败）更基础、更稳定。

Method / Design（研究方法与设计）

  • Design: correlational research; self-report surveys were used to collect the quantitative, primary data（相关研究，问卷收集第一手量化数据）
  • Analysis: Pearson's correlation coefficients, then a mediation analysis to test whether social support carries part of the link between core self-evaluation and depression

8b. Evaluating Ma, Quan & Liu (2014)

⚡ Quick Summary — 评价的四个抓手

  1. 方法符合伦理：相关研究不需要人为操纵变量——研究"自我评价/抑郁"本来就不可能把人随机分组去抑郁
  2. 量表经过信效度检验：Cronbach's alpha +0.82，且与 Rosenberg 自尊量表显著相关（同时效度）
  3. 样本大但单一：538 人统计功效高，但全是中国大陆本科生——以偏概全（ethnocentric）
  4. 自陈报告有偏差：社会赞许性（social desirability bias）在集体主义文化中更明显；CSES 第 2 题"有时我觉得很沮丧"和抑郁量表重叠
AO3 — Evaluation, Analysis & Application Strengths and weaknesses

✅ Strengths（优点）

  • Ethically possible correlational design: This topic does not require any artificial manipulation of variables, which would be impossible ethically in a study investigating core self-evaluation, social support and depression. This means the researchers were able to collect credible data via self-reports about people's real experiences regarding support from friends and family, self-esteem and self-efficacy, and a range of physical, cognitive and emotional symptoms of depression.
  • Questionnaires tested for reliability: All three questionnaires have been tested for reliability using Cronbach's alpha（克朗巴赫 α 系数）. For example, Esmail Hashemi (2011) found significant correlations between the individual items in the CSES, suggesting high internal reliability (+0.82). He also found a significant correlation between the CSES and self-esteem (Rosenberg, 1965), suggesting the scale has strong concurrent validity.
  • Large sample: The sample size was relatively large at 538, meaning the statistical power of the tests should be high — the correlation coefficients found are unlikely to be due to chance.

❌ Weaknesses（缺点）

  • Cannot establish causation: Correlational research means the researchers are unable to establish causation. Even though the study points to the role of social support as a mediator of depression, the researchers cannot claim that absence of perceived social support causes depression, without controlling for the effect of confounding variables.
  • Ethnocentric sample: The sample only included undergraduate students from mainland China, meaning the data is ethnocentric and may not be representative of people of different ages, or educational background, within or outside China.
  • Social desirability bias: All measures rely on self-reports. People may not be honest when reporting attitudes towards social support, as they may feel that negative responses would reflect badly on their family. This may be more evident in collectivist cultures（集体主义文化）where loyalty to in-groups is highly valued. Also, some CSES items may not be valid in collectivist cultures — e.g. "I determine what will happen in my life" may lack cultural relevance in cultures which emphasise external/situational factors rather than dispositional factors.
  • Scale item overlap: Item 2 of the CSES is "Sometimes I feel depressed", which helps explain the correlation with the measure of depression — the two scales may be measuring overlapping content.
Key Term — Cronbach's alpha（克朗巴赫 α 系数）
A statistical measure which allows the researcher to establish internal reliability — the extent to which different items within a questionnaire are measuring the same underlying construct/variable. A value above 0.7 is usually considered acceptable. 就像检查一份 12 题的问卷是不是都在测"同一种东西"：如果第 1 题测自信、第 2 题却在测饭量，那 α 系数就会很低。
▶ Exam Key Points — Evaluating Ma et al. AO3
  1. Strength: correlational design is ethical — no manipulation possible/needed; gathers real-life self-report data.
  2. Strength: all three questionnaires have established reliability (Cronbach's alpha +0.82 for CSES) and concurrent validity.
  3. Strength: large sample (538) gives high statistical power.
  4. Weakness: correlation ≠ causation; confounding variables uncontrolled.
  5. Weakness: ethnocentric sample (mainland Chinese undergraduates only).
  6. Weakness: self-report → social desirability bias, stronger in collectivist cultures; some CSES items may lack cultural relevance.

AO1 — Knowledge & Understanding Describe the study

Background（背景）

A greater prevalence of eating disorders in minority cultures compared with majority cultures suggests that culture can be an important risk or protective factor, depending on where in the world you live. The validity of existing studies linking Western media exposure and eating disorders is limited, as they were conducted in cultures where such disorders are highly prevalent and media consumption is also high. This prospective research（前瞻性研究） took place in a culture with no prior television experience.

中文理解：以前研究"媒体→进食障碍"都是在欧美做的——那里人人天天看电视，你根本分不清是电视的锅还是本来社会就有问题。斐济不同：1995 年前岛上几乎没电视，等于一个干净的"实验室"：研究者能观察"电视第一次进来"会发生什么。

Aim（目的）

To investigate the influence of prolonged exposure to Western television on attitudes towards eating and eating behaviour in adolescent girls in Fiji. Television had only been recently introduced in this culture. Research questions included:

  • whether television exposure might encourage disordered eating even in a culture which supports "robust appetites and body shapes"（丰腴的食欲和体型）and discourages exercise and dieting for weight loss
  • whether disordered eating in Fijian girls would be associated with body dissatisfaction, as it is in minority cultures
  • whether disordered attitudes towards eating might mediate the association between media exposure and disordered eating.

Method / Design（研究方法与设计）

  • Design: a cross-sectional study（横断面研究） comparing two groups of girls — before and after television exposure. The researchers also refer to the study as a 'naturalistic experiment'（自然实验）: it has an independent variable (before vs after TV exposure), but it is not manipulated by the researcher — it is something which varies in everyday life. The researchers did not install the satellite dishes that allowed the islanders to receive the channel; this was a naturally occurring change in Fijian culture which they decided to study.
中文理解：自然实验 = 研究者"捡"了个现成机会：电视是斐济社会自己引进的，研究者只是站在旁边观察前后变化。好处是真实（生态效度高）；坏处是他们无法控制电视之外同时发生的其他变化（比如经济开放、消费文化）——所以不能说"电视导致了"这些改变，只能说"电视来了之后"变化发生了。

Sample（样本）

  • Fiji was selected because, before television was introduced, there had only been one documented case of anorexia nervosa in the mid-1990s.
  • All participants were English-speaking girls from two secondary schools in Nadroga province（斐济 Nadroga 省的两所中学）.
  • Cohort 1 (1995): n = 63, mean age 17.3 — data collected within a month of television being introduced.
  • Cohort 2 (1998): n = 65, mean age 16.9 — data collected from girls at the same schools after three years of television exposure.

Procedure（步骤）

  • EAT-26（进食态度测验，Garner et al., 1982）: a 26-item eating attitudes test used to collect quantitative data, including questions about bingeing and purging. Scores over 20 were classed as high（超过 20 分即高风险）.
  • Participants were asked whether they had a television at home and, if so, how often they watched it.
  • The 1998 version of the questionnaire also asked about:
    • body image — e.g. would it bother you if you were too thin/heavy?
    • dieting — e.g. do you ever think that you should eat less?
    • differences in attitudes between participants and their parents regarding diet and weight — e.g. do your parents or family ever say that you should eat more?
  • Semi-structured interviews（半结构访谈） were conducted with girls in both cohorts whose EAT-26 scores suggested disordered eating behaviours. This allowed the researchers to check that vomiting was induced to control weight, for example. In 1998, 30 girls with disordered eating attitudes and behaviour were interviewed and asked open questions such as "What do you think about American TV?" and closed questions such as "Do you admire any characters on TV?" and "Do you ever wish you could be more like them?"
  • The interviews were audio-taped, transcribed and analysed using thematic content analysis（主题内容分析）.
Key Term — Prospective research（前瞻性研究）
A longitudinal study in which a group of participants are monitored over time, and comparisons are made between data collected at the beginning of the study and at more time points in the future. 简单说：研究者"提前埋伏好"，跟着时间走，看事情往后怎么发展，而不是事后翻旧账。

9b. Evaluating Becker et al. (2002)

⚡ Quick Summary — 评价的四个抓手

  1. 混合方法（mixed methods）：问卷给数字 + 访谈给故事，互相补充，可信度高
  2. 横断面设计的弱点：两批人不是同一批人——1995 和 1998 的差异可能是别的因素（如全球化、消费文化）造成的
  3. 样本太局限：只有 Nadroga 省两所学校，不能代表斐济其他地方
  4. 没诊断进食障碍：只用 EAT-26 + BMI，没有用 DSM/ICD 诊断；全部自陈数据，可能有需求特征（demand characteristics）
AO3 — Evaluation, Analysis & Application Strengths and weaknesses

✅ Strengths（优点）

  • Mixed methods — questionnaires plus interviews: The researchers collected data about eating attitudes and behaviour and television exposure using both questionnaires and interviews. The EAT-26 questionnaire allowed them to gather quantitative data from closed questions, e.g. "Feel extremely guilty after eating", with answers "always / usually / often" scoring 3, 2 or 1 respectively, while "sometimes / rarely / never" all scored 0. This acknowledges that everyone feels guilty about food sometimes, which should not count towards the overall score. The quantitative data allowed the researchers to use statistics to determine the probability of their results — in many cases the probability was low, suggesting television exposure was associated with changes in body satisfaction and dieting.
  • Interviews add credibility: Statistics do not allow participants to tell their story in their own words. Becker overcame this by including semi-structured interviews, where she could explore the role of peers and family members in shaping attitudes and behaviour, and find out more about why the girls were trying to lose weight and what they thought about characters on television. This increases the credibility of the study as the researchers provide direct quotes to support the themes that emerged in the thematic content analysis.

❌ Weaknesses（缺点）

  • Cross-sectional design — participant variables and cohort effects: The cross-sectional design means participant variables may be a threat to the validity of the findings. Although the girls in each cohort were matched for age, gender, school attended and ethnicity, it is impossible to know whether the girls in the 1998 cohort would have had more negative eating attitudes if they had been asked in 1995, or vice versa. Cross-sectional studies are also affected by cohort effects（队列效应） — television exposure may not have been the only aspect of Fijian culture impacting their well-being; other aspects of globalisation and consumerism may also have been increasing pressure on the young women.
  • Limited sample: The sample is restricted to only two schools in a single province of Fiji, which reduces the generalisability of the findings. Girls in other areas of Fiji, girls not in formal education, and girls in all-girls versus mixed schools may have behaved differently.
  • Did not actually measure eating disorders: The researchers did not use the DSM or the ICD to make a diagnosis; they simply looked at EAT-26 data and the girls' BMI. This means the conclusions about eating disorders should be viewed with caution.
  • Self-reported data and demand characteristics: All the data was entirely self-reported. The girls may have demonstrated demand characteristics（需求特征）— especially when being interviewed by an unfamiliar, American research team, they may have been more likely to agree with statements which showed approval of American and other Western women.
中文理解：评价 Becker 最关键的思维是区分"变化真的发生了吗？"（量化数据：是的，很可信）和"是电视导致的吗？"（不一定是：横断面设计 + 同期其他变化 + 样本局限，让因果结论要打折扣）。考试里这样分层答，就能拿到 AO3 的高分。
▶ Exam Key Points — Evaluating Becker et al. AO3
  1. Strength: mixed methods — EAT-26 quantitative data plus semi-structured interviews with direct quotes; credible, detailed picture.
  2. Strength: unique setting — a culture with no prior TV and rare eating disorders; strong baseline for the media explanation.
  3. Weakness: cross-sectional design → participant variables and cohort effects; cannot isolate TV from other globalisation changes.
  4. Weakness: sample limited to two schools in one province → limited generalisability.
  5. Weakness: no DSM/ICD diagnosis — only EAT-26 + BMI; all self-report → demand characteristics with the American research team.

10. Contemporary Study: Reichel et al. (2014)

"Glass Fairies" and "Bone Children" — Startle Responses to Emaciated Bodies in Anorexia Nervosa（厌食症患者对"皮包骨"身体的惊跳反应）

⚡ Quick Summary — 30 秒看懂这个研究

  1. 问题：厌食症患者说"怕胖"，但他们的身体对"极度消瘦"的图片到底是什么反应？说出来的话可能骗人，生理反应骗不了人
  2. 样本：柏林 36 名女性厌食症患者（14–21 岁，ICD-10 诊断，BMI 低于同年龄第 10 百分位）vs 36 名匹配健康对照
  3. 方法：实验室实验 — 看 52 张图片（12 不愉快 / 12 中性 / 12 愉快 / 16 恶病质），同时测 EMG 惊跳、皮肤电、ECG 心率 + 主观评分
  4. 关键结果：患者看到恶病质图片时惊跳被抑制（和看愉快图片一样）；对照组的惊跳几乎是愉快图片的两倍。但两组的主观报告没有差异——都说恶病质图片不愉快
  5. 结论：厌食症患者对极端消瘦有自动的趋近（appetitive）生理反应，尽管她们嘴上说反感——"理想化恶病质"可能是进食障碍的动机因素
AO1 — Knowledge & Understanding Describe the study

Background（背景）

Questionnaire data shows that people with anorexia nervosa report both 'fear of fatness'（怕胖） and 'drive for thinness'（追求瘦） (Thiel et al., 1997). However, objective measures tell a different story. For example, people with anorexia nervosa show greater aversive (negative) bodily responses to images relating to food and fatness than controls. Conversely, they do not show significantly different responses to controls when shown images representing a thin body ideal. Various measures have been trialled to find support for the idea that the drive for thinness is as strong as fear of fatness in people with anorexia nervosa, but so far, no objective evidence has been found.

This led researchers to wonder whether the type of stimuli used in these studies was simply not extreme enough to create a positive (appetitive) response in people with anorexia nervosa. Pro-ana forums/websites（支持厌食的网站） promote disordered eating attitudes and behaviour through the use of 'thinspiration'（"瘦身灵感"） — image galleries containing photographs of girls and women with visible ribs, hip and cheekbones, sunken cheeks and abdomens — signs of cachexia（恶病质）, the final irreversible signs of anorexic weight loss. The people in the images are referred to as 'glass fairies'（玻璃仙子）, 'bone children'（骨头小孩）and 'feather girls'（羽毛女孩）, leading site/forum users to develop positive associations with the imagery and a positive in-group identity with other users.

Key Term — Cachexia（恶病质） / Emaciation（极度消瘦）
Cachexia: a serious medical condition caused by extreme weight loss in which bones are visible. Emaciation: extreme thinness caused by illness or malnutrition.
可以理解为"皮包骨"——比普通意义上的瘦严重得多，是疾病状态，不是模特身材。

Aims（目的）

To compare emotional processing of images of extremely emaciated bodies (thinspiration) in adolescents and young adults with and without anorexia nervosa (AN). The researchers hoped that the use of such extreme images would be sufficient to show that people with AN have an appetitive reaction（趋近/吸引反应，positive response indicating attraction） to such images, whereas healthy controls have an aversive reaction（厌恶反应，negative reaction indicating dislike）.

The researchers expected that:

  1. Startle responses would decrease as images became more pleasant.
  2. Startle responses would be greater than controls for patients when viewing emaciation images.
  3. There will be no difference in self-reported data for the patients and controls when viewing emaciation images.
  4. Skin conductance, heart rate and self-reported arousal will be higher when viewing pleasant and unpleasant images (including emaciation) compared with neutral images.
  5. All participants will show an increase in skin conductance and heart rate when viewing emaciation images compared with other images.

Method / Design（研究方法与设计）

  • Design: an experiment with two designs combined:
    • Repeated measures（重复测量）: the IV was the type of image (pleasant, unpleasant, neutral or emaciation) — all participants saw all types of image.
    • Independent measures（独立测量）: the second IV was diagnosis — participants were either in the AN group or the control group.
    • Counterbalancing（平衡/抵消） was used to control order effects.
  • Dependent variables（因变量）:
    • Objective: startle response, skin conductance and heart rate.
    • Subjective: self-reported measures of pleasantness, arousal and dominance.

Sample（样本）

Recruitment In-/outpatient programmes in Berlin, Germany（德国柏林） Newspaper adverts, youth activity centres and a university campus

Key Term — Percentile（百分位）
A value which indicates the proportion of the population who scored lower than the participant on a given item. E.g. the 80th percentile for intelligence would mean the participant scored higher than 80 per cent of the population. 简单说：BMI 低于同龄人第 10 百分位 = 比 90% 的同龄人更瘦。

Procedure — Materials（材料）

  • Participants were shown 52 coloured 100 × 144 mm photographs: 12 unpleasant, 12 neutral, 12 pleasant and 16 emaciation body images.
  • Non-body pictures were chosen from the International Affective Picture System（IAPS，Lang et al., 2008）. None included food or inappropriate content, e.g. violence, blood.
  • The 16 emaciation images were chosen from pro-ana galleries containing 8000 photographs（从包含 8000 张照片的支持厌食网站图库中选出）. All showed signs of cachexia. Faces and other distinguishing features were digitally removed for ethical reasons, and all backgrounds were standardised.
  • 100 healthy volunteers rated the images (one to nine scales) for arousal, aversion and how underweight they appeared. The 16 highest scoring images were selected.

Procedure — Pre-test（前测）

  • Mood, arousal, hunger state and body dissatisfaction were assessed using 100 mm visual analogue scales (VAS)（视觉模拟量表）.
  • Participants sat 1.5 metres from a 1.5 × 1.7 m screen.
  • They were shown four landscape images with three startle-eliciting noises as an introduction (to familiarise them with the procedure).

Procedure — Main Task（正式实验）

  • Participants viewed the 52 images while wearing headphones. Each image was shown for 12 seconds, with a black screen between each image.
  • Stimuli were shown in two counterbalanced sets of 26 pictures; each set was presented in a randomised order. There was a 15-minute break between each set.
  • Seventy-five per cent (39 out of 52) of images in each category were shown with a startling noise, played 3.5, 4 or 4.5 seconds after the image appeared. Noises were also played during 25 per cent of the black screens — this ensured the noises were unpredictable. Each noise was 50 milliseconds long and 95 decibels (dB) in volume.

Results（结果）

  • Startle reflex: Average startle reflex was significantly greater for unpleasant than neutral images, and neutral images elicited a greater startle response than pleasant images — true of both patients and non-patients.
  • Patients: showed a significantly lower startle reaction towards the emaciation images compared with the neutral images. There was no significant difference for the patients between emaciation images and pleasant images — the extreme thinness images did not trigger a defensive response.
  • Controls: the control group's startle response to emaciation images was almost double that of the pleasant images (although not as high as the unpleasant images) — a strong defensive response.
  • Self-reports: Both patients and controls rated the emaciation images as unpleasant compared with the neutral images, and there was no significant difference in this self-reported data between the groups. Interestingly, the control group rated the emaciation images as highly unpleasant, yet their startle reflex was less extreme than that created by the unpleasant non-body images.
  • Skin conductance: showed no significant difference between the pleasant, neutral and unpleasant image types or between the two groups — except that emaciation images elicited higher skin conductance than neutral images in both groups.
  • Heart rate: Compared with the controls, the patients showed significantly lower overall heart rate and heart rate deceleration（心率减速） when viewing both the emaciation images and the non-body images; they also self-reported lower arousal. In the controls, overall heart rate and heart rate deceleration showed the greatest change after viewing unpleasant pictures, followed by emaciation, neutral and pleasant images.
  • Self-reported arousal: There was no significant difference between patients and controls — all participants rated the emaciation and unpleasant images as similarly arousing.

Conclusions（结论）

  • Adolescent female patients diagnosed with anorexia nervosa show automatic, appetitive physiological reactions（自动的趋近性生理反应） towards emaciated body images — despite self-reports that suggest they find these images highly aversive.

10b. Evaluating Reichel et al. (2014)

⚡ Quick Summary — 评价的四个抓手

  1. 诊断被严格核查：用 SIAB-EX 结构化访谈确认诊断，信度 +0.8，比依赖二手数据的研究强
  2. 客观测量 + 明确剔除标准：EMG 数据有清晰的接受/拒绝规则（如潜伏期 <20ms），减少研究者主观判断
  3. 心率的效度问题：95% 的厌食症患者本来就有心动过缓（bradycardia）——心率差异可能只是疾病本身的表现
  4. 样本单一：只有 14–21 岁女性、全在柏林——不能推广到男性、儿童或中老年人
AO3 — Evaluation, Analysis & Application Strengths and weaknesses

✅ Strengths（优点）

  • Diagnosis verified: The patients' diagnosis was checked using various well-established measures, for example the Structured Interview for Anorexic and Bulimic Eating Disorders (SIAB-EX). This measurement has strong inter-rater reliability (+0.8) and construct validity (Fichter and Quadflieg, 2001). This is a strength in comparison with studies that rely on secondary data relating to diagnoses that may not reflect the person's current mental health status.
  • Objective measures with clear rules: Many of the measures were highly objective. For example, the study includes a detailed description of how the startle reflex was obtained using the data relating to the EMG magnitude and latency. There was very clear-cut information about if and when a trial should be rejected, e.g. latency less than 20 ms, and the point at which a participant was classified as a non-responder, leading to the rejection of all of their data (n = 2). This helps to make the study more scientific, as the procedure removed researcher bias relating to whether data should be rejected or not.
  • Control of confounding noise: The researchers only collected skin conductance and heart rate data for images which were not accompanied by a startling noise, because the noise could have affected these involuntary bodily responses — making it impossible to determine whether differences were due to the type of image or the confounding effect of the unexpected noise. This helped to improve the internal validity of the findings.

❌ Weaknesses（缺点）

  • Heart rate data may not be valid for this group: 95 per cent of people with a diagnosis of anorexia nervosa have bradycardia（心动过缓） — low heart rate caused by the parasympathetic nervous system attempting to conserve energy. Mild physical exertion can cause rapid tachycardia in such patients. This means that any abnormalities of heart rate observed in the study may be an effect of having anorexia nervosa rather than telling us anything about the causes of this condition.
  • Reduced data due to technical difficulties: Data was only collected on 13 of 52 trials (25 per cent) for skin conductance and heart rate, and data was rejected from 16 participants due to technical difficulties, which reduced the sample size for this aspect of the study and the possibility of finding significant results.
  • Mere exposure alternative explanation: The decrease in physiological reactions to the emaciation images in the anorexia nervosa group may not indicate an appetite for this type of imagery. It may simply be that the people in this group were more familiar with signs of cachexia compared with people without the condition, and this is why the images did not produce the same aversive responses. The relationship between mere exposure and liking is well established in psychology (Zajonc et al., 1968). The researchers noted that the people with anorexia nervosa in this study knew each other and therefore were exposed to such imagery every day; they may also have been users of pro-ana forums themselves, and this was not assessed in this study.
  • Limited sample — age and gender: It only included people with anorexia nervosa aged 14 to 21 years and did not include any males. All participants were also from Berlin in Germany, making the sample ethnocentric. The results should be generalised with caution, as the sample was not representative of boys and men, younger children, or middle-aged and older adults.
中文理解：这个研究在考试中最常被问的两点：① 客观性（objectivity）——生理测量 + 明确剔除规则，非常科学；② 推广性（generalisability）——全是柏林 14–21 岁女性患者，结果不能随便推广。另外要记住"熟悉度"这个替代解释：患者天天见这类图片，见多了不害怕，不代表"喜欢"。
▶ Exam Key Points — Evaluating Reichel et al. AO3
  1. Strength: verified diagnoses using SIAB-EX (inter-rater reliability +0.8) — stronger than secondary-data studies.
  2. Strength: objective measures (EMG magnitude/latency) with explicit rejection criteria (latency < 20 ms) — removes researcher bias, more scientific.
  3. Strength: skin conductance/heart rate only collected on no-noise trials — controls the confounding effect of the noise, improving internal validity.
  4. Weakness: heart rate data may reflect bradycardia caused by AN itself (95% of patients), not emotional response.
  5. Weakness: only 25% of trials used for skin conductance/heart rate; data from 16 participants rejected → reduced power.
  6. Weakness: mere-exposure alternative explanation (familiarity with cachexia, Zajonc et al., 1968); possible pro-ana forum use not assessed.
  7. Weakness: sample limited to 14–21-year-old females from Berlin → ethnocentric, limited generalisability.

✅ Checkpoint — 课本原题（先自己回答，再点开对答案）

Q1 Who were the participants in Rosenhan's study?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

8 pseudopatients（假病人） — three women and five men, one of whom was Rosenhan himself — plus the real hospital staff and the real patients they encountered. The pseudopatients presented at 12 hospitals in 5 US states. In addition, 118 genuine patients were involved (35 of them suspected the pseudopatients were faking).

Q2 How did the pseudopatients collect qualitative data in the Rosenhan study?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

Through covert participant observation（隐蔽参与观察）. They recorded their observations openly, pretending the notes were part of their treatment ("writing behaviour"), and took notes about staff–patient interactions, the time staff spent with patients, and the hospital routine (e.g. medication, ward life). On average, staff spent only 6.8 minutes per day with patients.

Q3 How did Suzuki et al. measure hypocholesterolemia in their study?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

Through blood tests（血液检测）. Hypocholesterolemia（低胆固醇血症）was defined as a total cholesterol (TC) level of less than 150 mg/dL. The researchers also measured other blood indicators of undernutrition: low total protein (TP < 6.7 g/dL = hypoproteinemia), low fasting plasma glucose (FPG < 70 mg/dL = hypoglycaemia) and low triglycerides (TG < 50 mg/dL = hypotriglyceridemia).

Q4 What is the difference between effectiveness and efficacy?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

Efficacy（效力） = how well a treatment works under ideal, controlled conditions — as tested in randomised controlled trials (RCTs), where therapists are highly trained, supervised and follow strict manuals.
Effectiveness（效果） = how well a treatment works in routine clinical practice（常规临床实践） — with real patients who have complex needs, comorbid disorders and therapists who adapt the therapy. Hans & Hiller (2013) studied effectiveness by meta-analysing non-randomised outpatient studies.

Q5 Why would a researcher use a funnel plot?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

To identify publication bias（发表偏倚） in a meta-analysis. Effect size is plotted on the horizontal axis and sample size on the vertical axis. A symmetrical inverted funnel indicates no publication bias; an asymmetrical funnel (e.g. missing studies in the bottom corner) indicates bias — typically because small studies with null results were never published.

Q6 Name the three covariables in the study by Ma et al.
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

The three variables measured were: core self-evaluation（核心自我评价，CSES）, perceived social support（感知社会支持，MSPSS — 来自家人、朋友、重要他人） and depression（抑郁，Zung SDS）.

Q7 How did Becker et al. measure disordered eating in their study of girls and young women in Fiji?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

Using the EAT-26 questionnaire（26 项进食态度测验，Garner et al., 1982） — scores over 20 were classed as high risk — plus semi-structured interviews（半结构访谈） with girls whose scores suggested disordered eating (30 girls in 1998), and measurement of height/weight for BMI. The interviews were audio-taped, transcribed and analysed using thematic content analysis.

Q8 How did the researchers select the emaciation images?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

The images were chosen from pro-ana galleries containing 8000 photographs（支持厌食网站图库）. Faces and distinguishing features were digitally removed for ethical reasons and backgrounds were standardised. Then 100 healthy volunteers rated the images (on one-to-nine scales) for arousal, aversion and how underweight they appeared — the 16 highest scoring images were selected for the study.

Q9 Which group showed a greater startle response in the study by Reichel et al. — people with a diagnosis of anorexia nervosa or healthy matched controls?
▶ Answer（点开看答案）

The healthy matched controls. Their startle response to the emaciation images was almost double that of the pleasant images. In contrast, the AN patients' startle response to emaciation images was suppressed — it did not differ significantly from their response to pleasant images. So the patients showed an appetitive reaction and the controls an aversive reaction.

📝 Exam Practice — 考试题型实战（课本原题）

Q1 Describe the procedure of the study by Rosenhan (1974) from the point at which the pseudopatients were admitted to the hospitals. AO1 · 4 marks
▶ Model answer plan（点开看答案框架）
  1. After admission, pseudopatients stopped reporting symptoms and behaved normally — they told staff they felt fine and no longer heard voices.
  2. They took notes openly as part of "writing behaviour", recording staff–patient interactions and ward life.
  3. They were observed by real patients (35 of 118 suspected them) but staff treated them as patients — e.g. staff spent on average only 6.8 minutes per day with patients.
  4. They were discharged after 7–52 days (average 19 days), all but one diagnosed with "schizophrenia in remission"（精神分裂症，缓解期） — 2,100 pills were prescribed but none were swallowed.
Q2 Compare Rosenhan (1974) and Suzuki et al. (2014). You must refer to reliability in your answer. AO1 + AO3 · 4 marks
▶ Model answer plan（点开看答案框架）
  • Point 1 — data type and reliability: Suzuki used objective blood tests and standardised BMI categories (machine-measured, replicable → high reliability), whereas Rosenhan relied on qualitative observation notes taken by pseudopatients (subjective interpretation → lower reliability).
  • Point 2 — sample and consistency: Suzuki's large standardised sample (333 patients + 191 controls, DSM-IV-TR criteria) allows consistent, comparable measurement across hospitals; Rosenhan's 8 pseudopatients across 12 hospitals showed inconsistent diagnoses (all but one labelled schizophrenia), highlighting low diagnostic reliability.
  • Conclusion: Suzuki's quantitative design produces more reliable data, but Rosenhan's finding — that the same "sane" behaviour was diagnosed differently — raises a different, equally important question about the reliability of diagnosis itself.
Q3 Analyse your chosen contemporary study in terms of credibility. AO3 · 6 marks
▶ Model answer plan — using Becker (点开看答案框架)
  • Define credibility（可信度）: whether the findings can be trusted as a true picture of what was studied.
  • For credibility: mixed methods — EAT-26 quantitative data plus semi-structured interviews with direct quotes supporting each theme (e.g. "I just want to be slim because [the television characters] are slim"). Triangulation of questionnaire + interview + BMI strengthens confidence in the findings.
  • Against credibility: data was entirely self-reported — girls may show demand characteristics, especially with an unfamiliar American research team; the selection of quotes is subjective; the cross-sectional design (different cohorts in 1995 vs 1998) means participant variables and cohort effects could explain the differences.
  • Balance: the pattern of convergent evidence (statistics + quotes + themes) makes the study reasonably credible, but causal claims about television must be cautious.
Q4 Assess your chosen contemporary study in terms of objectivity and generalisability. AO3 · 16 marks

Essay structure（16 分论文怎么搭）:

ParagraphContent
1. IntroDefine objectivity（客观性）and generalisability（可推广性）；state which contemporary study you will assess and why (e.g. Reichel — strong on objectivity, weak on generalisability: a useful contrast).
2. Objectivity — forReichel: objective physiological DVs (EMG startle, skin conductance, ECG heart rate) recorded by machines; explicit data rejection rules (latency < 20 ms) remove researcher judgement; counterbalanced, randomised stimuli presentation → high objectivity.
