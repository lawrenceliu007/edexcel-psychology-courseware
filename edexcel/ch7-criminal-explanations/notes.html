§1 · Theory 1 — Self-Fulfilling Prophecy 自证预言ANSWERS → 课件 §4
Key term 填空
Self-fulfilling prophecy（自证预言）: a theory explaining that a behaviour may result from a belief or expectation 信念或期待 someone has. This expectation influences a person's behaviour in such a way that it causes the expectation to come true 期待最终自我实现.
The process — number the steps 1–4 排序
OrderStep
The person begins to internalise the label（把标签内化）and eventually acts in a way consistent with it — they fulfil the prophecy.
A false expectation is believed about a person, so they are labelled as a particular type of person.
The person is treated differently（被区别对待）according to the label; behaviour inconsistent with it is ignored.
Being treated differently, the person may be isolated from normal society and drawn into criminal groups.
Applied to criminal behaviour — fill the chain 填空
A person is falsely labelled as criminal or antisocial → people treat them with suspicion or caution 怀疑与戒备 → good behaviour that doesn't fit the label is ignored 被忽视 → they become isolated and are drawn into criminal groups 犯罪团伙 → they internalise the label and cause trouble because it is expected of them 被期待如此 → once they offend, the expectation is confirmed 被证实.
Nature–nurture stance 天性 vs 教养立场
SFP is a nurture 教养 explanation: people become criminal because of the way people treat 对待 them — e.g. a child from an antisocial family is expected to offend and treated accordingly. But it ignores 无视 innate influences such as brain functioning or genetics 遗传.
这个立场是评价题的固定采分点：纯 nurture 视角 → 忽略先天因素。

§2 · SFP Evidence — Two Classic Studies 证据研究ANSWERS → 课件 §5
Rosenthal & Jacobson (1968) — the "Bloomers" study
SettingAn academic setting in San Francisco 旧金山, USA.
What they didSome students were labelled as academic bloomers 快开之花 with great potential. In fact students were allocated to this group randomly 随机分配, not by intelligence.
Duration & measureThe study ran for a year 一年, measuring at the start and end.
What they foundThe IQ of 'bloomers' was significantly higher 更高 than non-bloomers — even though bloomers were not necessarily the highest IQ at the start.
ConclusionTeachers' expectations 期待 influenced their behaviour towards students 对学生的行为, and it was this that changed the IQ scores.
Jahoda (1954) — the Ashanti day-names study
Who & whereThe Ashanti 阿散蒂族 people of , who name boys according to the day they were born 出生的星期.
The labels'Monday' boys are considered quiet and placid; 'Wednesday' boys are thought to be aggressive and short-tempered.
Evidence sourcefive years 五年 of records at a local juvenile court 少管所法庭.
Key numbersNearly % of violent offences by Wednesday boys vs % by Monday boys.
ConclusionCultural expectations 期待 and explicit labels led to different treatment 待遇 (e.g. Wednesday boys treated with greater suspicion 怀疑) — many conformed to the label set by their namesake.
Pair the studies 配对记忆
Rosenthal & Jacobson = 教室里的期望（IQ 变了）——proves the chain expectation → treatment → behaviour change, but in education, not crime.
Jahoda = 名字里的期望（犯罪记录变了）——the one study that links the chain directly to antisocial behaviour.
考试问 who found a link between violent offending and the day of the week they were born? — 答 Jahoda (1954).

§3 · Evaluating Self-Fulfilling Prophecy 评价自证预言ANSWERS → 课件 §6

任何一道 evaluate self-fulfilling prophecy 的题，下面三条骨架都适用。填空后各配一句自己的话，就是完整的 AO3 段落。

The three-sentence skeleton 三句答题骨架
#ArgumentFill in the missing reasoning 补全推理
① 证据薄The evidence base is thin for crimeThere is no proven direct link between IQ 智商 (Rosenthal & Jacobson) and criminality — most SFP research is in education 教育, and the criminal claim rests almost entirely on one study: Jahoda (1954).
② 方法限Ethics forbid experiments → correlation onlyIntentionally labelling 贴标签 children to cause antisocial behaviour would be unethical, so experiments are prevented — only correlation 相关 can be shown, and other variables 变量 can never be ruled out.
③ 视野窄It ignores other explanationsBiological factors (brain functioning, genetics 遗传), peer pressure, politics and social or economic circumstances 环境 are all ignored — the theory is too narrow on its own.
Two more evaluation points to have ready 两条备用评价
PointThe evidence (fill in)
Ethical problems in the supporting studyRosenthal & Jacobson allowed some students to receive less attention 得到更少关注 than others, interfering with their education; teachers were deceived 被欺骗.
False beliefs are hard to studySFP is by definition a false belief 错误信念; beliefs are studied with self-report 自我报告 measures, which rely on insight, self-disclosure and honesty 诚实.

§4 · Theory 2 — Social Learning from the Media 媒体社会学习ANSWERS → 课件 §7
Key terms 填空
Social learning theory（社会学习理论）: a social-cognitive 社会认知 theory that explains criminal behaviour as the result of modelling such behaviour from observing it via the media or watching other people 模仿观察到的行为.
Vicarious（替代的/间接的）: learning through the consequence of another person's behaviour 另一个人行为的后果.
Core claims — fill in 填空
  • An individual cannot learn offending behaviour without observing 观察 someone commit a crime — either directly (a peer) or indirectly (crime-related TV programmes 电视节目).
  • Reproduction is motivated by vicarious reinforcement（替代强化）: watching a criminal get away with an offence or reap rewards 逃脱惩罚或得到好处.
  • On TV, criminals are often glamorised（被美化）— violence committed by 'good guys 好人' — and victims' suffering is sanitised 被淡化, with punishment absent.
  • Media criminals act as role models（榜样）: often have power 权力, or are the same gender as us 与我们同性别的.
  • The theory also has a cognitive component: someone may choose not to commit a crime immediately 延迟模仿/不马上模仿 — and news showing negative consequences may discourage imitation.

§5 · SLT Evidence — From Bobo Dolls to Whole Communities 证据研究ANSWERS → 课件 §8
Bandura, Ross & Ross (1963) — the lab experiment
What they didChildren watched a video of adults behaving aggressively towards a Bobo doll 充气不倒翁玩偶.
Finding 1Children copied the aggression 模仿了攻击行为 they had watched.
Finding 2Children were equally likely to copy aggression from a cartoon 卡通 version where the adult was dressed as a black cat 猫.
LimitationThe long-term 长期 impact of witnessing aggression was not followed up 未追踪.
Tannis MacBeth Williams (1986) — the town that got TV
DesignA natural experiment 自然实验 on the introduction of television to a small community in British Columbia, Canada.
Sample16 young people, compared with two control 对照 groups in nearby communities.
FindingAfter only two 两年 years of TV, the children were twice as aggressive 攻击性翻倍 as the controls.
Williams' own cautionThe aggression was more likely due to the increased value placed on materialistic lifestyles 物质至上生活方式 than to TV violence.
Comstock & Paik (1994) — the meta-analysis
MethodA meta-analysis 元分析 of correlational 相关 studies.
FindingMany studies reported a positive 正 correlation between TV violence and aggression measures.
Key numberOverall correlation coefficient r = +0.19 — not particularly strong, and inflated by large sample sizes 样本量 making weak effects look significant.
The cooling ladder of evidence 证据的降温阶梯
Bandura = 实验室里小孩确实模仿（连卡通猫都模仿）→ Williams = 真实社区通了电视后攻击性翻倍（但她自己归因于物质主义价值观）→ Comstock & Paik = 大规模相关只有 （弱相关，还被大样本"吹"显著）。
记住 +0.19：写 "a weak correlation of only +0.19 (Comstock & Paik, 1994)" 立刻显得有据可依。

§6 · Evaluating Social Learning Theory 评价社会学习理论ANSWERS → 课件 §9
The three buckets of cold water 三盆冷水
Evaluation pointFill in the reasoning 补全推理
① 方向不明
The causation may run backwards		It could be that aggressive children seek out and prefer violent programmes 好斗的孩子主动选择暴力节目 — the direction of cause and effect is unknown 未知. The media may reflect pre-existing aggression rather than create it.
② 第三变量
Third variables explain criminality better		Social class (Flood-Page et al., 2000): children from lower socio-economic groups watch more delinquent 违法倾向 TV and are more likely to be delinquent. Also personality — sensation seeking 寻求刺激 (Slater et al., 2004) — and ASPD 反社会人格障碍. These could each produce both the TV habits and the behaviour.
③ 效应弱
The correlation is weak and inflated		r = leaves ~96% of the variance unexplained; correlations do not establish causality 因果, measure other variables, or indicate direction 方向.
Lab finding ≠ real lifeBandura's long-term impact was not followed up 未被追踪 — copying a bobo-doll punch in a lab is a long way from a criminal career 犯罪生涯.
The textbook's cautious conclusion — learn this wording 课本的谨慎结论
Despite a phenomenal amount of research using a range of methods, there is no convincing evidence that criminality results from observational learning, and no unequivocal evidence links exposure to violence with aggression — the relationship is neither simple nor straightforward.
8 分作文里，把这个 balanced verdict 放在结尾当 AO3 conclusion——敢于写「证据不支持强结论」比硬吹理论更能拿分。

§7 · Theory 3 — Antisocial Personality Disorder (ASPD) 反社会人格障碍ANSWERS → 课件 §10
Key terms 填空
Personality disorder（人格障碍）: an individual's way of thinking, feeling or relating to others 思考、感受或与人相处 differs significantly from normal — extremes of personality; requires a clinical diagnosis 临床诊断.
Comorbidity（共病）: the presence of more than one disorder in the same person 同一人身上有多种障碍.
The seven diagnostic characteristics — tick and translate 勾选并配中文

A diagnosis requires three or more. Fill in the Chinese gloss for each.

Characteristic中文

Deceit and manipulation — lying, aliases, conning for gain
Impulsivity / failing to plan ahead
Aggression and irritability — getting into fights
Reckless disregard for own or others' safety — reckless driving
Irresponsible behaviour — failing work or financial obligations

Diagnosis rules — fill in the numbers 诊断规则填空
Diagnosis requires 3 三 or more of the 7 characteristics. Not diagnosed until age 18 十八岁, but evidence of conduct issues before age 15 十五岁 must be seen. Prevalence in the general population: 1–4% (Lenzenweger 2007) — much higher in prison / substance-abuse subgroups, much more common in males 男性 (possibly under-diagnosed in females because the criteria emphasise aggression). To be clinically diagnosed, the characteristics must be （僵化的）, （持续的）and cause significant impairment 功能损害.

§8 · ASPD Evidence — Behaviour → Genes → Brain 三层证据链ANSWERS → 课件 §11
Layer 1 — Behaviour: Hodgins & Côté (1993)
Compared offenders with ASPD and without. Findings: offenders with ASPD had a significant history of offending during childhood 童年犯罪史 and more convictions in adulthood 成年后更多定罪 than offenders without ASPD → the disorder leads to a strong risk 风险 of offending.
Layer 2 — Genes: the twin and meta-analysis evidence
StudyDesign & sampleHeritability 遗传度
Torgersen et al. (2008)1386 Norwegian twin pairs, structured interview vs DSM-IV criteria38% of twin variation
Mason & Frick (1994) twin and 12 / 3 十二项双生子+三项收养研究 adoption studiesover 50% of variance
Ferguson (2010)38 studies (1996–2006), combined sample 96,918 participants56% of variance

三个数字像记比分一样记牢：38% → 50%+ → 56%。

Layer 3 — Brain: Yang & Raine (2009)
A meta-analysis 元分析 of 43 brain-scanning studies (structural and functional) found significantly reduced grey matter 灰质减少 and reduced function in the prefrontal cortex 前额叶皮层 of individuals displaying antisocial behaviour. The prefrontal cortex is known to moderate aggression 调节攻击性 — impairment there may explain antisocial behaviour. The impairment could be inherited 遗传的 or acquired through brain damage 脑损伤. Other differences found: the amygdala 杏仁核 and temporal lobe 颞叶.
Command word — 'justify' 指令词
'Justify' = rationalise a decision 用理由为判断辩护. The best way to justify whether a theory is credible is to offer evidence in the form of a research study 甩出一个具体研究 that supports it. 甩 Ferguson (2010) 或 Yang & Raine (2009) 就是满分动作。

§9 · Evaluating ASPD + Three Theories Compared 评价与对比ANSWERS → 课件 §12–§13
Evaluating ASPD — the key idea: risk, not destiny 关键观念：风险 ≠ 必然
PointFill in the reasoning 补全推理
Childhood roots (strength)Simonoff et al. (2004): 225 childhood twin pairs diagnosed with conduct disorder 品行障碍 — this predicted ASPD in adulthood 成年反社会人格障碍 10–25 years later. Implication: early identification and intervention 干预 programmes could prevent later offending.
Comorbidity (weakness)Diagnosis is problematic because of high comorbidity 共病 (anxiety, depression, substance misuse, ADHD). Environmental factors — adverse childhood, low socio-economic class, urban living — may produce protective strategies that resemble ASPD → some diagnoses may be misdiagnosed 误诊 (false positives).
Elevated risk, not inevitabilitySome people with ASPD never offend and live functional lives — risk varies with severity of symptoms, comorbid conditions (ADHD, substance misuse) and upbringing 教养. Treating ASPD as deterministic loses balance marks.
Genes load the gun, not the triggerEven the highest heritability figure (56%) leaves a substantial share to the environment 环境; and brain evidence is correlational 相关的 — reduced prefrontal function could be cause or result 结果 of lifestyle.
