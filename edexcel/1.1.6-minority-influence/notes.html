This booklet walks through Moscovici's (1976) theory of minority influence: why numbers are not the point, what behavioural style means (consistency + flexibility), how conversion gradually shifts majority opinion, the two supporting studies, and the evaluation you need for 8-mark questions.

The whole theory in one sentence 一句话总结
Because minorities have no power in numbers, their influence comes from their behavioural style — consistent conviction, supported by flexibility — which works through informational social influence, ends in internalisation, and produces social change through the slow process of conversion.

Key term 关键术语
Minority influence（少数派影响）

A form of social influence where people reject the established n of the majority and move towards the behaviour and o of a minority group.

Unpack the definition 拆定义

reject the established normsThe majority's current way of thinking is treated as "normal" — the minority steps from it

3 · The engine room: ISI → internalisation1.1.6 MINORITY INFLUENCE
The chain 链条

Minority influence travels through i social influence: the minority provides a viewpoint or argument the majority comes to take seriously. Because people accept the information as true, the result is internalisation — the d form of conformity.

Compare the endpoints 对比
ComplianceInternalisation
Public behaviourGo along
Private beliefDisagree
DepthShallow
Typical sourceNSI (majority pressure)
Quick check 快问快答

Why does minority influence end in internalisation rather than mere compliance?

中文理解 三个词一条链
少数派改变你靠的不是吓你（NSI），而是说服你（ISI）。你一旦觉得「有道理」，观点就住进脑子 = internalisation：人不在场你也照样这么认为。
连起来记：少数派 → ISI → internalisation。

4 · Style 1: Consistency1.1.6 MINORITY INFLUENCE
Key term 关键术语
Consistency（一致性）

The minority keeps saying and doing the s thing — the same viewpoint, maintained over t , by all members of the minority.

Two mechanisms 两个机制
MechanismWhat happens in people's heads
It signals certaintySame message in week one and week fifty → they must really b this
It creates doubtA consistent challenge makes the majority's own position look sh by comparison
The examinable sentence 必背句

Social change "could not have occurred without a c viewpoint being established and m , and minority individuals being d to their cause."

中文理解 重复本身就是说服力
第一次听「要回收」觉得多事；第一百次听到同样的话就开始想「是不是真有问题」。
consistency 的本质：把「一个奇怪的想法」重复成「一个值得认真对待的想法」。dedication（坚持投入）常与它一起考。

5 · Style 2: Flexibility1.1.6 MINORITY INFLUENCE
Key term 关键术语
Flexibility（灵活性）

Minorities are rarely effective unless they show f in their viewpoint and are not r in their beliefs.

Two styles, two fates 对比表
Minority styleHow the majority sees themResult
Rigid & uncompromisingPerceived as e Less likely to be b
Flexible & acceptingLess extreme and more r Viewpoint more likely to be considered; doubt cast over majority beliefs
Hold both at once 双招并用

Consistent about the c aim, flexible about how to get there — never moving the d , but willing to negotiate the road.

中文理解 像谈判，不像呐喊
死硬派：「必须养猫！不养绝食！」→ 被当偏激。
灵活派：「先寄养两周试一试，费用我出。」→ 目标没变，姿态有弹性，父母开始认真考虑。
秘诀：终点坚定，路线灵活。

7 · Conversion: the slow slide1.1.6 MINORITY INFLUENCE
Key term 关键术语
Conversion（转化）

The process where individuals in the majority group g come to a the minority viewpoint.

Order the stages 排序

Number these stages 1–4 in the order a typical majority member moves through them:

___"maybe they're right"
___"odd idea" — dismissed
___accepted — internalised
___"worth a thought"
Why the endpoint matters 终点

When the two lines meet, the belief itself has moved — so the change s even when the original campaigners are long gone.

中文理解 温水式的改变
conversion 的关键词是 gradual（渐进）。类比烧水：不是点火就开，而是一格一格升温，某刻突然沸腾——社会变化看似「突然」，其实是 minorities 一直在加热。

9 · Evidence 1: Moscovici et al. (1969)1.1.6 MINORITY INFLUENCE
The study in brief 研究概况

A s of experiments investigating minority influence on a colour p judgement task. Participants in a majority were swayed by incorrect judgements made by a m in the group.

Findings + design logic 设计逻辑
Design choiceWhy it matters
Colour judgement taskSimple perception task — answers easy to check, so shifts to wrong answers show real i at work
Minority gives wrong answersInfluence is measured d : agreeing with known-wrong answers = moved
Consistency is the headlineMinorities exerted influence particularly if they were c (full procedure: textbook pp. 59–62)
Headline 结论句

Minorities can exert some influence over majority opinion, particularly if the minority were c in their viewpoint.

中文理解 这个实验在证明什么
颜色判断本无争议——多数人若跟着给出明显错误的答案，唯一解释就是被影响了；而「坚持重复同一答案」的少数派影响最大 = consistency 的直接证据。

11 · Who listens to a minority?1.1.6 MINORITY INFLUENCE
The audience matters 听众

Minority influence can be affected by individual d and c — some people and cultures are more or less likely to be swayed.

Key term 关键术语
Social identity theory & the ingroup（社会认同理论与内群体）

We are more likely to identify with a minority if they form part of our i — the group we feel we b to.

Quick apply 快速应用

The same environmental campaign is presented by (a) students from your own school and (b) strangers from another city. Which audience is more likely to accept it, and which theory explains why?

中文理解 自己人说的才算
ingroup（内群体）＝「我们这一边的」。少数派让你觉得「他和我们是一伙的」，说服就成功一半。
这一条是 1.1.6 与 1.1.7 之间的桥：少数派说什么只是一半，听众是谁、在什么情境、来自什么文化是另一半。
