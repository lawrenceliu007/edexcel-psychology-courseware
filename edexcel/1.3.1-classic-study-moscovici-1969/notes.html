The whole study in one sentence 一句话总结
A consistent minority of two shifted the answers of a naive majority of four (8.42% vs a control baseline of 0.25%), and it shifted them deeply: even people who never said "green" aloud later judged colours privately towards green, so a consistent minority modifies the majority's perceptual code more than its social response, while an inconsistent minority collapses to 1.25%.

Fill the gap 填空

Minority influence is when a small group changes the views or perceptions of a group. Here it was tested with confederates against naïve participants.

A perceptual code is a way of or interpreting information. When a minority consistently presents a viewpoint, it can the majority's perceptual code.

Consistency means the minority changes its answer: the confederates said the blue slides were every single time.

The mirror-image idea 镜像想法

Majority influence tends to change what people (social response). A consistent minority tends to change what people (perceptual code). The direction of pressure and the depth of the change go together.
