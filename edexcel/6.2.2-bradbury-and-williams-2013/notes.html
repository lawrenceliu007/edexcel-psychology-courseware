The whole study in one sentence 一句话总结
Using secondary data from real hung-jury trials in four American states (2000–2001), Bradbury and Williams found that juries composed mostly of white jurors were more likely to convict Black defendants (p < .01, H1 supported), mostly-Hispanic juries showed the same direction but only at p ≤ 0.1 (H2 not supported), and mostly-Black juries were least likely to convict — so the diversity of the jury pool can bias the outcome of a trial.

Race and Juries — Why This Study Exists6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
The problem the study attacks 问题所在

Race and crime is heavily researched: are some races over-represented in crime statistics and prisons? Are some races treated differently at different stages of the justice system? Bradbury and Williams zoom in on one stage — how the racial make-up of the jury shapes the verdict.

The psychology behind it 心理学背景
In-group / out-group bias（内群体/外群体偏差）：我们天然偏袒「自己人」（in-group），对「外人」（out-group）更苛刻。陪审员也是人 —— 如果陪审团的种族构成一边倒，被告可能因为「属于外群体」而被判得更重。

The Aim and the Two Hypotheses6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
Aim 研究目的
To examine whether the racial make-up of a jury affects its decision-making.
Two hypotheses 两条假设
LabelThe hypothesis, word for word
H1Black defendants will be more likely to be convicted by juries composed of a higher number of white jurors.
H2Black defendants will be more likely to be convicted by juries composed of a higher number of Hispanic jurors.
怎么记住 H1 / H2
两条假设结构完全一样，只有陪审团的多数族群不同：H1 = white（白人），H2 = Hispanic（拉美裔）。背的时候只背「谁当多数 → Black 被告更容易被判罪」这个句式，再往里换族群。
Write them out 默写练习

H1: Black defendants will be more likely to be convicted by juries composed of a higher number of jurors.

H2: Black defendants will be more likely to be convicted by juries composed of a higher number of jurors.

Exam tip 考点提示

The Seven Control Variables6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
Why controls at all 为什么要控制变量

Imagine a jury convicts — was it because jurors were mostly white, or because the prosecution's case was simply overwhelming? To isolate the effect of race, the researchers measured seven control variables capturing how strong each case was, ensuring there was a sufficient case for the jury to decide on.

List them 默写七项
  1. Quantity of evidence
  2. Strength of the case
  3. Length of the trial
  4. Length of the jury deliberations
  5. Presence of written to the jury instructions
  6. Case type: or offences violence / property
  7. Lawyer type: or public / private
Exam tip 考点提示
七个控制变量是这研究最常见的考点之一。记忆分组：证据两条（数量、强度）＋ 时间两条（审判时长、商议时长）＋ 材料一条（书面指示）＋ 类型两条（罪行类型、律师类型）。考「name two control variables」时挑最好写的：quantity of evidence、strength of the case。

The p-value trap 显著性陷阱（考试最爱）
p < .01 = 巧合概率不到 1%，高度显著 → H1 成立。
p ≤ 0.1 = 巧合概率还有 10%，心理学惯例里不够显著 → H2 不能被支持。
Hispanic 组的方向与 H2 一致（也判得多），但统计上撑不住。标准写法：「The direction supported H2, but the effect was only significant at p ≤ 0.1, so H2 cannot be supported.」

The Conclusion 结论6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
What they concluded 结论原文要点
  • Diversity within the jury pool is likely to have an impact on the outcome of jury decision-making.
  • Black defendants are more likely to be convicted if the percentage of other-ethnicity jurors is higher than the percentage of Black jurors.
  • The selection process of jury members can therefore bias the outcome of the trial.
  • Black defendants were more likely to be convicted of substance crimes — raising the question of whether the criminal justice system is tougher on Black defendants for substance-related offences generally.

Evaluation · Strengths6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
Strength 1 — Real trials (ecological validity) 真实案件

The data represented characteristics of actual jury trials, as opposed to mock juries. This provides more valid data regarding actual jury decision-making than a laboratory experiment — the findings are more ecologically valid than other research methodologies.

Consequence: we can trust that the race effect happens outside the lab, in real courtrooms.

Strength 2 — Seven control variables 控制变量充分

The controls ensured they were truly investigating the racial composition of the jury rather than other factors (evidence quantity, crime type…) known to influence jury decisions. This ensures a level of validity — findings can be confidently applied to race and jury decision-making.

Strength 3 — Corroborating evidence 旁证

Anwar et al. (2012) found Black defendants were convicted 81% of the time by white jurors — supporting Bradbury and Williams, making the results more reliable.

Strength 4 — Objective coding 客观编码

Variables were coded (e.g. violent crime 0 = no, 1 = yes), producing quantitative data — more objective and less open to researchers' interpretation.

评价句式（背这个结构）
Point（真实数据）→ Because（不是模拟陪审团）→ Consequence（生态效度高，结论可信）。

Evaluation · Limitations6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
Limitation 1 — Cannot replicate 无法重复

Because real trial data was used, it is not possible to replicate the court case to see if the verdicts would be the same again.

Consequence: harder to establish reliability directly.

Limitation 2 — Cannot control everything 控不干净的变量

Other factors — jurors' personal experiences of crime, personal biases, pre-trial publicity — may have had an unknown impact. With real trials this was impossible to control despite the attempts.

Limitation 3 — Representativeness 代表性存疑
  • Real-trial data is rare — hard to be sure findings represent all trials; they may reflect an unknown uniqueness of these trials and jurors.
  • Only trials in America — other countries have different judicial processes, so may not represent defendants worldwide.
  • Only Black defendants — findings may not apply to defendants of other races; further research into other ethnic groups is required.
Limitation 4 — Coding oversimplifies 编码简化了现实

The DV coding may have overlooked that trials are complex: defendants can be charged with multiple crimes, convicted of some and acquitted of others — variations in the data would have been omitted.

一句话总结评价
真实案件带来生态效度，也带来失控（不可重复、混入偏见、代表性窄、编码丢失细节）。考试时成对写：优点+后果，缺点+后果。

Socially Sensitive Research & the Batson Rule6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
Socially sensitive research 社会敏感研究

This study focuses on defendant and juror race, so it is socially sensitive. Why?

  • It could potentially reinforce stereotypical (false) views about certain ethnicities being more likely to be involved in crime, or specific crimes such as substance-related offences.
  • These stereotypes may spread into the public domain, causing tension between ethnic groups or labelling groups as more criminal.
  • It could be used to claim certain ethnic groups are racist in their decision-making — with negative implications for the groups represented this way.
The application — and its legal limit 应用与限制

Application: the research can inform how juries are selected, ensuring a mix of ethnicities to promote fairness in the legal process.

The limit: in practice this may be difficult and also not permitted. Under the Batson rule in the USA, the court is not allowed to use a peremptory challenge to exclude a jury member because of their race.

key term
Batson rule：美国法庭规则 —— 律师不得以种族为由用「无条件回避权」（peremptory challenge）排除某位陪审员。也就是说，研究证明了「构成影响判决」，但法律禁止按种族挑陪审员。

Common Traps 常见错误辨析6.2.2 BRADBURY & WILLIAMS (2013)
Trap or truth? 判断对错
StatementVerdict
“Both hypotheses were supported by the results.”Trap. Only H1 (white jurors, p < .01). H2's direction matched but p ≤ 0.1 — not significant.
“The researchers ran new mock jury trials in a laboratory.”Trap. Secondary data — real trials that had already happened.
“The study shows juries should be selected by race to make trials fairer.”Trap. The Batson rule forbids using race to exclude jurors. The application is promoting a mix — not selecting by race.
“All juries were more likely to convict for violent crimes.”Trap. The reverse — less likely to convict for violent and property crimes than substance-related offences.
“Anwar et al. (2012) contradicted Bradbury and Williams.”Trap. Anwar supported them — 81% conviction by white jurors.
“Because real trial data was used, the study can be easily replicated.”Trap. Real cases cannot be replicated — that is a limitation.
为什么这些坑有效
每个错句都是真实知识的一半：方向对但显著性错、数据真但来源错、应用好但法律限制漏。背「Trap or truth」表是考前性价比最高的一页。

Page 16 — Practice B (model)

Strength: The data came from real trials rather than mock juries, so the findings have high ecological validity — we can trust the race effect exists in actual courtrooms. Limitation: real cases cannot be replicated, and factors such as jurors' personal biases or pre-trial publicity could not be controlled, so the conviction differences may not be due to race alone. Verdict: despite the limits, the corroborating evidence from Anwar et al. (2012) — 81% convictions by white jurors — makes the findings credible.
