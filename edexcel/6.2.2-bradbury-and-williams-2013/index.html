6.2.2 BRADBURY AND WILLIAMS (2013)
Diversity and Citizen Participation: The Effects of Race on Juror Decision-Making

Topic F · Criminological psychology Studies: contemporary Spec 6.2.2
Does the racial make-up of a jury change whether a Black defendant is convicted? Real trial data says it does.

What this section is really asking

  1. Real trials, not mock juries. Secondary data from hung-jury cases in four American states, 2000–2001.
  2. Two hypotheses: more white jurors → more convictions of Black defendants; more Hispanic jurors → the same.
  3. The finding: mostly-white juries were significantly more likely to convict (p < .01); the Hispanic effect was only marginal (p ≤ 0.1) — so H2 was not supported.
  4. Conclusion: jury selection can bias the outcome — which makes this a socially sensitive study with a real application.

1. Getting Started

Two juries. The same charge, the same strength of evidence, a Black defendant in both. One jury is mostly white; the other is mostly Black. Do they reach the same verdict?

🎯 Have a guess first No notes

If the racial make-up of a jury changes its verdicts, what does that imply about “a jury of your peers”?

The textbook opens this study with a thought experiment: smartly dressed defendants seem to receive more innocent verdicts than scruffily dressed ones. Appearance, race, accent — all of them are not the evidence, and all of them may still matter. Bradbury and Williams tested the biggest one: race.

中文理解：这一问为什么重要
陪审团制度的前提是「由你的同侪（peers）审判」。如果陪审团的种族构成能改变判决， 那么「抽签抽出来的陪审团」就可能系统性地偏向某一方——这不是个别陪审员的偏见， 而是制度层面的公平问题。

2. Race and Juries

Race and crime is extensively researched in criminological psychology. This includes whether certain races are over-represented in crime statistics and prisons, and — more importantly for this study — whether different races are treated differently at various stages of the criminal justice system, particularly how they are perceived by jurors.

The bias being tested: in-group / out-group

Bradbury and Williams investigated in-group / out-group bias using verdicts from criminal cases across several states of America.

中文理解：in-group / out-group 怎么用在这里
in-group（内群体）＝「和我们相似的人」；out-group（外群体）＝「和我们不同的人」。
心理学发现：人们更信任内群体、更容易对外群体成员做出负面判断。
放到陪审团里：如果陪审团里几乎没有与被告同种族的陪审员，被告就成了「外群体成员」， 判罪的可能性可能因此升高。6.1.5 讲过的这个概念，在这里变成了可以测量的假设。

3. The Aim and the Two Hypotheses

Aim： to examine whether the racial make-up of a jury affects its decision-making.
HypothesisWhat it predicts
H1Black defendants will be more likely to be convicted by juries composed of a higher number of white jurors.
H2Black defendants will be more likely to be convicted by juries composed of a higher number of Hispanic jurors.
中文理解：为什么要两个假设
如果只有白人陪审员多才判得多，那可能是白人 vs Black 的特殊关系。
再加一个 Hispanic 假设，就能看清楚：是「任何非 Black 陪审员都会这样」， 还是只有某一族群才这样。结果会告诉你这两个假设命运不同 —— 一个被支持，一个没有。

4. The Sample

Everything about this sample is unusual — in a good way. Learn each point as an evaluation card.

FeatureDetail
Type of dataSecondary data, collected from an earlier study of real trials.
Where and whenFour American states — Arizona, California, New York and Washington, DC — held between 2000 and 2001.
Which casesOnly cases with a “hung jury” — juries that could not agree on guilt.
What the data includedDemographic characteristics of the jury, jury selection and offence type.
Which defendantsOnly trials with Black defendants were examined, because they made up 60 per cent of the cases.
中文理解：为什么专挑 hung jury（僵局陪审团）
陪审团意见分裂到判不下来的案子，最有可能是「证据与偏见在拉锯」的案子—— 恰好是种族构成可能起作用的地方。整场一边倒的案子，谁当陪审员结论都一样，测不出效应。
代价是：这种案子在所有案件里很少见，所以后面评价里有一条「代表性」的批评就来自这里。

5. The Variables

VariableWhat it was
Dependent variableWhether or not a trial resulted in a conviction (coded 1 = conviction, 0 = no conviction / hung jury).
Independent variableThe racial make-up of the jury: percentage of Black, white and Hispanic jurors.
Seven control variablesQuantity of evidence · strength of the case · length of the trial · length of jury deliberations · presence of written instructions to the jury · case type (violent or property offences) · lawyer type (public or private).

Why seven control variables matter

They measured the strength of the prosecution case, to make sure there was a sufficient case for the jury to decide on. Without them, a mostly-white jury convicting more often might simply mean those juries faced stronger cases — not bias.

中文理解：控制变量是在「堵别的解释」
真实庭审数据不像实验室能随机分组，所以「white 陪审多的案子判得多」可能有别的解释： 证据更足？审得更久？律师更强？这七个变量就是把这几条路逐个堵上， 让剩下的解释只剩「陪审团种族构成」。这和 6.2.1 里「知道真实车速」是同一种思路。

6. Logistic Regression

logistic regression（逻辑回归）： a statistical analysis that examines the relationship between the dependent variable and the independent variables being investigated, and calculates the probability of them being related.

The researchers undertook complex statistical analysis, including a logistic regression. The nature of this test helped them identify which juror characteristics may influence decision-making, and the effect of the percentage of Black, white and Hispanic jurors on the likelihood of conviction.

中文理解：为什么用逻辑回归
因变量只有两个值（判罪 / 不判罪），不是连续数字。逻辑回归就是处理「是 / 否」结果的方法： 它输出的是「在其他条件相同时，陪审团构成每变一分，判罪的概率变多少」。
「其他条件相同」正是靠上一页那七个控制变量实现的。

7. The Results

Five findings. The first two are the hypotheses; the last two are the offence-type effects.

FindingStatistical status
Black defendants are less likely to be convicted by juries composed mostly of Black jurors (than Hispanic or white jurors).Supports the general effect
Juries comprising mostly white jurors are more likely to convict Black defendants.Significant at p < .01 — highly significant. H1 supported.
Juries composed of mostly Hispanic jurors were more likely to convict Black defendants.Only significant at p ≤ 0.1 — H2 cannot be supported.
Juries, regardless of composition, were less likely to convict a Black defendant for a violent crime than a substance-related offence.Clear effect
Juries were also less likely to convict for a property offence than a substance-related offence.Only significant at p ≤ 0.1
中文理解：p < .01 与 p ≤ 0.1 的差别
p < .01：由巧合造成这种差距的概率不到 1% —— 非常可信，H1 成立。
p ≤ 0.1：巧合的概率还有 10% —— 在心理学惯例里不够显著，所以 H2 不能被支持。
考试陷阱：Hispanic 组的方向和 H1 一样（也判得多），但统计上撑不住—— 所以答案要写「方向一致但未达显著」，不能写「H2 被证明」。

8. The Conclusion

What Bradbury and Williams concluded

Diversity within the jury pool is likely to have an impact on the outcome of jury decision-making. Black defendants are more likely to be convicted if the percentage of other-ethnicity jurors is higher than the percentage of Black jurors.

The selection process of jury members can therefore bias the outcome of the trial.

The substance-crime question

The finding that Black defendants were more likely to be convicted of substance crimes raises the issue of whether the criminal justice system is tougher on Black defendants for substance-related offences generally.

中文理解：结论为什么指向「陪审团的选拔」
陪审员是随机抽的，但如果抽出来的构成本身就能预测判决结果， 那么「随机」并不等于「公平」。结论落在制度层面：陪审团遴选过程（selection process） 可能系统性地影响审判结果。这就是为什么下一节说它是「社会敏感研究」。

9. Evaluating · Strengths

▶ 1. Real trial data — more valid than mock juries
  • The data represented characteristics of actual jury trials, as opposed to mock juries.
  • This provides more valid data regarding actual jury decision-making than an experiment in a laboratory or a mock jury study — the findings can be considered more ecologically valid.
▶ 2. Seven control variables
  • The control variables ensured they were truly investigating the racial composition of the jury rather than other factors, such as quantity of evidence and type of crime — factors known to influence jury decision-making.
  • This ensures a level of validity, and the findings can be confidently applied to the area of race and jury decision-making.
▶ 3. Supported by other research — reliability
  • As real trial data was used, it is not possible to replicate the court case to see if the verdicts would be the same again.
  • However, other research has shown similar findings: Anwar et al. (2012) found that Black defendants were convicted 81 per cent of the time by white jurors, which supports Bradbury and Williams — meaning their results could be considered more reliable.
▶ 4. Coding gives objective quantitative data
  • Coding procedures quantified the dependent and control variables (e.g. violent crime: 0 = no, 1 = yes; conviction: yes / no).
  • This provides quantitative data which is more objective and less open to interpretation by the researchers.
中文理解：四条 strength 的分工
真实庭审管生态效度；七个控制变量管内部效度；Anwar 81% 在不能复制的条件下补可靠性； 编码管客观性。四个角都占住了 —— 写评价时挑两条展开即可。

10. Evaluating · Limitations

1. Not everything could be controlled

It is not possible to control for all variables. Jurors' own personal experiences of crime, other personal biases, and any pre-trial publicity may have influenced the decision — these could not be controlled because the trials were real. They may have had an unknown impact.

2. Representativeness — three layers

  • Hung jury cases are rare, so it is difficult to be sure the findings represent all trials; the cases may reflect an unknown uniqueness of the trial and the jurors.
  • America only — other countries have different judicial processes, so it may not represent defendants across the world.
  • Black defendants only — the findings may not apply to cases where the defendant belongs to a different race. Further research into other ethnic groups is required before the effect can be said to hold across races.

3. The coding was too simple for complex trials

The dependent measure may have overlooked that many trials are more complex: defendants can be charged with multiple crimes, convicted of some and acquitted of others — perhaps the more serious offences. This would have been overlooked, so variations in the data may have been omitted.

中文理解：三条 limitation 的共同点
都是「真实数据」的代价：真实 → 不能控制一切、不能复制、案子特殊、编码只能简化。
对照记忆：mock jury 研究好控制但假；真实庭审真但难控制。 这个 trade-off（取舍）写进 8 分题就是一条完整的 AO3。

11. Wider Issues · Socially Sensitive Research

Because this study focuses on defendant and juror race, it is considered socially sensitive.

What could go wrong

  • It could potentially reinforce stereotypical (false) views about certain ethnicities being more likely to be involved in crime, or in specific crimes such as substance-related offences.
  • These stereotypes may extend into the public domain and cause tension between different ethnic groups, or be used to label groups as more likely to engage in criminal activity.
  • It could also be used to claim that certain ethnic groups are racist in their decision-making — which has negative implications for the populations represented in this way.
中文理解：「社会敏感」是什么意思
不是说研究做错了，而是研究结果可能被误用： 「白人陪审团判得多」这种结论，可能被拿去给某个族群贴标签、制造群体对立。
考试写法：承认风险 + 说明为什么仍然值得做（不研究它，司法系统里的偏见就永远不会被发现和纠正）。

12. Application — and the Batson Rule

What the research suggests

An important application: it can help inform the way juries are selected, to ensure a mix of different ethnicities is used to promote fairness in the legal process.

Why it is difficult in practice

In practice this may be more difficult and also not permitted. According to the Batson rule in the USA, the court is not allowed to use a peremptory challenge to exclude a jury member because of their race.

peremptory challenge（无因回避）： a lawyer's right to reject a potential juror without giving a reason. The Batson rule forbids using this right to exclude jurors on the basis of race.
中文理解：研究建议与法律规则的冲突
研究说「陪审团构成影响判决 → 那就选得多样化一点」；但法律说「不能按种族挑选或排除陪审员」。
这不是矛盾，而是底线不同：制度不能按种族操纵陪审团，但可以在遴选池层面保证来源多元。 考试里点出这个冲突，就是一条成熟的 AO3。

15. Exam Lab

As a contemporary study, this can be asked through its aim, sample, procedure, results, conclusions and evaluation. Two drills cover the most likely formats.

Drill 1 · describe the procedure. Learn it as four moves: secondary data → hung jury cases in four states, 2000–2001 → Black defendants only (60%) → logistic regression with seven control variables.

🎯 Try it4 marks

Describe the procedure of Bradbury and Williams (2013). Write four sentences, one per move, then compare.

Secondary data was collected from an earlier study of real trials in four American states — Arizona, California, New York and Washington, DC — held between 2000 and 2001. The cases chosen were ones with a hung jury, where juries could not agree on the verdict. Only trials with Black defendants were examined, because they made up 60 per cent of the cases. The dependent variable was whether the trial resulted in a conviction, the independent variable was the racial make-up of the jury, and the analysis — a logistic regression — included seven control variables measuring the strength of the prosecution case.

16. Key Terms

secondary data 二手数据
Data collected earlier by other researchers and re-analysed here — real trial records rather than a new experiment.
hung jury 僵局陪审团
A jury that could not agree on guilt or innocence — the case type studied, where evidence and bias may be in balance.
logistic regression 逻辑回归
A statistical analysis examining the relationship between the dependent and independent variables and calculating the probability of them being related.
in-group / out-group bias 内群体/外群体偏差
Favouring people similar to oneself and judging out-group members more harshly — the bias tested through jury composition.
control variable 控制变量
Seven factors measured so other explanations (evidence strength, trial length, lawyer type) could be ruled out.
statistical significance 统计显著性
p < .01 is highly significant; p ≤ 0.1 is too weak — which is why H2 was not supported.
ecological validity 生态效度
Real trial data reflects actual jury decision-making better than laboratory or mock jury studies.
socially sensitive research 社会敏感研究
Research whose findings could reinforce stereotypes or be misused — a key wider-issues debate for this study.
peremptory challenge 无因回避
A lawyer's right to reject a potential juror without giving a reason; the Batson rule forbids using it to exclude jurors by race.
coding 编码
Converting information into numbers (e.g. conviction = 1, none = 0), producing objective quantitative data.
representativeness 代表性
Whether the sample reflects the wider population — limited here by rare hung-jury cases, one country, and one defendant race.
Anwar et al. (2012) 支持性研究
Found Black defendants were convicted 81 per cent of the time by white jurors — supporting the reliability of Bradbury and Williams.
