5.3.2 METHODS: CROSS-CULTURAL RESEARCH, META-ANALYSIS & THE UNCRC
etic approach · effect size · children's rights · judging research quality · 跨文化、元分析与儿童权利公约

Topic E · Developmental psychology Methods Spec 5.3.2–5.3.5 📖 Textbook Book 2 · Ch.5 · pp.78–83

Learning Objectives 学习目标

  • Describe cross-cultural research and what it can (and cannot) tell you about nature and nurture.
  • Describe meta-analysis, including effect size, and weigh its strengths against the file drawer problem.
  • Describe the UNCRC (1989): participation versus protection rights, consent and assent when children take part in research.
  • Use the quality toolkit: reliability, validity, generalisability, credibility, objectivity — each with an example from this topic.
  • Answer exam questions on meta-analysis and on judging developmental research.

What this page is really asking

  1. Is attachment the same everywhere? Cross-cultural research answers this by comparing standardised data across countries — and the answer feeds straight into nature versus nurture.
  2. How do you combine many studies into one conclusion? Meta-analysis. Its headline product is the effect size: one number summarising the overall impact across every study included.
  3. What do children get that adult participants do not? The UNCRC (1989) — a legally binding agreement that reshaped how research with children is done.
  4. How do you judge whether a study is any good? Five tools: reliability, validity, generalisability, credibility, objectivity — each with a ready-made example from this topic.

追问猜完先别走：这两个问题没有选项，先自己想，再点开提示。

追问一：用一种文化的测试工具去测另一种文化的孩子，测出来的差异，是「文化差异」还是「工具不适配」？你怎么区分？

提示

很难区分，这正是 etic（客位）方法的软肋。日本孩子在陌生情境里分离时哭得更凶，可能是反抗型依恋，也可能只是日本婴儿很少经历分离、对这次经历反应更大。5.2.1 里 imposed etic（强加客位）的批评说的就是这个。所以要配上民族志（emic）才稳。

追问二：如果一项研究从来没人能复制，你还敢相信它的结论吗？

提示

要看为什么没法复制。如果是程序没写清楚、原始材料丢了（比如 Bowlby 的 44 小偷访谈没有录音），那结论确实该打问号。如果是伦理上不该复制（比如极端忽视的个案），那不能复制不等于不可信，但确实意味着它孤零零地站着，需要别的证据来补。

两个追问先放在心里。往下读，你会拿到两个研究工具、一条儿童权利公约，和五个判断研究好坏的尺子。

1. Cross-Cultural Research

Cross-cultural research（跨文化研究：在两个或以上文化收集同样的标准化数据，比较差异是否显著） answers one question: is a behaviour the same everywhere? For attachment, that usually means collecting quantitative data with a standardised questionnaire or structured observation in two or more cultures, then comparing.

The logic connects straight to nature versus nurture. If attachment looks similar across the world despite very different child-rearing styles, nature looks stronger. If it differs between cultures, nurture looks stronger. And there is a third point: without cross-cultural research, all evidence would come from one country and be ethnocentric（民族中心：只适用于一个国家、带着文化偏见）.

Cross-cultural research takes the etic（客位：强调文化共通性，常用一种文化设计的工具去测另一种文化） approach — the opposite of ethnography's emic stance. That is its weakness too: tools designed in one culture may not fit another, which is exactly the imposed etic problem you met in 5.2.1.

中文理解：跨文化研究 = 两个以上文化收集同样的标准化数据再比较。逻辑链：分布相似 → 支持天性；分布不同 → 支持教养；不做跨文化 → 全部证据都是民族中心的。它走 etic（客位）路线，和民族志的 emic（主位）相反；etic 的软肋正是 imposed etic（强加客位）批评：工具从一个文化搬到另一个文化，可能不适配。

2. Meta-Analysis

A meta-analysis（元分析：系统性挑选很多同题研究，把它们的原始数据合并成一个大数据集重新分析） combines existing studies into one big data set. The studies are chosen systematically, so they all attack the same question the same way. Because the data was collected by others, meta-analysis uses secondary data.

The headline output is the effect size（效应量：一个数字概括某变量在所有纳入研究中的整体影响大小） — one number that sums up how strong the effect is across every study included. When individual studies seem to contradict each other, or effects look tiny, meta-analysis reveals the overall trend.

  • Strength: statistical power. Big combined samples mean small real effects are more likely to be detected.
  • Weakness: the studies differ. Variables may be measured in different ways. De Wolff and Van IJzendoorn (1997) found effect sizes differed depending on how caregiver sensitivity was measured — so methodological differences, not real differences, may explain conflicting results.
  • Weakness: the file drawer. Meta-analyses typically include only published, peer-reviewed studies, and journals prefer positive findings. Null results get filed away. Some researchers deliberately add unpublished dissertations and conference papers to fix this — but then unreviewed data may be biased in other ways.

中文理解：元分析三句话：合并同题研究成一个大数据集（用二次数据）；产出效应量（一个数字概括整体影响）；强在统计力大（样本大，小效应也能测出来），弱在纳入的研究方法不一（de Wolff & Van IJzendoorn 1997 发现测敏感性方式不同、效应量就不同）加文件柜效应（阴性结果被雪藏）。收录未发表研究能补文件柜，但没同行评审又是新问题。

3. Ethics and the UNCRC (1989)

The BPS code requires respect, responsibility, integrity and competence from all psychologists, and its human-research code classifies under-16s as vulnerable participants. Children also occupy a marginalised position in many societies: as Samantha Punch notes, they may not realise that taking part in research is voluntary, or that they can withdraw.

In 1989 the United Nations adopted the UNCRC（儿童权利公约：联合国儿童权利公约，1989 年通过，对 193 个缔约国具有法律约束力，保障所有儿童在公民、政治、经济等各方面的权利） — a legally binding agreement covering every child regardless of race, religion or abilities. It reshaped research with children in three ways.

  • Participation versus protection. Children have the right to take part in decisions that affect them — researchers must treat them as equal partners and hear their voices. But they also have the right to be protected, and psychologists must balance the two: there is a real cost to not doing research, such as missing evidence for better education and children's services.
  • Consent and assent. Young children cannot fully judge the consequences of taking part, so parents give consent. But under the UNCRC children must still be fully involved: information must be age-appropriate, with simplified language, pictures or video. During the study, researchers watch for non-verbal signs of wanting to stop — looking away, no eye contact. This is lack of assent（不同意：孩子用行为表示不愿继续参加，研究者应当结束该环节）, and it means stop. Children rarely exercise the right to withdraw, because they are used to complying with adults even against their own wishes — which makes them vulnerable to exploitation.
  • Privacy and safety. Children's data is confidential, and researchers may need to explain this to parents. But safety overrides privacy: anything a child discloses that raises concern about their immediate safety must go to an appropriate expert.

One more practical note: children can also help plan research — designing and piloting child-friendly materials. And ethics committees and institutional review boards (IRBs) use the UNCRC itself to check that studies comply while still delivering their aims.

中文理解：UNCRC 三块。一，参与权对保护权：孩子有权参与影响自己的决定（研究里要当平等伙伴），但也要被保护（不做研究也有代价，比如教育服务缺证据）。二，同意与同意行为：16 岁以下由父母给 consent（知情同意），孩子自己给 assent（同意行为）；孩子出现不看人、走神这类信号就是收回 assent，研究者必须停。孩子很少主动行使退出权（习惯了服从大人），所以特别容易被利用。三，隐私与安全：数据保密，但安全问题必须上报专家。

4. Judging Research: The Toolkit

Five words do most of the work when you evaluate any study in this topic. Each one comes with a ready-made example you can use in the exam.

  • Reliability（信度：结果能不能被重复出来）: a study is reliable if its findings replicate, which requires a standardised procedure. In observations, check inter-observer reliability — Ainsworth and Bell (1970) found a correlation of 0.93 for proximity- and contact-seeking behaviours across 14 randomly selected infants. Test-retest reliability is the other check: same test twice, a short interval apart.
  • Validity（效度：测的是不是真想测的东西）: threats include demand characteristics, researcher effects, order effects（顺序效应：同一份问卷前后测两次，第二次的回答被第一次影响）, and social desirability bias（社会期许偏差：知道被观察时，做出更符合社会规范的行为，家长被评判育儿技能时尤其明显）. Song et al. (2010) used digitally altered voices and a photo task, which hurt ecological validity. Single-blind designs (O'Connor et al.) reduce researcher effects; wait-list controls and active control groups handle maturation and expectancy.
  • Generalisability（可推广性：样本结论能否推广到目标人群）: depends on sample size and, more importantly, sampling technique. Stratified samples generalise best but are rare; opportunity and volunteer samples are the worst, and unfortunately the most common.
  • Credibility（可信度：方法与分析是否值得信任）: strongest when researchers present findings cautiously — "the intervention was associated with improvement", not "caused it". Qualitative research gains credibility through triangulation and member checking（成员校验：把分析结果拿回给参加者看，看是否贴合他们的真实经历；对儿童较难实施）.
  • Objectivity and subjectivity. Objectivity means describing what you see, not interpreting what it means. Double-blind designs help. Reflexivity（反身性：研究者反思自己的经历和信念如何影响了数据解读，有些论文会公开写出这些反思） acknowledges the biases that remain. Ainsworth used two observers and checked inter-rater reliability, but her Ugandan observations may still have been filtered through her Eurocentric upbringing in Canada.

Back to the briefing: five tools, one per judgement. When the exam asks whether developmental research is scientific, generalisable or ethical, each of these gives you a paragraph — with a named study attached.

中文理解：五把评判尺，各配一个现成例子。信度：Ainsworth & Bell 0.93 的观察者间相关。效度：顺序效应（问卷前后测）、社会期许偏差（家长怕被评育儿）、生态效度（Song 2010 的假声音照片任务）；对策有单盲（O'Connor）、等待名单、主动对照组。可推广性：分层样本最好但少见，机会和自愿样本最差却最常用。可信度：措辞谨慎（associated with 而非 caused）、三角互证、成员校验（儿童难做）。客观性：双盲提客观，反身性承认残余偏差（Ainsworth 在乌干达的观察可能带着加拿大成长背景的滤镜）。

5. Exam Lab

Q1 · Explain 4 marks

Explain one strength and one weakness of meta-analyses in developmental psychology.

Strength: statistical power. Meta-analyses combine very large samples from diverse backgrounds, which increases the statistical power of the tests (1 mark). This makes it more likely that small but real effects are detected, and helps reveal overall trends when individual studies contradict each other (1 mark).

Weakness: the studies differ methodologically. The included studies may measure the same variable in different ways (1 mark). De Wolff and Van IJzendoorn (1997) found that effect sizes differed depending on how caregiver sensitivity was measured, so methodological differences between studies may be responsible for differences in their results, reducing the validity of the comparison (1 mark).

Marking guidance: 1 mark for identifying each point, 1 mark for explaining or exemplifying it. The file drawer effect is an acceptable alternative weakness.

Q2 · Discuss 8 marks

Assess whether research in developmental psychology can be considered generalisable.

Against generalisability. Opportunity and volunteer sampling dominate developmental research, and both are the least representative techniques. Many studies use single schools or communities: Ashdown and Bernard's sample came from one Melbourne school with low socioeconomic status and mostly second-language English speakers, so their findings may not transfer to other settings. Cross-cultural meta-analyses also have gaps — Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg included no studies from South America, Africa or Eastern Europe.

For generalisability. Large combined samples help: Cassibba et al. meta-analysed 67 studies covering 627 children and 2258 adults, and Van IJzendoorn and Kroonenberg pooled 1990 mother–infant pairs across eight countries. Longitudinal studies like Rutter's Romanian adoptees have been reassessed repeatedly since the 1990s, so findings rest on more than one data point.

Judgement. Generalisability in this topic is usually moderate rather than strong: the designs are sound, but the sampling is often convenient rather than planned. Meta-analyses partially rescue generalisability by pooling samples, though they inherit whatever biases the included studies carry.

Marking guidance: AO1 accurate knowledge of sampling and specific studies; AO3 named examples on both sides plus a justified conclusion about the extent of generalisability.

7. Key Terms

cross-cultural research
跨文化研究：两个以上文化收集标准化数据并比较，检验行为是否普遍
etic
客位：强调文化共通性，常用一种文化的工具测另一种文化
meta-analysis
元分析：系统性挑选同题研究，把原始数据合并成一个大数据集重新分析（用二次数据）
effect size
效应量：概括某变量在所有纳入研究中整体影响大小的一个数字
file drawer effect
文件柜效应：阴性结果不被发表而被雪藏，让已发表文献显得更支持某结论
UNCRC (1989)
儿童权利公约：1989 年通过的联合国公约，对 193 个缔约国有法律约束力
assent / consent
同意行为／知情同意：孩子自己的参与意愿／监护人给出的法律许可，两者都要
lack of assent
不同意信号：孩子用行为（移开视线、不看人）表示不愿继续，研究者应当结束环节
IRB
机构审查委员会：用 UNCRC 等标准审查研究是否合规的伦理委员会
reliability
信度：结果能否重复；观察研究用观察者间相关检验（Ainsworth & Bell 0.93）
order effects
顺序效应：同一问卷前后测两次，第二次的回答被第一次影响
social desirability bias
社会期许偏差：知道被观察时做出更符合社会规范的行为
credibility / member checking
可信度／成员校验：方法与解释是否可信／把分析拿回给参加者核对是否贴合其经历
reflexivity
反身性：研究者反思自身经历与信念如何影响了数据解读
