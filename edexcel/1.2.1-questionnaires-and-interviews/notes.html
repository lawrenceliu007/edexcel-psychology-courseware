The whole topic in one sentence 一句话总结
Surveys gather self-report data through questionnaires and interviews; every question yields quantitative or qualitative data; a good instrument survives checks of reliability (consistent) and validity (measuring what it intends), avoids social desirability and response bias, and controls researcher effects — because what people say about themselves is not automatically what they do.

Social desirability in full 社会赞许性

Social desirability occurs when a respondent does not give a answer, but one which depicts them in a more light — an answer that fits prevailing norms.

It is particularly problematic for research into socially issues, or attitudes that go social norms.

Key term: privacy 隐私

Privacy: participants should not be asked personal questions that they may find , and the researcher must not obtain personal data that a participant would not disclose.

中文理解 坑在哪、怎么填
坑一在人（自我美化）→ 用反向陈述检测；

13 · Alternative hypotheses1.2.1 QUESTIONNAIRES & INTERVIEWS
What a hypothesis must be 三个要求

An alternative hypothesis is a about what is likely to occur. It should contain the under investigation and be a clear, and statement at the beginning of a study.

Directional or not? 方向问题

If previous research is limited or findings are , the prediction may only state that a or might be found — without saying which it takes.

Write two hypotheses 写两条

(a) Directional: students who score higher on a conformity scale report more agreement with the majority.

(b) Non-directional: a difference in self-reported conformity between year groups. (Say only that a difference exists.)

中文理解 备择假设 = 郑重的预测
开工前把「我觉得会发生什么」写成明确、可检验、精确的一句话，并写明变量。
有把握 → 指方向；研究少/结论乱 → 只说「会有差异/关系」。
检验：别人读完能照着做研究去验证对错，才叫 testable。
