Pilot Study, Controls, Ethics + Presenting the Quantitative Data 定量数据4.4 PRACTICAL · BEFORE THE MAIN RUN
Pilot study 试点研究

Run before the main observation to address potential problems and test your observational criteria. Questions it answers: where is the best to observe (sit near the front for driver interactions) position; how long the journey needs to be for enough data; whether you can observe everyone at busy times. Key point: a pilot is not used to test whether the investigation is or valid; reliable — it only checks apparatus and timings work correctly. It is also a chance to ask participants whether they felt uncomfortable or suspected anything.

Controls 控制

Recruiting a second observer helps, e.g. one watches passengers, one watches male; female. You must then consider reliability: the degree of agreement between two or more observers inter-rater, ensuring consistency and controlling for bias observer. The observation is likely (participants unaware) covert; check whether passengers notice your note-taking and become suspicious — that would pose a variable confounding.

Ethical considerations 伦理

Observations in places are considered acceptable because you would expect to be observed in day-to-day interactions there public. You collect no names or personal data, only gender and observed behaviour, so no ethical guidelines are contravened. You might gain consent after the observation is complete, though this may not be practical.

中文理解：pilot 的边界最容易考
一句话卡死：pilot 查流程行不行（器材、时长、站位），查信度效度是主实验的事。公共场合观察伦理过关的理由也记两条：本来就默认会被看见；只记性别和行为，不碰个人信息。

Running the Chi-Squared Test 卡方实操4.4 PRACTICAL · WORKED CALCULATION
Why chi-squared 为什么选卡方

The study predicts a , uses unrelated data / groups, and the data is level difference; independent; nominal. The full nine-step method is in 4.2; here is the compressed three-step version used in the practical.

Step 1: Expected values 期望值

Formula: E = row total × column total ÷ total overall

Step 3: df, critical value, decision 自由度与判定

df = ( − 1) × ( − 1) = rows; columns; 1. For a non-directional/two-tailed hypothesis, df = 1, p < 0.05, the critical value is 3.84. Observed critical value → significant, reject the hypothesis 6.07 ≥ 3.84; null. Less than a five per cent probability the difference is due to chance. Direction found by examining the cells: were more likely to thank the bus driver females.

Chi-squared has a column, not an 'n' column, because nominal data gives no individual scores df.

Evaluation: four dimensions 四个维度
DimensionPoints
Validity 效度Naturalistic setting in real life suggests a degree of validity. 'Thank you' is a relatively measure (no judgement involved) objective; but qualitative notes like 'small talk' involve more interpretation. Improvement: validation — get feedback from participants (e.g. interview regular bus users) to check your interpretation respondent
Reliability 信度Can another observer repeat the study and get similar findings? reliability: agreement between two or more observers; lack of consensus means the behavioural categories may need to be inter-rater; revised. –retest: repeat the observation on the same route later (a day, week or month) and compare test
Generalisability 概化Time of day affects the achieved sample; ages need consideration — an equal distribution minimises bias. More retired passengers may raise polite interactions (less time-pressured, more traditional values of respect)
Credibility 可信度Depends on scientific principles: , measurable phenomena replicability. Weigh overall strengths (naturalistic = valid measurement) against weaknesses (fixed time of day limits generalisation; repeated observations may be needed)
Triangulation 三角互证

: using more than one approach to investigate a research question Triangulation. A single method suffers from that method's own limits; checking results by other methods gives increased in the findings if similar results emerge confidence. Example: a with bus passengers on politeness, to see if their views match the behaviour observed questionnaire.

效度看情境真不真、类目客观不客观；信度看换个观察员换个日子还能不能复制；概化看时段和年龄偏不偏；可信度看符不符合科学原则。respondent validation 和 triangulation 是两个加分招：一个问当事人认不认，一个换方法对不对得上。
