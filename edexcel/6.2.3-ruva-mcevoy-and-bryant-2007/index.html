6.2.3 RUVA, MCEVOY AND BRYANT (2007)
Pre-trial publicity and source memory · 审前报道与来源记忆

Topic F · Criminological psychology Studies: contemporary Spec 6.2.3

What this section is really asking

  1. Two phases, one week apart. Students read either negative pre-trial publicity or unrelated articles, then watched a 30-minute trial video.
  2. Then some deliberated, some did not — 276 jurors talked it through in groups, 282 gave individual verdicts without discussion.
  3. The publicity did two things: it pushed verdicts towards guilty (73% vs 41% when jurors deliberated) and it blurred where each fact came from.
  4. Conclusion: pre-trial publicity contaminates memory itself, so jurors cannot simply be told to ignore it.

1. Getting Started

You are about to sit on a jury. But last week you read the news:

“LOCAL MAN ARRESTED IN WIFE'S SHOOTING DEATH. Neighbours say he had a temper. She was afraid of guns.”

The judge tells you: “Decide only on the evidence presented in this courtroom.”

Be honest with yourself — can you really unread that headline?

🎯 Your gut answerNo wrong answer

中文理解：这个实验在问什么
法官说「只看法庭证据」很容易；但新闻读过的内容已经存进了你的记忆。这个研究问的正是： 读过的新闻会不会被你误记成「法庭上看到的证据」？这就是source monitoring error（来源监测错误）。

2. Pre-trial Publicity — The Problem

Every defendant has the right to a fair trial and an impartial jury. But in today's world, news is everywhere. Research shows that media coverage before a trial can bias jurors against the defendant.

pre-trial publicity（审前报道）
News coverage about a case published before the trial begins — it may contain information that never appears in court.

What the justice system tried — and why it fails

StrategyDoes it work?
Voir dire — questioning prospective jurorsShown to be ineffective
Judge's instruction to ignore media coverageShown to be ineffective
Change of venue — move the trial elsewhereNot tested experimentally; rarely done; no right to request it
Bench trial — trial by judge only, no juryNot tested experimentally; largely prohibitive

Some countries have abolished juries altogether (Singapore, South Africa, India); others use juries only for selected offences. So: can we measure how much pre-trial publicity actually biases a verdict?

How a headline turns into a guilty verdict

The textbook names two routes by which prejudicial media reaches a juror's decision. Ruva et al. set out to test the second one.

Route 1 · Confirmation bias（确认偏误）

If a juror already holds a negative impression of the defendant, they selectively attend to negative evidence during the trial and let positive evidence pass by. The impression was formed before the trial started, but it decides what counts as evidence once the trial begins.

The verdict shifts without any memory error. Nothing is misremembered; attention is simply filtered.

Route 2 · Source monitoring error（来源监测错误）

The juror cannot tell apart information from the media and information from the trial. A fact read in a newspaper becomes "something the witness said in court".

The verdict shifts because memory itself is contaminated. This route is what the source monitoring test is built to catch.

confirmation bias（确认偏误）
Only paying attention to information which is consistent with previously formed impressions.
中文理解：两条路线，两种危害
确认偏误是「戴着有色眼镜看证据」：证据都在，只是你只挑对自己印象有利的看。记忆没坏，注意力被筛选了。
来源监测错误是「证据被调包」：法庭上根本没有的东西，被你记成「庭上听到的」。记忆坏了。
前一条路线靠「认真听证据」也许能救；后一条路线救不了，因为错在下游的记忆里，不在上游的注意力。这正是这个研究要证明的点。

3. The Aim

To test whether:

  • exposure to negative pre-trial publicity biases jurors toward guilty verdicts, and
  • whether deliberating in a group (collaborating jurors) versus thinking alone (nominal jurors) changes those judgments — including source monitoring errors.
中文理解：collaborating vs nominal
Collaborating jurors（协作陪审员）：真正坐在一起讨论并给出统一裁决的小组。
Nominal jurors（名义陪审员）：只是各自单独判断，研究时把个人答案加总，中间没有讨论。
对比这两者，就能看出「讨论」本身是纠错还是传染。

6. Phase One — Planting the News

Sessions of 16 or fewer participants. They were told a cover story: this is a two-phase study about “the stability of emotional reactions to different sources of information” — so nobody guessed it was about jury decisions.

Everyone filled in a personality test and a questionnaire (age, sex, ethnicity), then received a packet of articles:

Exposed groupsNon-exposed group
Nine edited news articles about the NJ v Bias murder investigation and trial (from the Morning Call online newspaper) — including facts never presented at trialNine articles about an unrelated crime — a woman accused of embezzling child support funds

Facts planted only in the articles (never in the trial)

  • Daniel Bias had a bad temper
  • Lise Bias's body was found in the doorway of the bedroom
  • Lise did not know how to use guns and disliked them
  • Daniel had wanted Lise to quit her job and start a family

Both sets of articles were matched for length — about ten pages in total. Afterwards: 15 minutes to recall as much as possible and rate their emotional response.

中文理解：为什么文章要与庭审「不重合」？
文章里故意放了一些法庭上不会出现的信息（坏脾气、不会用枪……）。如果被试在裁决时「想起」这些信息，它们只可能来自新闻。这样就能用来源监测测验精确地抓到「把新闻记成证据」的瞬间。

7. Phase Two — The Trial

Four to seven days later, participants returned and watched a 30-minute video of a real trial: NJ v Bias — a man accused of murdering his wife, pleading not guilty (claiming accidental self-shooting). His guilt was not clear.

Just like a real trial, they were told to ignore anything they had read before and judge only on the trial evidence.

Facts presented only in the trial（只在庭审中出现）

  • Detective John Flynn failed to do any fingerprint testing on the weapon
  • Daniel had his hand on his wife's hand when the weapon went off
  • Lise was standing by the mirror when the weapon went off
  • That day they had argued about a piece of jewellery

After the video, each participant gave:

MeasureScale
Verdictguilty / not guilty
Rating of guilt1–7 (1 = certain not guilty, 7 = certain guilty)
Sentence30–45 years (New Jersey guidelines)

These same three measures were taken three times, which is why the results come in three rounds:

Time pointWhen it was measured
Pre-deliberationStraight after the video, before any discussion or recall
Post-deliberationAfter collaborators deliberated, or nominal jurors recalled the trial alone
Post-source monitoringAfter the source monitoring test, as a final individual verdict
中文理解：为什么要测三次
只测一次，只能说明「读了新闻的人判得更重」。测三次，才能看出讨论有没有救回来：如果讨论后判罪率下降，说明陪审团制度本身有纠错能力。这个设计是为了回答「陪审团还值不值得保留」，不只是证明媒体有影响。

8. Deliberate or Recall

Collaborating jurorsNominal jurors
30 minutes to deliberate and reach a unanimous group verdict (guilty / not guilty / hung)Taken to a different room. 15 minutes recalling trial facts, 10 minutes indicating which facts most influenced their verdict; then the same verdict form (no hung option)
source monitoring test（来源监测测验）
A test designed to see whether a person can accurately attribute the original source of information — here: did each fact come from the articles, the trial, or both? Participants also rated their confidence in each answer.

Finally everyone gave an individual verdict, guilt rating and sentence once more, rated the defendant's credibility (“How honest did the defendant appear to be?”), and answered a debrief questionnaire (did you believe the cover story?).

10. Results — Source Errors and Credibility

Source monitoring errors 来源监测错误

  • Jurors exposed to negative pre-trial publicity made more source monitoring errors than those who read unrelated articles.
  • Exposed jurors misattributed article-only information to the trial, or to both — and were confident doing it.
  • Collaborating jurors were more accurate than nominal jurors — and more confident in both accurate and inaccurate judgments for trial items.
中文理解：什么叫「把新闻记成证据」
「他脾气坏」这条信息法庭上从未出现。但暴露组的陪审员在测验里说：「这是法庭上听到的」。不仅记错了，还很有信心。新闻内容就这样伪装成证据，悄悄推动了有罪裁决。
MeasureExposedNon-exposed
Defendant credibility（可信度均值）40.83 — less credible49.57
Believed the cover story83%85%
Suspected the real aim35%18%

The mediation chain 中介链条

Negative pre-trial publicity → source monitoring errors → higher guilt ratings → defendant seen as less credible. The effect of publicity on guilt was mediated by source monitoring errors.

One more twist: verdicts tended to become more lenient after deliberation — possibly because deliberation heightened the “reasonable doubt” standard, or because convicting feels more serious than acquitting.

11. Conclusions

  • Negative pre-trial publicity can bias a jury toward believing a defendant is more guilty — in guilty verdicts, guilt ratings and sentence length — and toward seeing the defendant as less credible.
  • Deliberation produced a shift in leniency.
  • Collaborating jurors were more accurate in source monitoring — there is a benefit in using juries: jurors catch each other's memory errors.
  • But exposure to publicity led jurors to misattribute media information to trial evidence — media coverage may alter the memory of a trial, creating a negative impression of the defendant.
中文理解：三个结论一句话版
负面审前报道推高判罪。集体讨论能让陪审员互相纠错，讨论后还会更宽容。但一旦读新闻的人把新闻误存为证据，讨论也救不回来，因为错的是记忆本身。

12. Evaluating · Strengths

Strength 1 · A real trial（真实庭审）

Mock jury research often shows participants a fictional trial summarised in a document. Ruva's participants watched real court proceedings of a real murder case, so the study has greater ecological validity than much research in this area.

中文：别人给被试一份假案卷摘要，这里让被试看真实凶杀案的庭审录像，更接近陪审员真正面对的材料。

Strength 2 · Standardised procedures（标准化流程）

Articles were matched for length (nine each, ten pages), everyone watched the same 30-minute video, timing was fixed (15 / 30 / 10 minutes), and participants were randomly allocated to collaborating or nominal conditions. The comparison between groups is therefore fair, and the study can be replicated.

中文：两组读的文章长度一样，看的视频一样，分配随机，所以判罪率的差别只能归因于「读没读负面新闻」。

Strength 3 · Objective measures（客观测量）

Guilt was measured as a guilty / not guilty verdict, a rating on a 1–7 scale, and a sentence in years. None of these depend on the researcher's opinion, so the data collected can be treated as scientific.

中文：判罪与否、1–7 评分、判多少年，都是硬指标，不是研究者「觉得」被试有多怀疑。这是量化数据能写进 AO3 的底气。

Strength 4 · Deception handled carefully（欺骗处理得当）

The cover story was necessary, otherwise participants would guess the aim. Most believed it (83% exposed vs 85% non-exposed). Asked directly, 35% of exposed jurors suspected the real aim (vs 18% non-exposed), but analysis showed suspicious participants did not respond differently.

中文：用了欺骗，但大多数人信了；就算起疑，作答也没有差别。说明欺骗没有污染数据，伦理和效度两头都有交代。

中文理解：这四条怎么在 8 分题里用
挑一条写透，比四条都写一遍分更高。写法是「优势 + 这优势让结论变得可信在哪」：例如真实庭审 → 材料接近现实 → 结论能外推到真实陪审团。只写「生态效度高」不加解释，拿不到高分。

13. Evaluating · Limitations

Limitation 1 · Only 30 minutes to deliberate（讨论时间太短）

Real jurors may deliberate as long as reasonably needed. The 30-minute limit means collaborating jurors' verdicts may not reflect verdicts given sufficient time.

中文：真实陪审团想讨论多久就讨论多久，这里只给 30 分钟，讨论组给出的裁决未必是充分讨论后的结果。

Limitation 2 · Not a real jury experience（不是真正的陪审体验）

Participants never received a summons, went through juror selection, were sworn in, or heard the judge's instructions. They never sat in a real courtroom or decided the fate of a real defendant. Knowing a verdict would actually convict someone might have made them more lenient.

中文：没有传票，没有遴选，没有宣誓，也没有法官训示，更不用真的决定一个人的命运。知道自己的一票会把人送进监狱，被试可能会更宽容。

Limitation 3 · Groups of four to six, not twelve（小组人数不够）

Real juries have 12 members. In smaller groups, individual dissenters get less support from others, so biased views can go unchallenged. This may explain why collaborating jurors held a more negative impression of the defendant than nominal jurors.

中文：人少，少数意见更容易被压住，偏见没人挑战。这可能就是讨论组对被告印象更差的原因。但注意课本同时说：协作对裁决本身没有显著影响。

Limitation 4 · Students in one US state（样本不具代表性）

The sample was university students in Florida, USA, the majority white. Real jurors are selected randomly from the electoral roll, so this sample does not represent them, nor jurors from other states or other countries. We cannot assume all jurors would be biased by negative media coverage.

中文：被试是佛罗里达的大学生、以白人为主；真实陪审员从选民册随机抽取。样本对不上，结论不能推广到所有陪审团。

Limitation 5 · Underestimates real-life publicity（低估真实影响）

Participants were told to dismiss specific articles they had read only days before, so they could discriminate better than a real juror would. Real jurors absorb coverage over weeks or months, from many sources, and would confuse the two more easily. Real effects could be larger than those measured.

中文：被试读的是几篇指定的文章，几天后就被提醒丢掉。真实陪审员被媒体泡上几周几个月，来源分得更不清。所以这里测出的是下限，真实危害只会更大。

social loafing（社会惰化）
In large groups there is a tendency for some members to avoid participating.
中文理解：12 人陪审团到底该不该保留
课本用这个研究翻出了一个反常识的结论。小群体（4 到 6 人）的问题是个别异议得不到支持，偏见无人挑战。但讨论并没有显著改变裁决，只让被告印象更差。
于是问题反过来问：既然大群体也会 social loafing（有人搭便车不参与），而挑 12 个陪审员成本高、对陪审员的经济和家庭负担也重，那么 12 人陪审团的必要性就需要重新论证。
这一层是这道评价题能拿高分的地方：从一个方法的缺陷，推到制度层面的追问。

14. Wider Issues — Applying the Findings

The research has significant practical application: if jury decisions are biased by negative pre-trial publicity, measures must go beyond instructing jurors to ignore media (which we know fails).

For high-profile cases（高关注度案件）

  • Move the trial to a location with less publicity about the defendant（change of venue）
  • Or use a bench trial — the judge decides alone, no jury

Either would reduce the chance of an unfair trial.

中文理解：为什么这条应用「考试好写」
它直接回应了第 2 节的失效清单：口头指令没用 → 所以要物理隔离（换地方）或取消陪审团（bench trial）。研究结论与对策一一对应，前后咬得上。

16. Exam Lab

AO1 (4) + AO3 (4) = 8 marks Generic answers score 0

✍️ Question 1 — DescribeAO1

Describe the procedure of Ruva, McEvoy and Bryant (2007). (4)

Frame your answer: sample → two phases → measures → deliberation/recall → source monitoring test.

558 university students (mean age 20.6) were told a cover story about emotional reactions. In phase one they read either nine articles of negative pre-trial publicity about the NJ v Bias murder case or nine unrelated crime articles, matched for length, and recalled them for 15 minutes. Four to seven days later they watched a 30-minute video of the real trial and gave a verdict, a 1–7 guilt rating and a 30–45-year sentence. Collaborating jurors then deliberated for 30 minutes to reach a unanimous verdict, while nominal jurors recalled trial facts individually. Finally all completed a source monitoring test attributing each fact to the articles, the trial, or both.

✍️ Question 2 — EvaluateAO3

Evaluate the study by Ruva, McEvoy and Bryant (2007). (4)

Frame your answer: one strength + consequence, one limitation + consequence — all tied to this study.

One strength is that participants watched a real murder trial rather than a fictional summary, so the findings have greater ecological validity than typical mock jury research. However, collaborating jurors only deliberated for 30 minutes in groups of four to six, unlike real juries of twelve that may deliberate for as long as needed, so the verdicts may not reflect what well-resourced deliberation would produce. The study may also underestimate the bias: real jurors absorb media coverage for weeks or months and would discriminate worse between coverage and trial evidence, so real-world effects could be even stronger.

✍️ Question 3 — ApplicationAO2

Suggest how the justice system could reduce the bias found by Ruva et al. (4)

Since simply instructing jurors to ignore media coverage has been shown to be ineffective, high-profile cases could be moved to a location with less pre-trial publicity about the defendant, or tried as a bench trial where the judge decides alone. Either measure physically separates the decision-makers from the biased information, reducing the source monitoring errors that Ruva et al. showed mediate the link between publicity and guilty verdicts.

17. Key Terms

pre-trial publicity
News coverage of a case before the trial; may contain information never presented in court.
source monitoring test
A test of whether a person can accurately attribute the original source of information.
collaborating jurors
Group members who deliberate together and give one unanimous verdict.
nominal jurors
Individuals judging alone; their answers are pooled without discussion.
bench trial
Trial by judge only — no jury.
voir dire
Questioning of prospective jurors to screen out bias; shown to be ineffective here.
hung jury
A jury that cannot agree on a verdict.
cover story
A false explanation of the study's aim, used here to hide the jury focus.
