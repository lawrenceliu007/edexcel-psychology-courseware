The whole study in one sentence 一句话总结
Of 558 students, those who read negative pre-trial publicity about a real murder trial voted guilty far more often (73% vs 41% collaborative; 65% vs 37% nominal), made more source monitoring errors — mistaking article-only facts for trial evidence, confidently — and rated the defendant as less credible (40.83 vs 49.57), while group deliberation shifted verdicts toward leniency and helped catch memory errors.

Pre-trial Publicity — The Problem6.2.3 RUVA, MCEVOY & BRYANT (2007)
The right at stake 权利问题

Every defendant has the right to a fair trial and to be judged by an impartial jury and/or judge. But media coverage before the trial can be a source of bias.

Key term 关键术语
pre-trial publicity（审前报道）
News coverage about a case published before the trial — which may include information never presented in court.
What the justice system tried — and the verdict on each 应对措施
StrategyShown to work?
Voir dire（陪审员资格审查） ineffective
Judge's instruction to ignore media ineffective
Change of venue（更换审判地点）Not tested experimentally; done; no right to request rarely
Bench trial（法官审，无陪审团）Not tested experimentally; largely prohibitive

Extra context: some countries have abolished juries altogether (Singapore, South Africa, India); others use juries only for a select number of offences.

为什么口头指令没用（先想再翻答案）
因为新闻内容已经存进记忆。「不要想」不能删除记忆 —— 只能靠来源监测（想起来时分辨「这是哪里来的」），而来源监测恰恰会出错。这正是本研究的核心机制。

The Aim and the Two Juror Types6.2.3 RUVA, MCEVOY & BRYANT (2007)
Aim 研究目的

Test whether (1) exposure to negative pre-trial publicity biases jurors toward guilty verdicts, and (2) whether deliberating in a group versus thinking alone changes the judgments — including source monitoring errors.

Two key terms 两个关键术语
TermMeaning
Collaborating jurors 协作陪审员Deliberate together for 30 minutes and give one unanimous group verdict (guilty / not guilty / hung)
Nominal jurors 名义陪审员Judge alone; individual verdicts are pooled — no discussion
为什么这个对比重要
如果讨论组更准 → 「陪审团制度」本身有价值（成员互相纠错）；如果讨论组更糟 → 讨论可能传染偏见。实验结果支持前者，但有个前提 —— 详见 p.11。
Warm-up 热身

1. Jurors who deliberate together and must reach one verdict are called jurors.

2. Jurors who judge individually with no discussion are called jurors.

3. The test that checks whether people can correctly attribute where information came from is the test.

Conclusions 结论6.2.3 RUVA, MCEVOY & BRYANT (2007)
Four conclusions, one line each
  1. Negative pre-trial publicity can bias a jury toward guilt — in verdicts, guilt ratings and sentence length — and toward seeing the defendant as less credible.
  2. Deliberation produced a shift toward leniency.
  3. Collaborating jurors were more in source monitoring — a benefit of juries: members catch each other's errors.
  4. But exposed jurors media information as trial evidence — media coverage may alter the memory of a trial, and no amount of discussion fixes a corrupted memory.
三个结论一句话版（中文）
① 负面审前报道推高判罪。② 集体讨论能纠错、也更宽容。③ 一旦新闻被误存为证据，讨论也救不回来 —— 错的是记忆本身。

Evaluation · Strengths 评价：优点6.2.3 RUVA, MCEVOY & BRYANT (2007)
Strength 1 — A real trial 真实庭审

Mock jury research often uses a fictional trial summarised in a document. Ruva's participants watched real court proceedings of a real murder case → greater ecological validity than much research in this area.

Consequence:

Strength 2 — Standardised procedures 标准化

Articles for length (nine each, ~ten pages), the same 30-minute video for all, fixed timings, random assignment → the experiment is and the comparison fair.

Strength 3 — Deception handled carefully 欺骗的处理

The cover story was necessary. % (exposed) and % (non-exposed) believed it; % of exposed jurors suspected the real aim vs % non-exposed — but analysis showed suspicious participants did differently, so suspicion did not distort the results.

评价句式（背结构）
Point → Because/Example → Consequence。每个优点都带后果，才有分数。

Evaluation · Limitations 评价：缺点6.2.3 RUVA, MCEVOY & BRYANT (2007)
Limitation 1 — Only 30 minutes 讨论时间太短

Real jurors may deliberate as reasonably needed. The 30-minute limit means the verdicts may not reflect verdicts given time.

Limitation 2 — Not a real jury experience 陪审体验缺失

No (summons), no juror , not sworn in, no judge's instructions, not a real courtroom, not a real defendant's fate. Knowing the verdict would truly convict might have made participants more .

Limitation 3 — Groups of four to six, not twelve 人数不对

Real juries have members. Smaller groups give dissenting views support — biased opinions can go .

Limitation 4 — The study underestimates real life 低估现实

Participants were told to dismiss specific articles read only before. Real jurors absorb media over or months and would discriminate between coverage and trial evidence → real jurors could make source errors and be biased.

高分句（中文）
实验室里效果已经这么强；真实世界媒体浸泡更久更深 —— 实验结果是下限，不是上限。

Application 应用6.2.3 RUVA, MCEVOY & BRYANT (2007)
The logic 逻辑链

Jury decisions are biased by negative pre-trial publicity → instructing jurors to ignore media (voire dire too) → so measures must go beyond instructions, especially for cases.

Two concrete measures 两项对策
  1. the trial — move it to a location with less publicity about the defendant.
  2. Use a trial — the judge decides alone, no jury.

Both reduce the chance of an unfair trial.

Link it back 呼应前文

Why do these two work when instructions don't? Because they physically separate the decision-makers from the biased information, rather than asking memory to un-learn.

Key term
bench trial: trial by judge only — no jury.
voir dire: questioning of prospective jurors to screen out bias (ineffective here).
