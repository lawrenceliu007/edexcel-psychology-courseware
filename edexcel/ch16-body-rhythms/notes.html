§2 · Four types of body rhythm 人体的四类节律ANSWERS → 课件 §2
Definition 定义
Body rhythms are natural, recurring 重复发生的 cycles of biological processes in the body.
The four types — sorted by duration 按周期长短分四类
TypeDuration 周期长短Example 例子
Circadian（昼夜节律）约 24 小时The sleep-wake cycle
Ultradian（超昼夜节律）Shorter than 24 hours睡眠中的睡眠周期/阶段
Infradian（亚昼夜节律）超过 24 小时The menstrual cycle (≈ 28 days); SAD is also infradian
Circannual（年度节律）About one year动物一年一度的行为
Memory aid 记忆法
ULTRA = shorter ("ultra-fast" cycles happen many times a day) · INFRA = longer ("infra-slow", slower than a day). Circadian comes from Latin circa (about) + diem (day) = about a day.
Zeitgebers 授时因子
An exogenous zeitgeber（外部授时因子）is an external 外部的 event that helps to reset 重置 our biological clock. The main zeitgeber is 光. Three others named in the textbook: , and 温度、运动、吃饭时间.

§3 · The sleep-wake cycle & the SCN 睡眠觉醒周期与视交叉上核ANSWERS → 课件 §3–4
Key terms 关键词填空
The suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN)（视交叉上核）is a cluster of neurons located in the 下丘脑. Its role is to act as a 时钟/计时器 that maintains the sleep-wake cycle — the body's internal pacemaker（内部起搏器）.

The pineal gland（松果体）responds to darkness by secreting 褪黑素, a hormone which regulates the circadian rhythm.
The light → sleep pathway 通路填空
DAY 白天 Light in the blue-green spectrum is received by retinal cells（视网膜细胞）→ detected by the → the SCN tells the pineal gland to 抑制 suppress melatonin → we stay 清醒.
NIGHT 黑夜 Darkness falls → less light detected → the SCN signals the pineal gland to 分泌 produce melatonin → melatonin is released into the bloodstream and promotes 困倦 → we sleep.
§4 · How the cycle varies 节律的三类变化
(1) Across the lifespan 一生的变化 — melatonin levels
Newborns <3 monthsLittle melatonin of their own — received through 母乳
3 months → pubertyLevels continue to 上升
Puberty 青春期Levels 下降 decline — explains why teenagers 熬夜晚睡
Adulthood to ~40Levels 平稳 even out
After ~40 → old ageLevels steadily — may explain why older people 醒得更早
(2) Between individuals 个体差异

" people" rise early and are alert in the morning; " people"（早鸟型 / 夜猫型）rise later and are alert late.

(3) Lifestyle 生活方式

Three disruptors named in the textbook: , and 轮班、旅行时差、咖啡因 — caffeine interferes with 褪黑素 function.

§5 · Evidence for the SCN — animal studies 动物研究证据ANSWERS → 课件 §5
Study 1 — DeCoursey et al. (2000) 花栗鼠研究
Procedure: free-living eastern chipmunks in the Allegheny Mountains (US) wore 无线电项圈. 30 chipmunks had surgical to the SCN; 24 had sham surgery; 20 were intact controls.

Findings: significantly more SCN-lesioned chipmunks were killed by 黄鼠狼 in the first 80 days. They showed increased 夜间在洞穴中的活动, which attracted predators.
Study 2 — Ralph et al. (1990) 仓鼠 SCN 移植
Procedure: an SCN from a 供体 donor hamster was transplanted into a hamster whose own SCN had been 损毁 destroyed.

Findings: the transplant not only 恢复 restored the circadian rhythm — the recipient 采用了供体的节律.
StudyWhat it shows 说明了什么One weakness 一个弱点
DeCoursey (2000)
Ralph (1990)
Two cautions when using this research 两点谨慎
  1. Animal research: chipmunks and hamsters are not humans — be cautious when 泛化 generalising to the human sleep-wake cycle.
  2. Other pacemakers exist: 外周振荡器 are biological clocks in organs and tissues around the body (e.g. regulating 肝脏与消化), synchronised with the SCN.
Wider issue — animal ethics 动物伦理
DeCoursey exposed chipmunks to 被捕食 after lesioning their SCN. Animal research demands a 成本收益分析 before animals are put at risk. Write one sentence: is this research justified?

§6 · Evidence for exogenous zeitgebers 外部授时因子证据ANSWERS → 课件 §6
Removing the zeitgebers: what happens? 拿走授时因子会怎样
StudyKey findings 关键结果（填空）
Siffre (1962)
case study · French Alps cave		 Two months alone in a cave 130 米 underground — no light, no watch. When told to resurface on 14 September he thought it was 8 月 20 日. His free-running cycle was about 24 小时 30 分.
Siffre (1972)
Midnight Cave, Texas		 Six months underground. Twice he adjusted to a -hour sleep-wake cycle: 36 hours awake + 12 hours asleep.
Aschoff & Wever (1962)
WWII bunker · 3–4 weeks		 85% of participants adjusted to a sleep-wake cycle of 25–27 小时 — agrees with Siffre.
Folkard et al. (1985)
12 students · speeding clock		 Isolated with only a clock, secretly sped up. Students 能同步 to 23-hour days but 无法适应 22-hour days — their cycle averaged 25.48 小时. Conclusion: the internal clock 抵抗极端外部改变.
Skene & Arendt (2007)
blind participants		 Partially sighted people have a 正常 cycle; fully blind people with no light perception suffer daytime 嗜睡, napping and poor night sleep.
Phase shifts 相位移动 — Boivin et al. (1996)
⬇ Phase delay 相位延迟⬆ Phase advance 相位提前
Shifting the pacemaker to a 更晚 time — e.g. travelling 向西Shifting the pacemaker to an 更早 time — e.g. travelling 向东
Put the logic together 串逻辑（考试就按这个写）
① Remove light (Siffre; Aschoff & Wever) → the clock free-runs at about hours → so light normally 校准 resets it to 24h.
② But the clock is not a slave to external cues (Folkard) → it has strong 自主性.
③ No light perception → desynchronised cycle (Skene & Arendt) → light is 必需品 essential.
Conclusion sentence to memorise 背下来： Internal pacemakers and exogenous zeitgebers 共同作用 work together to maintain a normal sleep-wake cycle.

§8 part 1 · The menstrual cycle — the six steps 月经周期六步

The cycle is about 28 days and prepares the body for 怀孕. Fill the key hormone at each step:

  1. Menstruation（月经期）: the lining of the 子宫 sheds — lasts about 5–7 days.
  2. The pituitary gland releases 卵泡刺激素 (FSH), which stimulates the ovaries to produce eggs.
  3. 雌激素 (oestrogen) is lowest on day 1 and rises as eggs mature.
  4. Ovulation（排卵）at about day : a mature egg is released into the fallopian tube.
  5. 孕酮 (progesterone) is released to prepare the uterine lining for implantation.
  6. If the egg is not fertilised, oestrogen and progesterone levels 下降 → the lining is shed → the cycle repeats.

§8 part 2 · The HPG axis 下丘脑-垂体-性腺轴ANSWERS → 课件 §8
The command chain 指挥链填空
The internal pacemaker of the menstrual cycle is the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis. It works as a 反馈回路 feedback loop at each stage:
  1. The 下丘脑 releases GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone).
  2. This stimulates the 垂体 to secrete (FSH) and 黄体生成素 (LH).
  3. FSH triggers the development of eggs and the production of 雌激素.
  4. LH triggers the release of the egg during 排卵.
  5. After ovulation, 孕酮 prepares the uterine lining for implantation.

§10 · Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD) 季节性情感障碍ANSWERS → 课件 §10
Definition & symptoms 定义与症状
SAD is a 心境 mood disorder: episodes of major depression during 秋冬季节.
Learn these five symptom keywords 背下五个症状关键词：
① Low 情绪 and hopelessness   ② 疲倦与嗜睡   ③ Lack of 动力与兴趣   ④ Changes in 食欲   ⑤ (AO2 hook) symptoms return 每年冬天 — a 12-month cycle.
Two biological mechanisms 两大机制（2025 真题 AO1 采分点）
MechanismHow it works 填空
🌙 Melatonin
褪黑素		 Winter darkness → the body produces melatonin for 更长时间 → individuals feel 疲倦与嗜睡.
☀️ Serotonin
血清素		 Serotonin is known to 减少 reduce in low light → less mood regulation → 情绪低落. One theory: people with SAD show increased 再摄取 reuptake of serotonin.
Root cause 根本原因: reduced exposure to daily → disruption to the 昼夜睡眠觉醒周期.
Research evidence 研究证据（填关键数字）
StudyKey finding 关键结果
Michalak & Lam (2002)22 studies: latitude–SAD correlation r = 0.66. Caution: 迁移是个混淆变量.
Magnusson & Axelsson (1993)Icelanders who migrated to 加拿大曼尼托巴 had 更低 SAD rates.
Qin et al. (2015)8 macaques in reduced daylight → huddling, reduced movement, weight loss — reversed by 抗抑郁药.
Golder & Macy (2011)5.09 亿 tweets from 84 countries: shorter days → 更少积极情绪.
Ma et al. (2006)593 participants: 86 kcal/day more in autumn vs spring.
Danilenko et al. (1994)（矛盾证据）Higher daytime winter 褪黑素 in SAD patients — but other studies failed to 复制 replicate → melatonin evidence is mixed.
Levitan et al. (1998)Double-blind RCT: serotonin 激动剂 agonist drugs reduced sadness in 14 female SAD patients.

§11–12 · Treatments for SAD — light therapy & SSRIs 治疗ANSWERS → 课件 §11–12
Light therapy 光照疗法 — the numbers 数字填空
BrightnessAbout 10,000 勒克斯
Duration20–60 minutes daily, autumn & winter
TimingIn the 早晨, just after waking (Lewy et al. 2007 — corrects the winter 相位延迟 phase delay)
How it worksThought to 减少 melatonin and 增加 serotonin — although the mechanism is unknown.
Evidence for light therapy 光疗证据
StudyKey finding
Rosenthal et al. (1984)Bright light morning + evening for two weeks → depression ratings 下降; dim light → ratings 上升 → effect depends on 亮度 luminosity.
Golden et al. (2005)元分析 meta-analysis of RCTs: bright light significantly reduced depressive symptoms; dawn light ≈ 抗抑郁药物试验效果.
Eastman et al. (1998)96 patients, 3 groups; morning bright light > placebo (sham 负离子发生器) — but withholding treatment raises 伦理 issues.
SSRIs 选择性血清素再摄取抑制剂 — mechanism first 先填机制
SSRIs 阻断 block the reuptake of serotonin by binding to the transporters → more serotonin stays in the 突触间隙 → mood is 提升 elevated. 机制与 SAD 的血清素理论严丝合缝 —— "病因研究指导治疗" 是现成的 AO3 点。
StudyKey finding
Lam et al. (1995)68 patients, fluoxetine vs placebo: 59%% vs 39%% achieved ≥50% symptom reduction after 5 weeks.
Moscovitch et al. (2004)187 patients; 舍曲林 sertraline (50–200 mg/day) for 8 weeks beat placebo; drop-out very low.
Lam et al. (2006)Light therapy vs fluoxetine head-to-head: 同样有效 no difference — but light therapy was 更快.
