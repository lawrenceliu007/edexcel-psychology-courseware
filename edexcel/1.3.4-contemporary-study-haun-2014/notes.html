The whole study in one sentence 一句话总结
Given the same box task, children (mean 28 months) abandoned their own learned strategy to match a majority of familiar peers (12 of 18 switched), while chimpanzees and orangutans almost exclusively stayed with what they knew (10/12 and 8/12 stayed), so conformity to peers emerges early and distinctively in humans.
