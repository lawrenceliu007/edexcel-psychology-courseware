The whole therapy in one sentence 一句话总结
Psychoanalysis is a non-directive therapy aiming at self-insight by making the unconscious conscious: free association, dream analysis, transference and resistance each leak unconscious material past the defences, the therapist interprets it, and the client relives the conflict safely as an adult — while object relations therapy redirects the same toolkit at the internal working model built by our earliest attachments.

1 · The puzzle: visible patterns, invisible reasons4.1.6 PSYCHOANALYSIS
Two clients 引入

One dates cold, critical partners — again and again. One loses every friendship in the same argument. Both can describe exactly what goes wrong. Neither can stop it.

Fill the gap 填空

Freud's answer: the real reason is not to them. It lives in the — and what we consciously believe about ourselves is the story the mind tells afterwards.

Quick check 快问快答

What would a therapy built on Freud's theory try to do — (a) give better advice, (b) bring hidden reasons into awareness, or (c) train avoidance of stress?

One more classification 分类回顾

Psychoanalysis is -directive: the client talks, the therapist listens and interprets. The mirror image is systematic desensitisation (4.1.5), which is -directive.

中文理解 为什么「看见了却改不掉」
你看到的模式只是表面，真正的动力藏在潜意识里——有你自己都不承认的愿望、冲突和记忆。治疗不能靠「给建议」，而要把潜意识翻到意识层面。看见真正的理由，改变才有可能。

5 · Free association4.1.6 PSYCHOANALYSIS
Key term

Free association（自由联想）: the client speaks freely about their thoughts, memories, feelings and dreams, and is asked not to at all, no matter how or irrelevant the information seems.

Why rambling helps 为什么「乱说」有用

Every time you swallow a thought ("that's silly", "I shouldn't say that"), a has done its job. Free association aims to access the unconscious by the ego defence mechanisms that normally it.

The two-minute experiment 两分钟实验

Say honestly whatever comes to mind for one minute, forbidding yourself to censor. Write down the internal editor's phrases that appeared ("don't say that", "that's irrelevant"). Each one is a defence mechanism caught working.

中文理解 自由联想 = 让「内心审稿人」下班
平时说话前都有自我审查——那些「审稿人」就是防御机制。自由联想只有一条规则：想到什么说什么，不许删改。环境越放松、越不评判，审稿人越松懈，潜意识漏得越多。

6 · Dream analysis (part two): displacement4.1.6 PSYCHOANALYSIS
The disguise itself 伪装术
Key term

Displacement（置换）: we dream of something which is actually of something else — the feelings move to a substitute.

Why the disguise works at night 为什么夜里防不住

During sleep our ego defence mechanisms are , allowing unconscious content to slip into the dream. The defences still demand a — but cannot block the dream entirely. That gap is where the therapist works.

The therapist's role 治疗师的角色

The role of the therapist is to these symbols to uncover the actual wish.

Practise interpretation 练一次解释

9 · Resistance4.1.6 PSYCHOANALYSIS
Key term

Resistance（阻抗）: the client's reluctance to explore thoughts and emotions, shown indirectly — missing or arriving to sessions, or the topic of conversation.

Resistance is information 阻抗是信息

Why does the therapist treat lateness and topic-changing as material rather than rudeness? (Hint: where does the client resist?)

The sentence to remember 背这句
The client does not resist randomly — they resist precisely where it hurts, so the shape of the resistance traces the outline of the unconscious conflict underneath. Understanding resistance becomes part of the therapy.
中文理解 迟到和跑题不是态度问题，是地图
平时守时的来访者，唯独聊到父亲那周迟到；平时健谈，唯独提童年就换话题。避开哪里，哪里就有东西。治疗师不指责阻抗，而是研究它。

11 · Object relations: the idea4.1.6 OBJECT RELATIONS SCHOOL
Three key terms

Object — the internal of another person carried in the mind（客体＝心里那个人的内部表征）.

Human psyche（人类心智） — the mind with all its and unconscious aspects: thoughts, feelings, memories and wishes.

Internal working model（内部工作模型） — a mental construct formed from our experiences, influencing our perception of relationships as stable/unstable, trusting/untrusting, secure/insecure.

The chain 链条

Fill and memorise: Early attachments with parents (objects) → internal of those relationships → working model → steers every later relationship and personality development.

Say it in your own words 自己说一遍

A client says every relationship ends the same way. Explain their pattern using object relations language.

类比：小时候学会的口音自己察觉不到，却决定你一生的口音——早期关系就是关系的「母语」。

12 · Object relations therapy4.1.6 OBJECT RELATIONS SCHOOL
What happens 治疗里做什么
  • A -directive therapy aiming to understand the client's relationships and internal representations of objects.
  • Emphasis on experiences in childhood and the quality of that were formed.
  • The client talks about early relationships and the feelings and thoughts they have about them.
  • Like psychoanalysis: analysis of and counter-transference, of , and exploration of the unconscious.
  • But the focus stays on relationships with others and how they have been by the client.
The goal 目标

To help clients between their self-identity and others, and to establish between them.

中文理解 治疗目标为什么是「分清你和别人」
早期关系一团糟时，来访者心里「你」「我」边界常是模糊的：要么过度依赖，要么过度隔离。治疗＝陪他把心里「别人的声音」一个个认出来：这个害怕是我自己的，还是我妈妈塞给我的？认出来，边界就立起来了。

14 · Evaluation: object relations therapy4.1.6 EVALUATION
The same subjectivity — with a "but" 同样的主观性，但有转折

The same criticism can be made: analysing symbols in the client's internal world is . However, therapists are trained to and minimise subjective interpretation, working with the client in a way to facilitate a understanding of the client's experiences.

The strength: developmental evidence 发展心理学证据

Object relations therapy is based on work in developmental psychology demonstrating the importance of early childhood relationships for later development. Its central claim — early attachments matter — is one of the best-supported ideas in the discipline (you will meet it again in Topic E: and ).

The limits 局限
  • Built on study evidence, like psychoanalysis.
  • Difficult to its effectiveness.
The "but" sentence to memorise 必背转折句
“While subjectivity is present, therapists are trained to acknowledge and minimise subjective interpretation, working collaboratively with the client to facilitate a shared understanding.” — 一句话同时展示弱点与防御，比单写一边高级。

Q1 model outline 8 分提纲示范

AO1: non-directive; aim = make the unconscious conscious; assessment of concerns/history/relationships/symptoms; free association (no censoring); dream analysis (manifest → displacement → latent); interpretation of transference (as if a parent) and resistance; relive safely as an adult. AO3: holistic — reaches causes a symptom-focused therapy would miss; generated object relations; but interpretations subjective → unreliable; an hour a week for years, needs self-insight → inaccessible; Eysenck (1962): ~half improved, more recovered untreated, no consistent measure of cure; a specific phobia would be treated faster by SD — a comparison worth making.

Q2 model outline 4 分提纲示范

Strength: rests on extensive developmental psychology showing early relationships matter (and therapists are trained to minimise subjective interpretation, working collaboratively). Weakness: built on case studies; effectiveness hard to measure; interpretations of the internal world remain subjective — a different therapist could conclude differently.
