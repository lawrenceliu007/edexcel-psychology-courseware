1 · What it is 它是什么

The cognitive interview (Fisher and Geiselman, ) is a specific way of asking designed to help witnesses recall information without the interviewer them. 1984 · questions · accurate · suggesting to

2 · The two memory principles it rests on 两条记忆原理
PrincipleTheoryWhich technique uses it
Bartlett (1932) 重构记忆Memory the event: fill the gaps. rebuilds · schemaschange order, change perspective
Tulving (1974) 线索记忆Sights, sounds, smells and feelings are with the event; reinstating context and state act as cues. encoded · retrievalreinstate , report context · everything
中文逻辑：四技术是两原理各配一对

Bartlett 说记忆是重建的，会拿现成的「剧本」（图式）填空，所以换顺序、换视角能打断剧本，让人不瞎编；Tulving 说记忆靠线索取回，所以还原情境、什么都说能多给几条线索。考试问「explain how the CI is based on memory research」，就把这两对配着写。

TechniqueWhat the interviewer doesOne-line application 应用

Enhanced Cognitive Interview6.1.2 · UNDERSTANDING THE OFFENDER
1 · What it adds 增强版加了什么

The enhanced cognitive interview (Fisher and Geiselman, ) uses the techniques as the CI, but incorporates techniques. 1992 · same · more

2 · Main aim and features 主要目标与特点
  • Main aim: support the witness through the event and ask questions appropriate to the witness's level while minimising . reliving · intellectual · distractions
  • First phase: establish a connection with the witness before they report everything. personal
  • Questions are questions, suitable to the witness's social and cultural . open · background
  • Approach: witness , not controlled by the interviewer — but requires significant training in skills. led · interpersonal
中文逻辑：CI vs enhanced CI 一句话分清

CI 管的是「问什么」（四技术），enhanced CI 管的是「怎么问」（先建立关系、按对方的理解水平问开放式问题、少打断）。写评价时可以拿这个当 strength：enhanced 版对小孩和文化背景不同的证人更友好。

Formulation Phase 1 — Offence Analysis6.1.3 · UNDERSTANDING THE OFFENDER
1 · Key terms 术语

Contingencies: factors associated with a offence. common · specific

Criminogenic factors: factors associated with the that to the criminal behaviour. individual · contributed

Psychological (case) formulation: an analysis of a convicted offender by looking at their , biological and social circumstances, events, and how they have the events that happened to them. relationships · life · interpreted

2 · Two jobs in phase 1 阶段一两件事
  1. Compare offences committed by different offenders to find patterns and commonalities that reveal . similar · motives
  2. Interview the offender to identify factors — the social and psychological factors that to their criminal behaviour. criminogenic · contributed
中文逻辑：先横向比案，再纵向问人

阶段一是两步：横着比（找同类型案件里的共同因素，比如盗窃案的 contingencies 常是经济困难或药物滥用），竖着问（问这个人本人的 criminogenic factors）。两步合起来回答一个问题：这次犯罪对他起了什么作用（function）。Darian 3 分题就按这个顺序写。

Worked contingencies 例子填空

Contingencies for theft can be financial or abuse. difficulties · substance

Give one plausible contingency for assault in a pub: e.g. alcohol consumption

Quick check 快问

Why compare similar offences before interviewing the offender? patterns and commonalities give insight into the offender's motives

2 · How the formulation is written 怎么成文

The formulation explains how the factors the offence: which were present in the situation, what beliefs drove the behaviour, and how early (for example being bullied) connect to the present act. produced · triggers · core · experiences

中文逻辑：概念化是「解释链」不是「标签」

成品的读法：餐馆打人案里，拥挤的环境是 trigger，他小时候被欺负的记忆被唤起，核心信念「不能被人占便宜」被激活，于是用暴力处理冲突。考试写 formulation 就是把「因素 → 触发 → 信念 → 行为」这条链写完整，只罗列因素不写链，分拿不满。

3 · Risk and intervention 用途

Identifying these factors helps assess the offender's risk of and develop to help offenders break the cycle of offending. reoffending · interventions

Model answer highlights 高分要点
Q1a Olivia 8 分骨架
Four paragraphs, one per technique, each: name the technique + apply to Rhys. 1. Reinstate the context: have Rhys mentally return to the store (weather, sounds, how he felt). 2. Report everything: uninterrupted free recall, even trivial details, since a small detail may cue the key facts. 3. Change the order: recall from the security guard chasing outwards backwards, to interrupt schema-based guessing. 4. Change perspective: describe events as the shopkeeper or guard saw them. Close with: open questions, no leading questions, witness-led.
Q1b + Q2 骨架
1b: Strength - Fisher et al. (1989), 47% more information, so more complete recall from Rhys. Weakness - Memon et al. (1997), more recall can bring confabulated details, so answers need verification. Q2 (3 marks): 1. Offence analysis: compare this offence with similar offences to identify contingencies. 2. Interview the prisoner to collect criminogenic factors (neglect, unstable relationships, addiction, homelessness). 3. Build the formulation: link the factors into a chain showing the function the offending served, then use it to plan treatment and assess reoffending risk.
