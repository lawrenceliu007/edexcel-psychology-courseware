AO1 = "Raine used a PET scan with 41 NGRI murderers." (just stating a fact)

§2b · Raine (1997) — Procedure 实验流程ANSWERS → 课件 §2
What the PET scan actually measured PET 在量什么
PET = Positron Emission Tomography（正电子发射断层扫描）. It measures the metabolic rate 代谢率 of different brain areas using a radioactive tracer 放射性示踪剂 (FDG). Active brain cells take up more glucose → more tracer → more signal 更多信号.
Put the procedure in order — number the steps 1–5 排序
OrderStep
CPT 持续执行任务 for 10 minutes — keeps attention active so brains are in the same state.
Practise the on a sequence of blurred numbers.
Inject the radioactive glucose tracer called FDG 氟脱氧葡萄糖.
Continue on the CPT for another minutes.
Perform the PET scan PET 扫描 to measure metabolic rate.

提示：答案在右边的 hint 区域里——但先自己排，再揭开看是否一致。

Why each step matters — match the reason 连线练习
StepWhy it was done 目的
10-minute CPT practice① So the brain is in a stable state during the scan → can compare fairly 公平地
FDG injection② Radioactive tracer lights up active brain cells 活跃脑细胞
32-minute wait③ Gives FDG time to be taken up 被吸收 by brain cells
PET scan④ Measures metabolic rate 代谢率 (activity level) in each brain area
Operationalisation — turn the concept into a number 把概念变成可测量的变量
The DV (因变量) was the metabolic rate 代谢率 in each brain area, measured in FDG uptake FDG 摄入量 units. The IV (自变量) was group membership 组别: murderer vs control.

Conclusion in one sentence 结论一句话
Raine concluded that NGRI murderers showed brain dysfunction in areas linked to impulsivity 冲动 and emotion regulation 情绪调节. However, this is only a correlation 相关 — it does not prove brain differences cause 导致 murder.
✅ Strengths 优点（写关键词）
  • Large sample: 41 NGRI murderers is large for this population 对这类人群算大.
  • Matched controls: same age & gender → improves internal validity 内部效度.
  • Standardised: same CPT + PET → objective 客观 comparison.
❌ Weaknesses 缺点
  • Unrepresentative: NGRI murderers are atypical 非典型的 violent offenders.
  • Correlation: lower brain activity could be the result 结果 of violence, not the cause.
  • Ethics: PET exposes controls to radiation 辐射 they didn't clinically need.

§3a · Brendgen (2005) — Twin design 双生子研究设计ANSWERS → 课件 §3
Twin logic 核心逻辑（必背）
MZ (identical, 同卵) share 100% of genes; DZ (non-identical, 异卵) share about 50%. If MZ twins are more similar 相似 on a trait than DZ twins, genes 基因 are likely involved. If MZ ≈ DZ, the trait is more environmental 环境.

Procedure — what they did 流程
  • Longitudinal design: data collected at 5, 18, 30, 48, 60 months + again at age 6 (focus).
  • Two rating sources: teacher 教师评分 ratings (spring term) + peer 同伴评分 ratings by classmates.
  • Scales used: PSBS-T (Crick et al., 1997) + Direct & Indirect Aggression Scales (Björkvist et al., 1992).
  • Item scoring: -point scale: 0 = never, 1 = sometimes, 2 = often 经常.
Why longitudinal matters 为什么是 longitudinal
Collecting data at multiple time points lets researchers track over development, instead of just one snapshot. But it also means more participants drop out — here, 322 → pairs (about % lost).

§3b · Brendgen (2005) — Results 结果ANSWERS → 课件 §3
Two types of aggression 两种攻击行为
Physical aggression（身体攻击）= hitting, fighting, kicking.
Social aggression（社会性攻击）= ignoring others, spreading rumours, threatening to withdraw friendship. Can be overt（公开的）or covert（隐蔽的）.
MZ vs DZ correlations — fill the conclusion 填空
Type of aggressionMZ similarityDZ similarityConclusion
Physical aggression much higher 远高于 lower strong genetic component 强遗传成分
Social aggression Better explained by shared environment 共同环境
Physical–social correlation Correlation found, best explained by General tendency to be aggressive may be genetic 遗传, but expression is environmental
Direction of influence 方向填空
Physical aggression may lead to 导致 social aggression, but not the other way round 反过来不会.
Reason: as children grow, they develop language/cognitive skills 语言与认知能力 and learn more socially acceptable 社交上可接受的 ways to express the same aggressive tendency.
Evaluation 评价（填空）
✅ Strengths
  • Two rating sources (teacher + peer) → triangulation 三角互证 increases validity.
  • Longitudinal data from early childhood 婴幼儿期 to age 6.
  • Large sample: 234 twin pairs.
❌ Weaknesses
  • Small sub-groups when split by sex → hard to generalise 推广.
  • Peer ratings by 6-year-olds may be unreliable 不可靠.
  • Zygosity check: not all twins had a DNA test DNA 检测.

MAOA in one sentence 一句话
The MAOA gene（单胺氧化酶 A 基因）breaks down neurotransmitters like serotonin. There are two variants: MAOA-L (low activity, higher aggression 更高攻击性) and MAOA-H (high activity, lower aggression).

Conclusion — gene × environment interaction
