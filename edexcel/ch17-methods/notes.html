§2 · Correlational research 相关研究ANSWERS → 课件 §2
Core idea 核心定义填空
A correlation（相关）looks for a 关系 relationship between two co-variables（两个协变量）.
Co-variables are the two variables being 测量 measured in a correlation. They are not an IV and a DV, because the researcher does 不操纵任何变量 not manipulate.
A correlation does not tell you that one variable 导致 caused the other to change.
Experiment vs Correlation 实验与相关的区别
Experiment 实验Correlation 相关
AimFind a causal relationshipFind a relationship 关系
VariablesIndependent variable (IV) & dependent variable 因变量 DVTwo co-variables 协变量
GroupsCompares differences between groups 组间Looks for a link within one group 组内
Cause?Can claim causation 因果关系cannot 不能 claim causation
Hypotheses for a correlation 相关研究的假设（三种都要会写）
TypeWhat it predicts 预测什么
Directional / one-tailed（定向/单尾）Predicts a positive 正 or negative 负 correlation.
Non-directional / two-tailed（非定向/双尾）Predicts a relationship 关系, but does not say which direction 方向.
Null hypothesis（零假设）Predicts no relationship 无关系.

Directional: There will be a correlation between hours of revision and test marks.

Non-directional: There will be a between hours of revision and test marks.

黄金规则：写相关假设时永远不写 "IV" 或 "DV"——只写 co-variable 1 和 co-variable 2。

§4a · Spearman's rank — the formula & ranking 排等级ANSWERS → 课件 §4
Why we use it 用途
Spearman's rank correlation coefficient（斯皮尔曼等级相关系数，r_s）tells us whether two ordinal 顺序 variables are related, and whether the result is likely to be significant 显著. It only works for correlation 相关 data.
The formula — copy it out and label it 抄一遍公式并标注
r_s = 1 − 6Σd²
n(n² − 1)
SymbolWhat it means 含义
r_sSpearman's rank correlation coefficient 相关系数（结果） — always between and −1 与 +1
dThe difference 差 between the two ranks for each pair of scores（同一行两个等级的差）
Σd²The sum 总和 of all the squared differences（所有 d² 加起来）
nThe number of pairs of scores 成对数据（不是人数的一半！）
The two ranking rules 排名两大铁律
  1. Rank each variable separately（两列分开排）。The highest 最高 score gets rank 1, the next gets rank 2, and so on.
  2. Tied scores（并列分数）share the average 平均 of the ranks they would have taken. 例如两个分数本来该拿第 6 和第 7 名 → 两个都拿 rank 6.5。

§5 · Evaluating correlation as a method 评价相关研究法ANSWERS → 课件 §5
✅ Strengths 优点（填关键词）
  • Ethical: lets you study relationships it would be unethical 不道德 to manipulate, e.g. stress & aggression.
  • Cost-effective: can use secondary data 二手数据 to check a relationship before spending money on an experiment.
  • Natural: measures behaviour in real life → high ecological validity 生态效度.
  • Useful pilot: a significant correlation may justify a later experimental 实验 study.
❌ Weaknesses 缺点（填关键词）
  • Causation problem: cannot tell which variable caused 导致 which.
  • Third variable: another factor may cause both 两者 co-variables to change.
  • Directionality problem: stress → aggression, or aggression → stress 压力?
  • Coincidence: some correlations are spurious 虚假的, e.g. mozzarella cheese sales and civil-engineering doctorates.
The third-variable problem in detail 第三变量问题（AO3 最爱考）
A study finds that stress is positively correlated with aggression. Three explanations are possible:
① Stress causes 导致 aggression.
② Aggression stress.
③ A third variable 第三变量 (e.g. hot weather 高温) causes both stress and aggression independently 各自独立地.
这就是为什么一个"显著的相关"绝不能写成"proves causation 证明因果"。
Write the money sentence 背下并默写这一句
"Correlation does not imply . For example, although stress and aggression are correlated, a third variable such as could cause both, or aggression could cause rather than the other way round."
In your own words 用自己的话写

Mechanism detail 机制细节填空
  • CAT: uses X-rays 射线 from multiple angles → a detailed still image of brain structure; good for detecting tumours 肿瘤, damage and structural abnormalities.
  • PET: injects a radioactive tracer called FDG（一种类似葡萄糖的物质）; active areas use more glucose and emit more gamma rays 伽马射线 → shows over- or under-active areas.
  • fMRI: uses a powerful magnet 磁铁 to detect changes in blood oxygenation 含氧量 while a person does a task vs a baseline.

✅ Strengths❌ Weaknesses
① Objective images: visual data can be checked by other researchers → improves reliability 信度.
② Precise localisation: pinpoints which brain areas 区域 are involved.
③ Scientific credibility: quantitative data, falsifiable 可证伪 hypotheses.		 ① Correlational: cannot prove brain differences cause 导致 aggression.
② Directionality: violent behaviour may change 改变 the brain, not the reverse.
③ Artificial tasks: lab tasks may lack ecological validity 生态效度.
④ Ethics: stigmatising labels like "aggressive brain 攻击性大脑" and screening for future violence.

§7 · Twin studies 双生子研究ANSWERS → 课件 §7
MZ vs DZ — fill the gaps 同卵与异卵对比
TypeAlso calledGenetic overlapHow formed 怎么来的
MZMonozygotic / identical 同卵100%One fertilised egg 卵子 splits into two embryos
DZDizygotic / fraternal 异卵~50%Two separate eggs fertilised by two separate sperm 精子
Concordance rate 一致率 — definition
The concordance rate is the percentage 百分比 of twin pairs in which both 两个都 twins show the behaviour being studied.
Rule: higher concordance in MZ than DZ → a genetic 遗传的 influence is suggested.
Two key studies 两个关键研究（数字必须记住）
StudyFindings 结果Conclusion
Gottesman & Shields (1966)
schizophrenia 精神分裂症		 MZ concordance = %  ·  DZ = % MZ much higher → genetics 遗传 may play a role
Brendgen et al. (2005)
aggression 攻击行为		 Physical aggression: MZ DZ (higher in MZ) → genetic
Social aggression: MZ DZ (similar) → environmental		 Different types of aggression may have different 不同 causes
Reading concordance data — the exam template 解释数据模板（背）
"The concordance rate was higher in MZ twins () than in DZ twins (), which suggests a influence. However, this is a correlation 相关, not proof of causation — MZ twins may also share a more similar 相似 environment."
Evaluation 评价
✅ Strengths❌ Weaknesses
① Natural manipulation: MZ/DZ pairs give a natural 自然 comparison of genetic similarity.
② Consistent evidence: many twin studies find higher 更高 MZ concordance for aggression traits.
③ Quantifiable: concordance rates are easy to compare 比较.		 ① Equal-environments assumption: MZ twins may be treated more similarly 相似 than DZ twins → higher concordance may be environmental 环境, not genetic.
② Epigenetics: genes can be switched on/off by experience → 100% shared DNA ≠ identical outcomes.
③ Sample size: large MZ/DZ samples are hard to find → limits generalisability 可推广性.
④ Nature–nurture: twins usually share their environment, so the two cannot be fully separated 分开.
