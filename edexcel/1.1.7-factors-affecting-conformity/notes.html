What this workbook covers 本册讲什么
Asch and Moscovici showed that people yield to influence. This booklet explains how much — through three lenses: individual differences (personality) — locus of control, desire for control; situation — group size, unity, ambiguity, source; and culture — from Berry's two subsistence cultures to Smith & Bond's 133-study meta-analysis. The same lenses also decide whether a minority gets heard.
How to use this booklet 怎么用
  1. Fill it in as you go — check the answer key (pp. 19–20) after each page.
  2. Pages 3–6 (personality) and 11–13 (culture) carry the named studies. Redo them from memory, under time.

The whole page in one sentence 一句话总结
How much people conform — and how readily they hear a minority — depends on who they are (internal locus of control, high desire for control resist), the situation they face (a united majority of three is strongest; a broken majority cuts conformity from 33% to 5.5%; ambiguity and credible sources raise it), and the culture they grew up in (cooperative, collectivistic cultures conform more — Smith & Bond, 1996).

13 · Smith & Bond (1996): the big test1.1.7 FACTORS AFFECTING CONFORMITY
Key term 关键术语
Smith & Bond (1996)

A m -analysis of conformity studies using an Asch-type procedure, across collectivistic and individualistic countries. Collectivistic countries showed h conformity.

Why collectivists conform more 解释

Collectivistic cultures emphasise group c and i , so social norms are expected to be adhered to. Individualistic cultures nurture greater i and are more t of dissent.

The caveat 课本的限制句

While we can draw general conclusions, there are many v that influence conformity, so the exact influence of s -c factors cannot be d .

中文理解 三个文化研究递进
① 各国各测一次（记分板）→ ② Berry 给因果解释（生存方式→价值观→行为）→ ③ Smith & Bond 用 133 项、17 国做总结：集体主义 > 个人主义，但变量太多，确切影响无法确定。
