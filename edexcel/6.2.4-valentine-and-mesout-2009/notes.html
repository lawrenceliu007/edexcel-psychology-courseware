The whole study in one sentence 一句话总结
After showing the Dungeon genuinely raises heart rate (74.7 → 86.9 bpm), Valentine and Mesout had 56 visitors meet a “scary person” in the Horror Labyrinth and then describe and identify them: only 17% of witnesses with high state anxiety (above the median 52) made a correct identification, versus 75% of calm witnesses — and males (19 accurate) beat females (7) — supporting the catastrophe model: high anxiety collapses eyewitness accuracy.

Why the London Dungeon? 为什么选鬼屋6.2.4 VALENTINE & MESOUT (2009)
The background 背景

Tim Valentine researched witness identifications for years. With Jan Mesout (2009) he wanted to overcome limitations of laboratory research on stress, by studying eyewitness identification in a real-life scenario — where witnesses were not aware their testimony would be tested.

Key terms 两个关键术语
The London Dungeon
A tourist attraction where visitors travel through rooms depicting London's history (e.g. the Great Fire), with actors acting out each theme.
catastrophe model of memory performance（突变模型）
physiological arousal/anxiety can induce a impairment of eyewitness recall and identification. high · marked

The Line-up 指认6.2.4 VALENTINE & MESOUT (2009)
How it worked 流程
  1. A -person photograph line-up; the scary person's photo placed in a selected position.
  2. Alongside other similar-looking actors.
  3. Instruction: “The person you saw in the line-up.”
  4. Guided to say if they could not the person.
  5. Then rated in the decision from – per cent.
Key term 关键术语
culprit-present line-up（目标在场指认列队）
A line-up that actually contains the person being searched for — so a wrong choice is a false identification, not a “target absent” catch.
为什么说「可能不在」（中文）
防止硬选：不说这句话，被试会默认「九张里必有一张」，乱猜也选一张。有了这句，「没认出」成为合法答案，指认成功才真实。—— 记住：后面应用题里这句指令也救不了高焦虑证人。

Evaluation · Limitations 缺点6.2.4 VALENTINE & MESOUT (2009)
Limitation 1 — Self-selected scare-lovers 自选样本

Participants had all to attend a scary place. They may scary entertainment and be affected in a way from people who avoid such places → limits to the wider population.

Limitation 2 — Real fear before consent 事先无法同意

The fear was genuine and came consent was possible (consent came only after the tour). Managed by debriefing and the right to , but the fear itself could not be consented to in advance.

Limitation 3 — Not a real crime 不是真案子

The scary person is an ; the encounter lasts seconds inside a -minute maze with ~30 other visitors. A real crime may involve longer exposure, a weapon, or consequences — a different stress profile.

一体两面（高分思路）
生态效度的来源（真实紧张、不知情）正是缺点的来源（无法事先同意、来的人爱找刺激）。优点缺点写成一对照应，是本题最漂亮的答案。

Three implications 三条应用
  1. During trials of real-life crimes, be in trusting accounts from witnesses who underwent significant or during the crime.

Page 18 — Practice A (model points)

Q1 56 visitors to the London Dungeon (29 female, 27 male), mean age 31.0, recruited after their visit with a reduction in admission price for completing questionnaires.
Q2 To show the Dungeon genuinely raises anxiety: 20 employees' heart rate rose 74.7 → 86.9 bpm in the labyrinth, and mean state anxiety (43.2) correlated with the heart-rate change — so the inventory validly measures stress rather than another emotion.
Q3 Nine-person culprit-present photo line-up; scary person's photo in a random position with eight similar actors; told the person may or may not be present; could say if unable to identify; confidence rated 0–100%.
Q4 State = situational, felt now (terror in the dark maze); trait = stable disposition to worry regardless of circumstances (measured as a personal baseline). Females showed higher state (52.8 vs 45.3) but equal trait anxiety.
