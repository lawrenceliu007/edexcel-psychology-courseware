The whole topic in one sentence 一句话总结
Criminal thinking is learned — so it can be retrained: CBT helps offenders notice their distorted thoughts (blaming others, seeing threats, self-justifying) and replace them with prosocial thinking, and the evidence is that named programmes cut reoffending by 15% (R&R), up to a third (MRT) and up to 30% (CBT overall) — but only for offenders who are motivated, and only if we also remember that poverty and jobs, not just thinking, predict reoffending.

First, what CBT is NOT（辨析题常考）

CBT is a form of counselling: it does not focus on overcoming emotional reactions to difficulties in the . The focus is mainly on the thinking of the offender, though past thoughts are acknowledged. CBT does not work — it requires from the offender. As a talking therapy, the offender must talk so the therapist can help them understand their reactions and consider alternatives.

§13 · Evaluation ③ — wider issues and debatesANSWERS → p.20
Debate 1: Psychology as a science — the RCT

In a randomised controlled trial, participants are randomly assigned to either the group or the group. Random allocation is — without it, variables may prevent cause-and-effect conclusions, so we cannot be sure improvements came from the treatment.

treatment / control / essential / participant

应用：Tong & Farrington 自己承认，meta-analysis 里的欧洲研究没有用 RCT —— 这正是他们结论的一处软肋，可以写进 AO3。

Debate 2: Gender bias

Data mostly concerns offenders (the largest group of convicted criminals in the UK). Biological explanations like levels or syndrome explain male offending but not female. Structured programmes were designed men in mind, so may not reflect the needs of female offenders.

male / testosterone / XYY / with

中文理解：两场辩论为什么「配得上」6 分
它们都不是「再补一条缺点」，而是质疑整个研究范式：
· RCT 辩论：不随机分组 → 组间本来就不等 → 疗效可能是「选出来的」而不是「治出来的」。
· 性别偏见：证据库几乎全男性 + 项目为男性设计 → 对女性犯罪者既不知道是否有效，也未必适合。结论的可推广性（generalisability）被砍掉一半。
