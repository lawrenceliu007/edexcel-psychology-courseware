  2. The person is as that type of person. labelled 被贴标签

SLT — Social Learning from the Media6.1.1 · EXPLANATIONS FOR CRIME
1 · Key terms in exam wording 考试用语

Social learning theory: criminal behaviour results from such behaviour from it via the media or watching other people. modelling · observing

Vicarious reinforcement: learning through the of person's behaviour. consequence · another

Modelling: imitating a that has been seen to be . role model · rewarded

2 · The chain of learning 学习链

补全链条（每空一词）：see the model act → see the act → store it as a script → reproduce it when the situation fits, possibly after seeing it. rewarded · later

中文逻辑：替代强化是核心词

孩子不用自己挨打才学会打人：他看到别人打人被奖励（赢得了比赛、被夸厉害），就等于自己被强化了一次。这叫替代强化。考试要写这个词的英文 vicarious reinforcement，define 题就背上面第一句原文。

3 · Nature-nurture position 立场定位

SLT is a -cognitive explanation: learning is social (what is watched) but the storage and reproduction happens inside the mind. It sits between SFP (pure environment) and ASPD (pure biology). social

Model answer highlights 高分要点
Q1a+1b ASPD 2+2 骨架
Describe: a persistent disregard for the rights of others, diagnosed only with evidence of antisocial behaviour before age 15; features include deceitfulness, impulsivity, aggression and lack of remorse. Justify credibility: twin studies (Torgersen 38%, Fergusen 56% of variance) and brain scans (Yang & Raine, reduced prefrontal function) give a measurable biological basis, and Simonoff (2004) shows childhood conduct disorder predicting adult ASPD.
Q3 Luca 8 分骨架
1. Mechanism: Luca watches violent games where fighting is rewarded (prizes, praise). 2. Key term: vicarious reinforcement - seeing the model's aggression succeed strengthens his own aggressive scripts. 3. Application: the scripts reproduce at home (fights with his brother) and at school (playground) when the situation fits. 4. Balance: this evidence is correlational; Luca may have been more aggressive before gaming, and the theory cannot prove direction.
