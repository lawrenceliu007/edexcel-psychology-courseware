Chapter 16: Body Rhythms（人体节律） Topic C

Biological Psychology / 生物心理学
Circadian, ultradian, infradian and circannual rhythms; the sleep-waking cycle, SCN and melatonin; internal pacemakers vs exogenous zeitgebers (DeCoursey, Ralph, Siffre, Aschoff & Wever, Folkard); the menstrual cycle and HPG axis; Seasonal Affective Disorder (melatonin & serotonin explanations); light therapy and SSRIs — with official mark schemes from recent Paper 2 exams.

Chapter 17: Research Methods — Correlation, Spearman, Scanning & Twin Studies（研究方法） Topic C

Biological Psychology / 生物心理学
Correlation and co-variables; scatter diagrams; Spearman’s rank test (step-by-step calculation, tied ranks, significance and critical values); CAT, PET and fMRI brain-scanning; twin studies, concordance rates and the nature–nature debate — with a real Paper 3 Spearman question (Jan 2025) and worked answer.

📙 Topic D: Learning Psychology（学习心理学）

Chapter 20: Classical & Operant Conditioning and Social Learning Theory（经典条件反射、操作条件反射与社会学习理论） New

Learning Theories and Development / 学习心理学 · Spec 4.1.1 + 4.1.2 + 4.1.3
Three answers to "how do we learn?": classical conditioning (Pavlov's dogs, the three stages NS→CS, generalisation/discrimination/extinction/spontaneous recovery), operant conditioning (Thorndike's puzzle box and law of effect, Skinner box, ABC model, the four consequences, token economies, four reinforcement schedules) and social learning theory (Bandura's ARRM stages, vicarious reinforcement) — plus the spec-required Skinner (1948) superstition-in-the-pigeon study, an 11-scenario consequence classifier, a four-phenomena quiz and the Rhea/Aanya/8-mark exam practice with essay plans.

🚔 Topic F: Criminological Psychology（犯罪心理学 · Paper 3）

Chapter 7: Explanations for Crime and Antisocial Behaviour（犯罪与反社会行为的解释） New

Criminological Psychology / 犯罪心理学 · Spec 6.1.1
Three rival explanations for why people offend, each with evidence and evaluation: self-fulfilling prophecy (Rosenthal & Jacobson's bloomers, Jahoda's Wednesday-born boys at 22% vs 6.9%), social learning from the media (Bandura's Bobo doll, Williams' TV town, Comstock & Paik's +0.19) and antisocial personality disorder (Hodgins & Côté, twin-study heritability up to 56%, Yang & Raine's prefrontal cortex findings) — with interactive activities, textbook figures and the 8-mark video-game exam question with essay plan.

Chapter 8: Understanding the Offender（理解罪犯：认知访谈与个案概念化） New

Criminological Psychology / 犯罪心理学 · Spec 6.1.2 + 6.1.3
Two tools for two questions: the cognitive interview for witnesses (Bartlett's reconstructive memory + Tulving's context-dependent memory → four techniques, enhanced CI, Geiselman's 41.15 vs 29.40 table, Fisher's +47%, Köhnken's 42-study meta-analysis, Holliday's child "inoculation" finding) and psychological (case) formulation for offenders (offence analysis, contingencies, criminogenic factors, core beliefs, Whitehead's Mr C case) — with a be-the-interviewer simulation, the Kaia formulation builder and the Olivia/Rhys 12-mark exam plan.

📓 Topic H: Clinical Psychology（临床心理学）

Chapter 20: Definitions and Debates in Diagnosis（诊断的定义与辩论） Clinical

Clinical Psychology / 临床心理学
Four definitions of abnormality (statistical infrequency, failure to function adequately, deviation from ideal mental health, deviation from social norms), classification systems (DSM-5, ICD-11, multi-axial diagnosis), culture-specific disorders, reliability and validity of diagnosis, medical model debates.

Chapter 21: Schizophrenia（精神分裂症） Clinical

Clinical Psychology / 临床心理学
Positive and negative symptoms, features (prevalence, onset, prognosis), biological explanations (dopamine hypothesis, genetics), drug therapy (typical vs atypical antipsychotics), family therapy, treatment–aetiology fallacy.

Chapter 22: Unipolar Depression（单相抑郁） Clinical

Clinical Psychology / 临床心理学
DSM-5 symptoms and classification, biological explanations (Monoamine Hypothesis, drug treatments), cognitive explanations (Beck's Cognitive Triad, Ellis' ABC model), CBT treatment approaches (cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation).

Chapter 23: Anorexia Nervosa（神经性厌食症） Clinical

Clinical Psychology / 临床心理学
Symptoms and features (DSM-5/ICD-11, subtypes, prevalence, prognosis), biological explanation (role of genes, twin/linkage/GWAS/polygenic studies), social learning theory (role models, vicarious reinforcement, media/social media), drug therapy and CBT-E treatment approaches.

Chapter 24: Studies in Clinical Psychology（临床心理学研究） Clinical

Clinical Psychology / 临床心理学

Chapter 25: Methods（临床心理学研究方法） Clinical New

Chapter 26: Clinical Practical（临床实操：内容分析） Clinical New
